The driver fixes seed `20261026330000 + group_index`, 20,000 trials per cell and
block size128. The group is one of six profiles at one of15 true-Elo values;
seven alternative protocols share its modeled paths. The complete protocol
group/max horizon, RNG, versions, actual pair probabilities and seed are recorded.
Changing grouping or horizon changes the random-number layout and is a different
input. The planner's separate `--full-grid` CLI has its own documented seed rule;
it is a valid new run, not the exact replay of this driver-generated table.

Core modeled outputs and gzip terminal samples are deterministic at fixed inputs,
seed, sampling layout and dependency versions. Execution receipts also record
elapsed time and UTC provenance; these observational fields are not deterministic
model output. No repeated full Monte-Carlo run was used to choose favorable cells.

True Elo means **logistic Elo**. The seven hypothesis intervals are **normalized
Elo (nElo)**: [0,10]/1500, /2500, /3000; [0,5]/3000 and /4000; [-1,4]/1500;
and non-regression[-3,1]/1500. Every decision is after a completed two-game pair.
A boundary hit on the final pair is H0/H1; an uncrossed final path is capped.

G95, G100r and G119 are three separate supplied conc5 paired profiles. They are
never pooled. The three draw-ratio models assume independent games and have no
measured concurrency. Actual conc7/G120 and conc8 paired profiles are missing;
no curve here is authenticated for them. Exponential tilting preserves zero
support and pentanomial shape ratios; it does not hold Pearson correlation fixed
or recover individual-game W/D/L. Infeasible means/hypotheses are rejected.

The online statistic is the supplied QA/S06 empirical-variance Gaussian paired
approximation. It is not exact arbitrary-profile Fastchess likelihood. Analytic
OC/ASN uses its delta-method drift/diffusion, including variance estimation, and
finite-cap Brownian first-passage probabilities. Mean games integrates survival;
the capped median has an atom at the cap. Nominal alpha/beta=.05 are planning
inputs, not a proof of exact native error rates or sequential CI coverage.
