1. **R1 — Do not ship.** Worker count alone permits no bypass; an explicit reviewed ownership policy is required. Standalone-model TSan cannot establish actual-engine safety. Required proof: synchronize all owner/foreign accesses or implement a compliant opt-in lease, reproduce the original engine race, and validate the actual candidate with full-engine TSan, real-table stress, lifecycle tests and semantic parity. A 45-minute deadline does not replace evidence.

2. **R2 — Do not call it race-free.** Holding all 1,024 stripe locks across recursion violates the default rule against retaining entry locks across recursion. The CHECK at acquisition 65 is detector-capacity failure, and the 15-second deadline leaves validation incomplete; neither establishes a race. Disabling the detector does not validate the candidate. Preserve the exact failure and provenance, redesign synchronization, then obtain completed actual-engine evidence.

3. **R3 — Eligible for bounded reviewed synchronization; not categorically prohibited.** The current skill explicitly allows an opt-in, exactly-one-worker lease with one coarse gate across recursion, table-bound owner TLS, sequential stripe draining before publication, and foreign stripe release before waiting/retrying. Required proof still includes release/acquire correctness, exception cleanup, no helper launch/join within the lease, complete accessor coverage, and actual-engine tests covering default Shared, opted-in and SMP paths, transitions, lifecycle edges and semantic parity. The stated existing passes support review but do not independently prove every requirement.

4. **R4 — Do not resize immediately.** Bestmove and worker join do not quiesce a previously blocked foreign probe; lease release may awaken it. Required proof: every search/helper, diagnostic, hashfull, prefetch and other accessor has stopped before resize or another lifecycle mutation.

5. **R5 — No arbitrary cross-thread deadlock-freedom claim.** Recognizing A→B→A through a TLS chain establishes only the stated same-thread recognition behavior. It does not prove safety for cross-thread A→B/B→A acquisition cycles. Require a reviewed ownership/lock-order argument covering supported nesting plus actual-engine evidence; unsupported cases remain uncertain. Mutex-operation counts measure synchronization operations, NPS measures search throughput, and equal-clock Elo measures playing strength. None substitutes for the others.
