1. **R1 — Do not ship.** The worker bypasses synchronization while public foreign readers still acquire locks, so those locks do not protect their accesses against the worker. A 45-minute deadline changes no evidence requirement. Required proof: audit every shared access; establish a consistent synchronization scheme; reproduce the original race and validate the candidate in the actual instrumented engine. Standalone model TSAN success is supplementary.

2. **R2 — Do not call it race-free.** The current skill explicitly says, “Never hold locks across recursion or worker joins.” Holding all 1,024 stripe locks across recursion violates that rule. The detector CHECK and deadline are an incomplete failed run, not race evidence or race-freedom evidence. Disabling the detector cannot itself establish correctness. Preserve the failure and limits; correct the prohibited locking approach and complete actual-engine and validator checks.

3. **R3 — Categorically prohibited by the current wording.** The coarse gate is held across recursive search, directly conflicting with “Never hold locks across recursion or worker joins.” The skill contains no bounded-ownership exception for explicit opt-in, one worker, draining stripes, or reviewed synchronization. Existing passing Release/ASan/TSan/regression evidence does not override that instruction. The protocol’s technical correctness remains unestablished by applying this document alone; eligibility would require an explicit skill exception plus its specified proof obligations.

4. **R4 — Do not resize immediately.** `bestmove` and a joined worker do not establish that foreign callers have stopped. The blocked foreign probe may resume and access storage. Required proof: quiescence of every search/helper, diagnostic, hashfull, and prefetch caller before mutation; no surviving handles across the table’s lifetime boundary.

5. **R5 — Cannot advertise arbitrary cross-thread deadlock freedom.** Finding A in one thread’s A→B→A TLS chain only establishes detection within that thread. It says nothing sufficient about another thread simultaneously acquiring B→A. Required proof: audit the complete cross-thread acquisition/wait protocol and establish an ordering or equivalent deadlock-prevention argument, with relevant concurrency validation. The current skill supplies no such proof. Operation counts establish counts; NPS/scaling requires repeated counterbalanced Release measurements; equal-clock Elo requires separate strength validation. None establishes the others.
