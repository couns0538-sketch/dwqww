## Mechanism and bounds

`corrected_eval` adds pawn and scaled material entries before dividing by256 (`src/search.cpp:614–620`). The update receives `best_score - ss->static_eval`, where `ss->static_eval` already includes those corrections (`:913,1194`). Let a fixed raw-evaluation error beΔ and update gain bea=w/256. Ignoring quantization/clipping, the current one-table recurrence is `x'=(1−a)x+a(Δ−x)`, hence correctionΔ/2. With two equal default-weight tables, total equilibrium correction is2Δ/3. Replacing the EMA with `x'=x+a(Δ−sum)` removes that particular attenuation.

This does not imply the current attenuation is wrong. Search scores are selected bounds, and the residual/target changes with the search itself. The proposed identical-key fixed-target feedback is not intrinsically unstable: at default material weight100, its two-table sum contracts by `1−2a`, with `a≤1/16`; even the advertised maximum material coefficient300 gives sum gain at most1/4. That ideal stability statement does not predict strength or behavior across aliased keys.

The diff clamp is±2048 fixed-point units (±8cp); entries clamp at±8192 (±32cp). Starting from cleared tables, the current EMA stays inside±2048, irrespective of the wider declared entry clamp. At depth15 with a constant clipped residual, integer truncation gives the observed fixed endpoint±2033 per table, or±15cp combined at default coefficient100. The proposed increment can reach±8192 per table and±64cp combined. It therefore changes **both forgetting/feedback and effective range**, even though the written constants remain identical. This cannot be interpreted as a pure decay-only effect or as evidence that a separate range change is necessary.

For production calls passing the existing non-mate guard, both best score and corrected static eval lie between−31871 and31871. The largest pre-clamp residual product is63742×256=16,317,952; after clipping, `abs(diff*weight)≤32768`; the proposed entry-plus-step is bounded by8320 before the clamp. Material multiplication at its advertised maximum is8192×300=2,457,600. These fit signed32-bit arithmetic. Real search depth enters positive and may be reduced once by IIR; `weight=min(depth+1,16)` remains positive and bounded. Arbitrary out-of-contract test integers could overflow the unchanged pre-clamp expression; the proposal creates no new overflow for valid search inputs.

Runtime work remains O(1) per existing update, with the same two lookups, table size, update call sites and no new allocation. Search-tree size, update counts and time can change because corrected evaluation affects pruning. No speed claim follows from the smaller arithmetic expression.

## Guards and practical risks

The actual call at `src/search.cpp:1190–1194` excludes check nodes, excluded-move searches and mate scores; it permits a missing best move or a noncapture and requires lower/upper bounds to point in the residual's direction. A quiet promotion is still a noncapture. Early pruning returns and abort returns bypass this final update. The proposal retains all of these conditions and leaves stored raw TT evaluation unchanged. It is not trained only on exact scores or universally quiet settled positions.

The main risks are:

1. **Noisy or biased bounds get stronger influence.** Removing EMA shrinkage and allowing64cp combined corrections can amplify selective-search feedback and change pruning, even when constant-target convergence improves.
2. **Aliasing and intended sharing transfer errors.** Each side has only16,384 pawn/material slots; the material key intentionally groups many positions. Both entries receive the same joint residual, so a learned adjustment can contaminate another pawn structure through the material table. With fixed keys and no clipping, unequal component offsets are retained by the increment even when their sum is adequate. This is identifiability/retention, not proof of instability.
3. **Stale state loses a forgetting mechanism.** Zero residual leaves the proposed entries unchanged; the EMA shrinks them. Worker clearing/`ucinewgame` resets histories, while `Clear Hash` does not clear these correction tables. Match restarts limit cross-game persistence but do not remove within-game drift or sharing. Existing clamps bound it; boundedness alone does not establish useful evaluation.

Do not combine a raw-eval target, gain retune, smaller range, table normalization or caller change with this candidate. Those would answer different questions. Keep the material coefficient100 and every other search/NNUE setting frozen.
