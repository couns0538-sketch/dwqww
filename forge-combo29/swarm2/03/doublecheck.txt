## 5. One discrepancy found and explained

The first copy of the traces I committed used the line number WITHIN my extracted slice (1..80)
instead of the book line number (2001..2080) that the record format requires. The double-check
re-run is what exposed it: the fresh game-2001 run wrote '2001' as its line number while the
committed full trace said '1'. All traces were re-run from the full book file with the correct
numbering (rerun at 20:31-20:32Z); the numbers in this report come from the corrected files.
The engines' behaviour was unaffected (the FEN per line was always the right one); only the
record label was wrong, and the re-run reproduced the same 8,253 records.
