The history-pruning and ProbCut event rates were lower in the warm soft
arm than in the warm hard arm. Small changes to broad history bonuses
could propagate across later plies under both budgets. I expect the
bonus maximum to matter less in the shallower search, and reverse
futility to be the closest of the two comparison settings.
