Setting formulas and shared inputs

- 302–306 builds integer quiet and noisy LMR tables from log(depth) × log(moves).
  The configured bases and divisors are in hundredths. Lookup is 313–316.
  Besides the actual reduction, 2122 and 2183 use the unadjusted value to obtain
  depth inputs for pruning. Quiet history adjustment is 2265; the additional
  cutoff-count adjustment is 2264.
- 323–324 computes min(HistoryBonusMax, HistoryBonusQuadratic × depth² +
  HistoryBonusLinear × depth − 32). Uses at 1565 and 1597 feed the quiet and
  capture statistics. Other direct uses: TT-cutoff history (1855, optional),
  late quiet continuation (2293), PV history (2350–2358), and fail-low
  continuation (2363). These scale future move ordering and history inputs as
  well as the fail-low update itself. The three settings do not independently
  gate a search step.
- 1298–1299 weights the material table contribution by MaterialCorrectionWeight
  / 100; 1334 updates its entry only when the weight is positive, under the
  correction-learning conditions at 2376–2380. The corrected static value is
  consumed at 1669 and 1872 (optionally overridden in eval by the TT), and
  drives improving at 1880–1884. This is a shared evaluation input, with no
  dedicated prune or extension.
- 1439–1445 uses width 2 × DrawJitter + 1 and the current local node count modulo
  width. Even an eligible repetition/fifty-move draw can output zero. The
  default DrawJitter = 0 at 95 means no jitter taken on PC20.

Counter boundaries

- Tested means arrival at the event predicate, except null move and ProbCut
  count actual tries and singular counts actual verification attempts.
- IIR is bucketed before decrement. LMR plies are the actual clamped
  difference in starting depth. Singular double is a subset of positive
  extensions. Negative extensions are separate; multi-cut is no extension.
- Aspiration tested means a completed root trial with a finite bound. Taken
  requires a retry after the fail and any capped-reopen exit checks.
- Counts are enabled only for UCI searches with one worker and one PV.
  Just before the bestmove line, the engine checks main + qsearch nodes
  against its final result nodes and emits a refusal on any mismatch.
- Capture futility, fail-low continuation bonus, eval swing and draw jitter
  are taken zero times under PC20. They are collected in the raw rows.
