Method
Read archived PGNs in forge-combo29/sol, compared their bytes to the
original local files, and matched each SHA-256 to its archived .meta.
Group games by the integer Round tag. A retained pair has two finished
games ending with the Result tag value, opposite A/B colors, and the
same exact six-field FEN tag. One complete pair contributes one FEN.
Intersect distinct FEN tag strings verbatim, including move counters.
Exclude any unfinished or unmatched game. Both historical matches were
interrupted even though their archived PGNs contain only whole pairs.
