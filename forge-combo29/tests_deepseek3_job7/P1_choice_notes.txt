So 60.5% of late quiet moves carry a correction in -4..4 cp, which cannot move `r` at any
sensible K (the term is (c*K)/1024 and needs |c*K| >= 512 to change a ply, i.e. |c| >= 16 at
K=32). That is the first thing to know about this feature: it is silent on three fifths of
the moves it could act on.

Design note on the clamp: the job says the change is limited to one ply either way, so the
term is clamp(-(c*K)/1024, -1, +1). At K=32 a correction of 16 cp is already enough to change
a ply, and the clamp caps it there.
