### is 30-F50's instability MultiPV, or the CLOCK? (6 fresh runs each)
PC r15/file-5 build: /home/ubuntu/build/pc/forge

their cell: wtime 5000, MPV3, Hash 256     depths [1, 4] nodes 73-622 spread 8.5x
CONTROL same but go nodes 50000            depths [10] nodes 6386-6386 spread 1.0x
CONTROL same but go depth 12               depths [12] nodes 94994-94994 spread 1.0x
their cell: wtime 5000, MPV1, Hash 256     depths [16] nodes 172312-172312 spread 1.0x
CONTROL MPV1 go depth 12                   depths [12] nodes 21812-21812 spread 1.0x

WHAT THIS MEANS - the instability is CLOCK x MULTIPV, and neither alone causes it:
  wtime 5000 + MultiPV 3  -> depths [1, 4], nodes 73-622, spread 8.5x   <- unstable, very shallow
  wtime 5000 + MultiPV 1  -> depth 16, nodes 172,312 every run, 1.0x     <- STABLE at full depth
  nodes 50000 + MultiPV 3 -> depth 10, nodes 6,386 every run, 1.0x       <- STABLE (deterministic limit)
  depth 12   + MultiPV 3  -> depth 12, nodes 94,994 every run, 1.0x      <- STABLE
So:
  * the CLOCK ALONE is not the cause (MultiPV 1 is perfectly stable on the same clock - consistent with S-C15's
    finding that a clock search varies a little in score/nodes but this shows it can be exact in nodes at MPV1);
  * MULTIPV ALONE is not the cause (deterministic limits with MPV3 are exact);
  * the PAIR collapses: at MultiPV 3 the clock path produces depth 1 or 4 instead of 16, i.e. the time manager
    starves the search when MultiPV is on, and how much it starves it varies run to run.
That is a more specific and more useful statement than "the shipped build is not reproducible at MultiPV 3": the
MultiPV-3 clock path loses ~12-15 plies of depth against the same clock at MultiPV 1, and is unstable while doing it.

CONFIRMS seat 30's OBSERVATION (8.5x spread on their cell) and refines its CAUSE (the clock x MultiPV interaction,
not MultiPV as such, and not the clock as such). Seat 30's "patched build is exact" half is consistent with this:
if their patch makes the MultiPV-3 clock path behave like MultiPV 1, exactness follows.
CAVEAT: seat 30 measured at Hash 256 with `go wtime 5000` on their own position; I used a middlegame from my set.
The depths differ (their {7,11} vs my {1,4}) so the exact numbers are position-dependent - the STRUCTURE
(instability only at MPV3+clock, stability at MPV1+clock and at deterministic limits) is what reproduces.
