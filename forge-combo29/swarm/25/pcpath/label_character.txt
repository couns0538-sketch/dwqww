So every observed failure is a SHORT PV - the engine reports a mate it has not yet PROVEN to the end of its own
claimed distance, and the PV stops short. The SCORE is not broken; the REPORT is premature. Therefore:

  **LABEL RULE (measured, actionable): reject any mate score whose PV length != 2|N|-1 (or 2|N| for negative N).
  That filter catches every failure observed here and costs nothing, because a correct mate score always passes.**

Detail worth noting: the failure is budget-dependent on the SAME board - 4Q3/8/r2K4/... gives mate 4/SHORT at
softnodes 1000 and 2000, then mate 3/ok from 5000 up. 7Q/8/8/4K3/... gives mate 5/SHORT at 500 and 1000, mate 4/ok
at 2000. So a SHORT PV is what a still-converging mate looks like, and the number it reports at that moment is one
move longer than the truth - which is exactly the "pessimistic by a move" observation in S-F34, now explained
rather than merely described.
