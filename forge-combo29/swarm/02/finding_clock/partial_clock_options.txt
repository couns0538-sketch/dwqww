# Reading: a clock naming the player who is NOT to move bounds nothing, so the search has NO limit and
# runs until it is stopped. The last row shows the engine does not 'ignore a missing clock' — a huge
# btime DOES bound a White-to-move search, because btime is White's clock in that branch. The engine
# simply never reads the clock named for the other side.
