known list says "an option without a value answers `info string invalid value for option X`", so the question
is whether that holds for the CHECK type too, not just spin. 7 is my real doubt: a check option that accepts
the word `banana` would mean the on/off rule of the brief is not enforced. 9 is a genuine risk because
loading a second net or book file can free and reallocate state.
