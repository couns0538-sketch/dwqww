## Why I predict this, from the source I have read
`plan_clock_budget` (`include/forge/clock_plan.hpp:27-50`) returns `reserve_empty = true` when
`time <= overhead`, and `Move Overhead` defaults to 10. So:
- `wtime 10` gives `available = 0` -> **reserve empty, no search at all**;
- `wtime 1` and `wtime 0` likewise;
- `wtime 30` gives available 20, soft = 20/22 = 0 -> clamped to 1 ms, hard = min(12, 5) = 5 ms;
- `wtime 100` gives available 90, soft = 4 + inc*0.75, hard = min(54, soft*5).

KILL RANGE for the whole job: if `wtime 10` or smaller produces a search that runs longer than the clock,
the engine loses on time in every such game, and that is the finding. If the engine answers instantly without
a bestmove or without any legal move, that is a worse finding.
