Committed before any search was run. Numbers are predictions, not results.

## Machine
- nproc is 4 (already observed before this prediction; it is a hardware fact, not a measurement of the engine).

## Expected wall-time behaviour, movetime budgets
For `go movetime T`, I expect the engine to stop close to T and slightly over it, because the last iteration
overshoots by the node time.

| budget | predicted median over 200 searches | range that kills the prediction |
|--------|-----------------------------------|---------------------------------|
| movetime 50  | 50 to 62 ms   | median < 40 ms or > 90 ms |
| movetime 500 | 500 to 540 ms | median < 460 ms or > 650 ms |

- searches over the limit (= wall time > budget + 50 ms) at movetime 50: predicted 0 to 8 of 200.
  Kill: 20 or more.
- largest single overrun at movetime 50: predicted under 250 ms. Kill: 500 ms or more (an overrun of 10x).

## Two equal baseline runs
- Two identical runs of 200 x `go movetime 50` are predicted to differ in median wall time by under 3 ms,
  and in total elapsed time by under 5 %. Kill: median difference 10 ms or more.

## Clocked budgets
For `go wtime 1000 btime 1000 winc 10 binc 10` (movestogo absent -> the engine divides the remaining time by
some constant), I expect each move to use roughly 3 % to 8 % of the 1000 ms, i.e. median 25 to 90 ms per search.
Kill: median over 200 ms (it would burn a fifth of the clock on one move) or under 5 ms (it would lose on time).

For `go wtime 10000 btime 10000 winc 100 binc 100`: predicted median 60 to 250 ms.
Kill: median over 700 ms or under 20 ms.

## What would surprise me
- An engine that ignores `movetime` and answers instantly (median under 20 ms) or runs the full 500 when asked 50.
- A hard 2x overrun on a 1000 ms clock (would lose games on time in the low-time endgame).
- Wall time growing with the position (a time manager that only checks the clock every N nodes usually shows a
  fat tail; a 99th percentile over 3x the median at movetime 50 would be that tail).

If any kill-range hits, it is a finding and goes in FINDINGS.md with its raw file.
