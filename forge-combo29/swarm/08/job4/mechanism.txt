### `go wtime` with movestogo (soft limit): stops at an ITERATION BOUNDARY
| control | plan soft | median wall | median last-depth time | undershoot | overshoot of soft (min..max) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| mtg1 | 495 | 347.3 | 347.3 | 0.0 | -271 .. +299 |

| mtg40 | 24 | 23.7 | 23.7 | 0.0 | -8 .. +73 |
| none | 45 | 39.3 | 39.3 | 0.0 | -17 .. +101 |

Reading: with movetime the deadline is checked INSIDE the search (should_stop), so the last
iteration is thrown away and wall == plan + ~1.7 ms. With a clock the break is at search.cpp:2612
`if (elapsed_ms() >= soft_basis * factor) break;` which sits at the END of an iteration, so nothing
is thrown away (undershoot 0) and the search overshoots soft by up to a whole iteration.
