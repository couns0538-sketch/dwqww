**The cap demonstrably changes the search**: turning it off reduces the node count on 9 of 11 boards,
which is the direction the clamping account predicts: clamping deep reductions lets the search cut more,
so the UNCAPPED variant reaches the same depth in fewer nodes. The mechanism account is SUPPORTED, with
the magnitude varying from 3,356 to 14,040 nodes. Note 2 boards go the other way (+621, +10,981), so it is
a strong tendency, not a uniformity — and I state that rather than the cleaner claim.
