## The claim, tested

| pattern | boards |
|---|---|
| base WAITING -> PC WAITING | **8** |
| base PROBING -> PC WAITING | **2** |
| base WAITING -> PC PROBING | **1** |


**NO — the pattern is NOT the rule, and 'dominant' would overstate it.** Only 2 of 11 divergences are
base-PROBES -> PC-WAITS. The other 9 are WAITING->PROBING x1, WAITING->WAITING x8. In particular 8 of 11 have BOTH moves waiting, so the repair
usually changes WHICH waiting move is played, not whether the move probes at all. The eyeball impression
from a few boards was wrong and this test replaces it.
