## The claim, tested

| their statement | my measurement |
|---|---|
| a depth search can NEVER produce a book answer | **0 of 3 book lines on `go depth 12`** |
| a clock search does produce them | **3 of 3 on `go wtime 6000`** |
| `movetime` should too (the other game_search form) | **3 of 3 on `go movetime 100`** |
| OwnBook false must suppress the clock book line | **suppressed** |

**CONFIRMS**: the book fires on both `game_search` forms (3/3 each) and NEVER on a depth search (0/3),
and `OwnBook false` suppresses it. Their corrected 3/3 reproduces exactly, and their false negative is
demonstrably a property of the instrument — my own depth arm is the same structurally-blind test.
