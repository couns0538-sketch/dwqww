My own mutation kinds, my R seed-801 positions, 120 mutations per kind per seed, two seeds.

| seed | drop | delete | insert | swap | replace | order (highest first) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 77 | 100.0 % | 100.0 % | 100.0 % | 60.0 % | 91.7 % | drop > delete > insert > replace > swap |
| 88 | 100.0 % | 100.0 % | 100.0 % | 46.7 % | 82.5 % | drop > delete > insert > replace > swap |

## The claim, tested

| question | answer |
|---|---|
| the ORDER is the same at both seeds | **YES** (drop > delete > insert > replace > swap / drop > delete > insert > replace > swap) |
| largest per-kind difference between seeds | **13.3 points** |
| their claim: within 0.3 points per kind | **NOT held on my sample (13.3 points)** |
| 'drop' is 100 % refused at both seeds | **YES** |

**CONFIRMS the shape**: the refusal profile is ordered the same way at two independent seeds and 'drop' is
100 %% at both, so it is a property of the parser rather than of the sampled positions. My percentages
differ from theirs (different positions and a different mutation generator), so I confirm the ORDER and
the seed-stability, not their exact figures. Their 'within 0.3 points' is checked as stated above.
