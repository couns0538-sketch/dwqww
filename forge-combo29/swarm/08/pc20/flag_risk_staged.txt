**No flag even on the worst boards at 200 ms.** The time manager handles the bare-endgame case across the
whole clock range, because the plan shrinks with the clock: at 200 ms the `available` is 190 and the plan
(soft 8 / hard 40 ms) is small enough that even an expensive iteration fits. So the residual risk I flagged
in 08-F34 does NOT materialise on these boards.
