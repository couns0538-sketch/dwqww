| question | answer |
|---|---|

**The tiny-clock zeros are now MEASURED**, closing the one gap 08-C13 named. Note the measurement floor:
the fake engine's 300 ms is caught at every size, but this does not prove a 1-2 ms overrun at wtime 1 would
be caught — the floor of one round trip is about 0.1-0.2 ms, so an overrun smaller than that at wtime 1
remains below what any harness can see, and I say so rather than implying the comparator is exact.
