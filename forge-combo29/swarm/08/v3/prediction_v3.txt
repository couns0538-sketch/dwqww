## My guess at the hottest v3 function, and what would kill it

The v3 path has three candidate hot spots, and I predict the ranking below for **`bench 13` under PC20 + the test
net, Threads 1**:

| rank | function | predicted share of bench-13 samples | kill range |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **`nnue::v3` incremental UPDATE** (refresh/applied-delta through the cache) | **35–50 %** | under 20 % or over 65 % |
| 2 | `nnue::v3` forward pass / evaluate | 15–30 % | under 5 % or over 45 % |
| 3 | `nnue::v3` refresh (full recompute) | 5–20 % | over 40 % |

**Why I predict the update dominates:** it runs at every node, while the forward pass runs at every EVAL leaf (fewer
nodes than updates in an alpha-beta tree), and a refresh runs only when the accumulator cannot be incrementally
updated. If the profile shows refresh above 40 %, the incremental path is failing too often — which would be the
finding rather than a speed opportunity.

**Kill for the whole prediction:** if any single v3 function is over 70 % of the samples, my ranking idea is
irrelevant and the path is dominated by one routine; I would say so plainly rather than fitting the ranking.

## What I expect NOT to be the hot spot
The search itself (`search`/`qsearch`/`movepicker`) should be a MINORITY of bench-13 time once the v3 net is loaded,
because file 7 says the v3 path replaces the built-in evaluation entirely and evaluation typically dominates a
net-driven bench. If search is still over 50 %, then the net is not actually being exercised by the bench, which
would be a harness problem worth reporting before any speed patch.
