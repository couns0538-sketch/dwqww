## Controls (enforced by assertion — a failure aborts, it does not print a verdict)
- every position must parse in python-chess and be accepted by the engine;
- each ILLEGAL case must have the ep capture ABSENT from python-chess's legal list (else it is not a pin case);
- each LEGAL control must have the ep capture PRESENT (else "the engine omitted it" is untestable).

All five passed. `check_S03F3_raw.txt` is the pasted output.

## Two things this check found on the way, both about my own test construction
1. My first attempt at a second ILLEGAL case, `7k/8/8/8/k2pP2R/8/8/8 b - e3 0 1`, was **REFUSED by the engine**
   — and python-chess flagged it `IMPOSSIBLE_CHECK` too. My constructed position was bad, not the engine. I
   replaced it rather than reporting the refusal as a discrepancy.
2. The textbook shape I settled on (`... rook a5, black pawn f5 just pushed from f7, white pawn g5, white king
   h5`) is the case a move generator is most likely to get wrong, and it is exactly right here.

## Why it matters to area 8
A perft or search-latency harness that used a pinned-ep position as a test case would measure a smaller tree if
the engine wrongly allowed the capture. It does not, so no tree size of mine is affected. This is a correctness
statement, not a speed one.
