## One clarification that sharpens their point rather than weakening it

The cp 0 here is NOT the drawn-root shortcut misfiring. **K+N vs K+N is a genuine draw**, so cp 0 is the
correct score. And python-chess confirms `is_insufficient_material() == False`, i.e. the material rule alone
does not declare it drawn - so the engine reached the right verdict by search, not by the material rule.

What 03-F24 demonstrates is a POPULATION defect, not a scoring one: 40 consecutive depth-6 self-play games from
one start funnel into this single position, so a "lone-king population" built that way is one sample wearing the
word population.

## Why it matters to me directly

My S-C21 sparse sets were hand-generated rather than self-played, for exactly this reason. And the mechanism is
the one S-C27a established: fixed limits are deterministic, so 40 games from one start give one line, not 40.
Anyone building a mate-board or endgame test set by self-play needs a varied OPENING source, not a repeated
start.
