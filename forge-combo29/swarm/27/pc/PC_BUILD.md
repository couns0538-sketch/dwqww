## The Threads-1 rule (coordinator, effective for these 12 hours)

**Any score I report is from Threads 1 unless the job is about threading.** Seat 04 measured the PC build at
Threads 4, depth 12, PC20, four fresh runs on one position: **+2576, mate 13, mate 18, mate 16** with nodes 5.0M
to 31.4M, while Threads 1 gave **+2207 four times with identical 75,311 nodes**. The move never varied.

So: scores at Threads > 1 are not trustworthy, node counts at Threads > 1 are not reproducible, and **move
choice appears to be the only currently trustworthy output of a multi-threaded search**. This is the same
phenomenon as my S-F7a (Threads 2 spread 18,993–34,983 nodes over five runs), now with scores added.

**Audit of my own record against this rule:** every measurement I have reported was at Threads 1 or left
`Threads` at its default of 1. The only Threads > 1 runs I ever made are the ones in `job4/f2_threads/` and the
warm row in `REPORT_job4.md`, and those are already labelled non-deterministic and withdrawn. Nothing of mine
needs provisional treatment, and no finding line of mine needs a "Threads > 1" caveat.

From now on every finding line names: **build** (base `e06310ec` or PC r15 `068f0927`), **settings** (none, S14
or PC20), and **Threads 1**.
