=== THE THREE BUGS IN THIS FILE, ALL FOUND BY ITS OWN CONTROLS ===
1. The timeout was unreachable: 'while now < deadline: readline()' blocks, so the deadline is never
   re-tested and a silent child hangs for ever. A timeout in name only.
2. A NEW reader thread per _until call: two threads competed for the same pipe, so the second call's
   reader swallowed lines the first was waiting for. Symptom: the fake engine answered uciok and then
   appeared to ignore isready.
3. THE GUARD SCANNED THE WRONG LINES: the invalid-FEN diagnostic arrives BEFORE readyok (during the
   position/isready exchange), not during the search. Scanning only the go output meant a real engine
   returned a startpos result with NO error raised - control 5 caught it. A harness that sends
   isready between position and go - the normal defensive pattern - loses the only signal.

AND: the fake engine buffered its stdout when piped, so the self-test waited 30s on 'no uciok'.
