audit_corpus_provenance.py - controls and three corpora
The three separable checks from tonight, packaged so an EXISTING corpus can be audited.

IN-PROCESS CONTROLS (run before any audit; refuse to audit if they fail)
  1 a row at 163x its depth peers IS flagged as mate-suspect
  2 a 0.47x-of-budget row IS classified nodes-style
  3 a 1.04x-of-budget row IS classified softnodes-style

CORPUS A - mate-contaminated (5 rows: four at 3,411 nodes, one at 556,387, all depth 7)
  mate-suspect rows: 1  -> "row 4: 163.1x its depth peers"
  FINDING issued, exit 1

CORPUS B - mixed budgets (two rows at 0.47x/0.91x of budget, two at 1.04x/1.00x)
  nodes-style: 2 ; softnodes-style: 2
  FINDING: corpus mixes `go nodes` and `go softnodes` at the same stated budget, exit 1

CORPUS C - clean softnodes corpus (two rows at 1.04x and 1.13x)
  nodes-style: 0 ; softnodes-style: 2
  "No inconsistency detected by these three checks." exit 0
  (and the tool states the BOUND: that rules out a mate/mixed-budget corpus, not every
   provenance problem - a row with no budget column cannot be classified)
