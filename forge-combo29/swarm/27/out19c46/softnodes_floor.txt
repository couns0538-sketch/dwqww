The engine reaches depth 64 at 1,344 nodes and STOPS - max_depth is clamped to 64.
Any budget above 1,344 therefore produces FEWER nodes than requested:
  softnodes 20000 -> 1,344 nodes = a 14.9x UNDER-shoot, not an overshoot.

AND THE ANSWER IS CORRECT: mate 1, a1a8, at every budget.
So this is NOT a wrong answer - it is that 'go softnodes N' is bounded above by
the work needed to reach depth 64, and on a small tree that is far below N.

CONSEQUENCE FOR S-N1: its claim 'overshoots N by 1.02x to 1.95x' assumed the search
always wants MORE nodes than N. It does not. The ratio is bounded by the tree, so it
runs from 0.07x (depth-64 clamp, small tree) to 3.50x (iteration boundary in a big tree).
