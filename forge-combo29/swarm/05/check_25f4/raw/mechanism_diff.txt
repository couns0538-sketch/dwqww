MECHANISM — what I actually established, and the hypothesis I had to withdraw
=============================================================================
I proposed (first version of this file) that the difference between my boards (boundary moves with depth) and
seat 25's board (boundary fixed at 99) was "whether the search can reach clock 100". I then TESTED it by
stripping all 12 pawns from their board (raw/pawns_test.txt). The hypothesis is WRONG:

| board                            | d4  | d6  | d8  | d10 |
|----------------------------------|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| seat 25's board (12 pawns)       | 97  | 99  | 99  | 99  |
| the same board with 0 pawns      | 100 | 100 | 100 | 100 |

The pawnless board is CONSTANT at 100, not depth-tracking. So removing the pawns did not reproduce my boards'
behaviour; it made the boundary sit exactly at 100 at every depth. My "reach the counter" story does not survive
its own test and is WITHDRAWN.

What the measurements actually show, with no mechanism attached:
 1. Boundary behaviour is POSITION-DEPENDENT, not material-dependent and not depth-dependent in general:
      - K+R vs K, K+Q vs K, K+B+B vs K (bare endgames): boundary falls ~1 per depth unit, counter+depth ~100-101
      - seat 25's 21-piece middlegame: boundary 97 at d4, then flat at 99 for d6..d12
      - the same middlegame with every pawn removed: flat at 100 for d4..d10
 2. So my S-F11 sentence "the boundary tracks counter+depth" is TRUE FOR THE BARE ENDGAMES I TESTED and FALSE as a
    general statement. It must be read as scoped to those positions. S-F13's "material-independent" claim is also
    too strong: K+B+B agrees with K+R/K+Q, but a middlegame does not, and a pawnless middlegame sits at 100.
 3. I have NO mechanism for any of this and I am not going to offer one. I did not read search.cpp.

Consequence for the disagreement with seat 25: both observations are correct about their own positions and my
earlier claim that they were "the same rule seen on boards of different mate distance" (S-F11) was an
over-generalisation from three bare endgames. It is corrected in FINDINGS.md and REPORT_job2.md.
