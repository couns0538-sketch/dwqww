PREDICTION — seat 06, area 6, job 2 (static evaluation vs the LEFT-RIGHT mirror)
written before the first measuring command. Never edited afterwards.

What is measured: `eval` of every G position and 500 R positions that have NO castling rights, and of the same
board under python-chess `board.transform(chess.flip_horizontal)` (files mirrored, colours untouched, side to
move untouched). Positions with any castling right are EXCLUDED and the exclusion is counted, because the
horizontal flip would move a rook away from the square its castling right names.

Why a difference is expected rather than a fault: chess is not left-right symmetric, and this engine's
hand-written parameters were texel-tuned on 61,536 self-play games (src/eval_params_table.inc line 1). I measured
the table before writing this prediction: 48 of 64 Pawn PSQT entries and 64 of 64 for Knight, Bishop, Rook,
Queen, King differ from their left-right mirror. So the hce MUST differ. The job is to measure how much, and to
name which terms can respond to the flip.

Prediction (each number with the range that kills it):
P1  positions with a differing `total`: more than 90 % of the compared positions.
    KILLED if fewer than 50 % differ (that would mean the flip is invisible, which the table says it cannot be).
P2  the number of positions differing in `nnue` EQUALS the number differing in `total`, exactly.
    KILLED by any single position where these two counts disagree (the built-in net is non-residual, so
    total == nnue whenever the net is on; this is a sharp internal-consistency check of my own harness).
P3  median |hce(self) - hce(h-flip)| is at most 120 centipawns and the maximum is at most 600.
    KILLED if the median exceeds 120 or any position exceeds 600 — a structural mirror bug (a file index used
    without mirroring) pushes deltas into the hundreds-to-thousands, while PSQT tuning differences are tens.
P4  control: a position against itself gives 0 differences. KILLED by any difference.
P5  the h-flip differences are NOT all in one direction: both positive and negative deltas occur.
    KILLED if every nonzero delta has the same sign.
P6  at least one position differs in hce while `total` differs too, and at least one position differs in hce
    while `nnue` differs by a different amount (i.e. the two components are not locked together).
    KILLED if hce and nnue deltas are equal on every position.
P7  excluding castling positions removes fewer than 15 % of G. KILLED if more than 15 % are excluded.

I will NOT call a left-right difference a fault. A left-right difference is what an asymmetric tuned parameter
table must produce. What would be a fault is a term that mirrors the board in code but reads a file index
unmirrored, and P3's magnitude bound is the test for that.
