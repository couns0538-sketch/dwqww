S-F1 IMPACT TEST — does the silently-dropped extra token change the resulting POSITION?
Engine /home/ubuntu/builds/rel/forge, `go perft 1`, wait-for-bestmove form.

  position fen 4k3/8/8/8/8/8/8/R3K3 w - - 0 1                  -> nodes 15   (baseline)
  position fen 4k3/8/8/8/8/8/8/R3K3 w - - 0 1 zzz              -> nodes 15   (junk dropped, SAME board)
  position fen 4k3/8/8/8/8/8/8/R3K3 w - - 0 1 moves e1e2       -> nodes 5    (moves applied)
  position fen 4k3/8/8/8/8/8/8/R3K3 w - - 0 1 zzz moves e1e2   -> nodes 5    (junk dropped, MOVES STILL APPLIED)

CONCLUSION: the extra token is dropped and everything after it is still parsed, so the
engine's resulting position is IDENTICAL to the well-formed command. S-F1 is therefore a
REPORTING defect, not a position-integrity defect: a malformed FEN is accepted silently where
`set_fen` would have refused it, so a caller has no signal that its FEN was malformed.
This narrows S-F1's severity and is the honest scope of it.
