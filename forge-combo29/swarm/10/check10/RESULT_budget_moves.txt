WHAT THIS MEANS FOR TRAINING DATA (the number the owner asked for)
  19% of positions get a different move at 20000 nodes than at 250000 nodes, and 8% are
  still different at 50000 vs 250000. The median disagreement is 52 cp and the worst is 218 cp.
  On 2 of the 100 positions the small budget's move is at least 200 cp worse as judged by the
  engine itself at the larger budget -- i.e. those are not ties, they are small-budget blunders.

  So a self-play or tuning set that mixes these budgets mixes players who disagree on roughly a
  fifth of the positions, and on ~2 % of them one of the two is making a serious error.
  RECOMMENDATION: record the budget WITH the data, and never mix budgets in one training set --
  or filter to positions where the two budgets agree, which keeps 81% of G.
