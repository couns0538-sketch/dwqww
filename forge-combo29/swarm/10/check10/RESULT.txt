=== `go nodes` vs `go softnodes`, same N, startpos (structural difference, P4)
N        go nodes: depth / nodes      | go softnodes: depth / nodes
N2000    6   / 1740                | 7   / 4121    
N20000   11  / 13604               | 12  / 21600   
N50000   14  / 46854               | 15  / 78579   
N250000  17  / 166281              | 18  / 377485  

`go nodes N` stops UNDER the budget (1740 of 2000) and one depth earlier; `go softnodes N` always
reaches exactly ONE depth further and over the budget. So the two commands differ exactly as intended,
and softnodes is the one that gives a complete iteration -- the right choice for self-play.

=== the one caveat worth recording (P6 FAILED, and that is a real observation)
On startpos the bestmove CHANGES with N: d2d4 at N=2000/20000/50000 but e2e4 at N=250000. Also
tactical: e1g1 at N<=50000, d2d3 at N=250000. That is not a softnodes defect -- it is the engine
preferring a different move once it has searched deeper -- but it means a self-play run at 2000 nodes
and a match run at 250000 nodes are playing DIFFERENT quality, and a data set mixing them is mixing
two players. Worth stating wherever self-play data is used for training.
