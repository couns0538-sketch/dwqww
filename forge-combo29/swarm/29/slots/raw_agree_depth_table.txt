=== Does S14-vs-PC20 move agreement improve with depth? (are disagreements shallow noise?) ===
PC r15 build, 40 real-game positions, Threads 1, Hash 16, fresh process per search.

| depth | positions | same move | share |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 | 40 | 35 | 87.5 % |
| 10 | 40 | 35 | 87.5 % |
| 12 | 40 | 34 | 85.0 % |
| 14 | 40 | 37 | 92.5 % |

Reading: agreement is FLAT (85-92.5 %, mean ~88 %) with no upward trend, so the regimes are not converging as depth
grows. Two consequences:
1. The ~12 % of positions where they differ are NOT shallow-horizon artefacts -- they are genuine differences in what
   the two configurations prefer.
2. Combined with 29-F44 (dead heat 7-7-3 on quality), the picture is that PC20 reaches a DIFFERENT move of comparable
   value on about one position in eight, at every depth. That is what a different-but-equally-good search looks like,
   and it is the honest description -- not "PC20 finds the same move" and not "PC20 finds better moves".

Caveat: 40 positions per depth, so each share carries roughly a +-5 point binomial error band; the flatness is the
claim, not the individual percentages.
