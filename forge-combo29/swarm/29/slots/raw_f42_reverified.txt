WHAT CHANGES AND WHAT SURVIVES:
1. **The depth-10 "sign flip" SHRINKS from +2.3 % to +0.3 %.** It is still not a saving, but it is now inside the
   noise of a per-position node count rather than a clear cost. 29-F41's "the switches are not free at low depth" was
   over-read: they are approximately FREE at depth 10 on R_2911, marginally costly at most.
2. **The match-depth saving is smaller than published: -11.9 % on R_2911 (not -19.5 %) and -10.7 % on G (not -12.7 %).**
   Both still clearly favour PC20, and the sign is stable, but the magnitudes were inflated by small samples in the
   same direction as the flip was.
3. **The stable claim is unchanged and is the one to relay:** PC20 searches a smaller tree at match depths on both
   sets (about 11-12 % here), and the depth-10 comparison is approximately neutral rather than a clear cost.

This is the FIFTH time tonight a small sample has distorted a magnitude or a sign in my own work (29-F38/39, F40/41,
F41/42, F43/44, now F42 itself). The direction of every surviving claim has been stable; the NUMBERS attached to them
have all moved toward smaller effects as the samples grew. That is worth stating plainly in the FINAL: quote the
direction with confidence, quote the magnitude with the n it came from.
