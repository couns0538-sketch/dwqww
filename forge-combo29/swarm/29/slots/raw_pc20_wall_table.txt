=== 11.9 % FEWER NODES BUT NO MEASURABLE TIME SAVING — nodes are not time ===
29-F42/F53 established that PC20 searches 10.7-11.9 % fewer nodes than S14 at match depths. The natural inference is
"so it is faster". This tests that inference directly, and it does not hold.

PC r15 build, 40 positions from R_2911, depth 12, Threads 1, Hash 16, interleaved ABBA (each position run both ways,
order alternating), wall time from sending `go` to reading `bestmove`, fresh process per search.

| metric | S14 | PC20 |
|---|---|---|
| median wall ms | 41 | 36 |
| mean wall ms | 47 | 45 |

**PC20/S14 time ratio = 1.0255 -> -2.49 % speed, 95 % CI [0.9085, 1.1647] -> INDISTINGUISHABLE.**

What this means, and it corrects an inference nobody has stated but everybody would make:
- **Fewer nodes did NOT produce measurable speed.** The medians favour PC20 (36 vs 41 ms) and the means too (45 vs
  47), but the paired CI spans 1.0 comfortably, on a machine shared with other seats. So the honest statement is "no
  measurable wall-clock difference", not "PC20 is 12 % faster".
- **The mechanism is plausible and testable:** the four switches do extra per-node work (`CuckooRep` maintenance,
  `HistRework` blending, `TtPruneV2` and `CorrHistV2` lookups). So a 12 % node reduction CAN be cancelled by a ~12 %
  per-node cost increase. I have not measured per-node cost directly; that is the next step if anyone wants it.
- Consistent with my own 29-N6 and 29-F5: on this shared 4-core box a 2-3 % wall-clock effect is not resolvable
  without very many interleaved pairs, and I am not claiming one.

WHAT TO TELL THE OWNER: the PC20 switches reduce the tree and do not cost move quality, but **there is no evidence
they make the engine faster in wall time**, and the node reduction alone must not be presented as a speed win. If the
owner wants speed, the switches are not the lever; if the owner wants the search shape (smaller tree, same quality),
they are worth having on their own terms.

CAVEAT: n=40 positions, ~40 pairs of wall measurements, on a machine with other agents running. A 12 % wall effect
would be visible; a 3 % one would not.
