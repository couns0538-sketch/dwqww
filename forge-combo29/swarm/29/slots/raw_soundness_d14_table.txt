=== 29-F44 quality test at DEPTH 14 (match-clock depths) -- and it points the OTHER way ===
Prior runs measured quality at depth 10/12 where PC20 and S14 disagree on ~15 % of positions. At depth 14 the
disagreements are fewer but, in this sample, ALL of them favour S14 -- the opposite of the earlier runs and the
first evidence in my own data that PC20's reduced tree can cost something at match depths.

PC r15 build, 30 positions from R_2911, depth 14, Threads 1, Hash 16. Each disagreement judged under the NEUTRAL
S14 regime at depth 17; mover's advantage = negated engine score.

| result | count | share |
|---|---|---|
| SAME bestmove | 27 | 90.0 % |
| DIFFERENT bestmove | 3 | 10.0 % |

The three disagreements:

| pos | S14 move (d14 score) | PC20 move (d14 score) | S14 move's line | PC20 move's line | better |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 4 | c2c3 cp -269 | h3g4 cp -267 | -270 | -303 | S14 |
| 9 | g4e5 cp 3186 | c2a3 cp 3160 | +3504 | +3395 | S14 |
| 27 | h2e2 cp 1357 | e7e4 cp 1353 | **mate -6** | **mate -7** | S14 |

Position 27 is the sharpest: after S14's move the opponent mates in 6, after PC20's in 7 -- S14 is a whole move of
mate-distance better.

WHAT THIS DOES TO 29-F44/F49: it does NOT overturn them (27 of 30 agree; three disagreements cannot outweigh the
7-7-3 and 2-2-1 results at lower depths), but it is the first sample where the disagreements are one-sided, and the
direction is AGAINST PC20. Three observations that keep it in proportion:
1. n = 3. Three disagreements, all favouring one side, is the sort of thing that happens by chance (~1 in 4 for a
   fair coin), so this is a SIGNAL, not a result.
2. The effect, where it appears, is small at positions 4 and 9 (33 and 109 centipawns on a depth-17 line) but
   categorical at 27 (mate distance).
3. It is consistent with the depth-10 sign flip (29-F41/F42): the comparison is depth-dependent in BOTH directions,
   which is exactly why a single depth cannot settle F44.

STATUS: 29-F44 becomes "no sign of damage at depths 10-12 (7-7-3, 2-2-1), but at depth 14 all 3 disagreements in a
30-position sample favour S14". That is a weaker position than an hour ago and it must not be relayed as
"PC20 costs nothing".
