=== THE OPEN QUESTION, CLOSED: depth 14 at 200 positions ===
The one open item in my handover was "does PC20's smaller tree stay quality-neutral at the depths the PC actually
plays (14+)?". This is that measurement at an adequate sample.

PC r15 build, 200 positions from R_2911, depth 14, Threads 1, Hash 16. Each disagreement judged by playing the move
under the NEUTRAL S14 regime at depth 17; mover's advantage = negated engine score.

| result | count | share |
|---|---|---|
| SAME bestmove | 169 | 84.5 % |
| DIFFERENT bestmove | **31** | 15.5 % |

Of the 31 disagreements: **PC20's move better 19, S14's better 10, tie 2.**

ANSWER: **PC20's tree is not merely quality-neutral at depth 14 — it is better in this sample, 19-10-2.** The
question I framed as "would a consistent disadvantage be the most valuable finding" came back the other way, which is
the better outcome for the owner: the switches do not need care at match depths on this evidence.

COMPLETE TALLY ACROSS EVERY SAMPLE I HAVE (all judged under the neutral S14 regime):

| depth | n (positions) | disagreements | PC20 | S14 | tie |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10 | 100 | 17 | 7 | 7 | 3 |
| 10 | 200 | 30 | 13 | 13 | 4 |
| 14 | 80 | 13 | 8 | 4 | 1 |
| 14 | 200 | 31 | **19** | **10** | 2 |
| 10 (late G) | 20 | 5 | 2 | 2 | 1 |
| **total** | **600** | **96** | **49** | **36** | **11** |

96 disagreements: 49 PC20, 36 S14, 11 ties. That is a modest advantage to PC20 overall and NO sample showing a
disadvantage beyond noise. (A sign test on 49-36 gives p ~ 0.10, so even the advantage is not significant — the safe
claim is "no disadvantage anywhere, possibly a small advantage".)

WHAT I GOT WRONG BEFORE REACHING THIS: at n=3 I read 0-3 against PC20 and published it (29-F50); at n=13 the same
configuration gave 8-4 the other way (29-F51); at n=31 it is 19-10 for PC20. The direction moved monotonically toward
PC20 as the sample grew, which is consistent with the small-sample bias I documented in 29-N38 — my errors ran toward
the more alarming story.
