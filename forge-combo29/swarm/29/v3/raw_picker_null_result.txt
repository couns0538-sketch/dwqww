## Why this is the honest outcome and what it implies
The picker's cost is **memory traffic through three history tables per candidate move**, not the arithmetic around it.
Squeezing it needs a structural change — fewer tables read per move, or a layout that makes one read serve more
candidates — and every such change alters the score values, which makes it a search change rather than a speed patch
under file 7's identity rule (every bench number AND every per-position node row AND every G eval must stay identical).
So the honest report for Step 2 is: **no bit-exact picker patch found on the v3 path in this session.** The
attribution stands as the useful artefact; the patch does not exist yet.
