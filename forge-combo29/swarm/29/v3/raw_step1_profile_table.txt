## Why this matters more than the prediction being wrong
The obvious assumption — that a net-driven engine spends most of its time in the net — is FALSE on this build. The
hand-written move picker is the biggest single cost centre on the v3 path, much as it was on the built-in path (my
29-F36: picker family ~4.3 % of a bench-13 run, scoring 3.8x selection). So the speed lever for the PC's real path is
**the move picker**, and file 7's framing ("a faster v3 path is the most useful speed find there is") needs
qualifying: a faster v3 path is worth at most the 19.75 % its family occupies.

Caveat: perf samples on a shared machine, per-symbol error near ±1 point; 19.75 % vs 20.75 % is a tie within that
error, so the honest statement is **"the move picker costs about as much as the whole net"**, not "more".
