=== Widening 29-C6: does the S-F23 mechanism (PC20 removes mate-PV truncation) hold at scale? ===
PC r15 build, 100 positions from R_2911, depth 12, Threads 1, Hash 16, fresh process per regime.
A mate score is TRUNCATED when the PV is shorter than 2N-1 (N>0) or 2|N| (N<0).

| regime | mate positions | truncated PV | rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| S14 | 19 | 0 | 0.0 % |
| PC20 | 20 | 0 | 0.0 % |

RESULT: on this position set S14 shows NO truncation at all, so PC20 cannot "remove" it. Both regimes are clean.

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR MY OWN 29-C6 -- and it is a weakening, not a confirmation:
- 29-C6 confirmed S-F23 on the TWO boards seat 05 published (O+G/192 and R/120). That stands: on those exact boards
  S14 truncated both and PC20 neither, reproduced on my harness.
- But the MECHANISM does not generalise to 100 positions from my own random-game set: there S14 never truncates, so
  the comparison has no defect to fix. Seat 05's own S-F21/S-F23 already noted the effect is per-position, and this
  is consistent with that -- the truncation is a property of certain boards, not of the S14 option set.
- So the correct state of 29-C6 is: CONFIRMS S-F23 ON ITS OWN TWO BOARDS, and does NOT show that S14 truncates in
  general. Anyone quoting 29-C6 as "PC20 fixes mate-PV truncation" is over-reading it; the honest form is "on the
  two boards seat 05 identified, the difference reproduces exactly".

Sample caveat: 19-20 mate positions per regime is small, and a set with more mate-heavy boards would test it better.
R_2911 at depth 12 yields only ~20 mate scores in 100 positions.
