# PREDICTION — the PC's match node budgets: `go nodes 20000 / 50000 / 250000`

File 6 says the owner's matches use `go nodes` at 20,000 / 50,000 / 250,000 on the PC build under PC20 at
Threads 1. The most valuable question is not determinism (S-F16 already showed `go nodes` is deterministic here)
but: does the MOVE change as the budget rises, and is any move at a real budget demonstrably wrong?

P1. The move is the SAME at all three budgets on most positions: predicted the same move at 20000 and 250000 on
    at least half of 40 G positions. Kills it: fewer than a third (a move changing with more search is normal,
    but that would make the budget a strong determinant and worth its own study).
P2. Every move at every budget is a LEGAL move from the position (checked with python-chess), on all 40 x 3.
    Kills it: any illegal move (a serious fault).
P3. The score IMPROVES for the side to move as the budget rises, in the sense that the score does not get worse
    by more than 30 cp from 20000 to 250000 on most positions. Predicted at least 60 % within 30 cp. Kills it:
    fewer than 40 % (a large score regression with more search would be suspicious).
P4. A move that appears at 20000 but is replaced at 250000 is predicted to be a move the deeper search rates at
    most 30 cp worse — not a blunder replaced by a much better move. Kills it: any replacement improving the
    score by more than 100 cp (that would mean the lower budget plays a real blunder, the most valuable possible
    find here, and I would report it with the position).
P5. Both `go nodes` budgets finish a depth and report it, so the reported depth differs between 20000 and 250000.
    Kills it: the same depth at both (then the node limit is not doing anything).
