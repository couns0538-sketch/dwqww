# PREDICTION — area 6 on the PC build under PC20: does each new option change the colour asymmetry?

Build: PC r15 (/home/ubuntu/builds/bld-pc/forge), verified against all six known numbers in pcbuild/PC_BUILD.txt.
Settings: Threads 1, Hash 16 (swarm rule: scores are Threads 1).
Depths: 1 and 2 (the instrument that gave 336/334 and 25/30 on the base tree), on 20 G positions plus the
S-F8/S-F12 probe position, mirror pair each time, fresh process and `ucinewgame` before every search.

Configurations: PC20 as file 5 defines it (S14 + the six), then PC20 with each of the four search options
turned OFF one at a time, then all four off (= S14 + MateAlphaGuard + OwnBook false).

P1. Under PC20 the colour asymmetry still exists at depth 2: predicted fewer than half of the 20 pairs have
    equal node counts. Kills it: 10 or more equal (then the PC build is materially more symmetric and I say so).
P2. No single one of CuckooRep / HistRework / TtPruneV2 / CorrHistV2 turns the asymmetry off. Kills it: any
    configuration reaching 20/20 equal node counts (that would name the component).
P3. The SCORE asymmetry (scores unequal across a mirror pair) is predicted LOWER on the PC build under PC20
    than on the base tree under S14, because three of the four options are "make the search more consistent"
    changes. Kills it: strictly more unequal scores on PC20 than on base+S14 at the same depth.
P4. At depth 1 the base tree had all 20 scores equal but node counts unequal (9/20 in my earlier full-window
    sweep). Predicted the same shape on PC20: scores mostly equal, nodes mostly unequal at depth 1.
    Kills it: a depth-1 score mismatch (that would be an evaluation asymmetry, a real find).
P5. The per-option node totals will differ from each other (each option changes the tree). Kills it: two
    configurations giving byte-identical node totals on all 20 positions (which would suggest the option did
    not take effect, and I would then check its option line).
