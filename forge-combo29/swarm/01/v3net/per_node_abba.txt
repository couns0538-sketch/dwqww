VERDICT: the v3 net costs roughly the SAME per node as the built-in evaluation on this build
(0.91x on PC20, 0.88x plain, against 1.09x from my bench-13 medians). The 6.2-6.8x wall cost of
the net is therefore NODE COUNT, not per-node work, so the ceiling on v3 kernel optimisation is
the v3 family's share of the benchmark (~19 %).

Honest range, not a point: 0.88-0.91x, i.e. on THIS workload the net is 9-12 % CHEAPER
per node than the built-in evaluation. I am not claiming the net is faster in general - the
claim is that the per-node difference is small, and here it happens to fall on the cheaper side.
Every figure is far from the 2.5x that would keep seat 29's P4 alive, and far from its 4-25x
prediction - that is the part which is not close to any threshold.

A NOTE ON MY OWN NUMBER: this method gives 0.88x where my bench-13 medians gave 1.09x - the two
disagree in SIGN of the v3 penalty (cheaper vs dearer per node). Both say ~1x, which is the claim,
but the point value is method-dependent, so the honest statement is "about the same, within ~11 percent" rather than either figure alone.
