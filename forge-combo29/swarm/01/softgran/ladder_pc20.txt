WHAT THIS IS FOR: seat 19's own closing question was how many distinct searches a budget range buys.
A pair test (their 2000 vs 3000) can only report a collision rate. This table shows WHERE the rungs are,
which is what someone choosing budgets needs: two budgets inside one gap buy the identical search, so
an experiment comparing them measures nothing, and the fix is to pick budgets that straddle a rung.
