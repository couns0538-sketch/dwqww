# PREDICTION — area 1, job 2 (extreme numbers in the FEN fields)

Committed and pushed ALONE before the first measuring command of job 2.

## What the job will do
Feed the engine FENs whose *non-board* fields are extreme or illegal, and record the exact answer lines,
`go depth 4`, sanitizer lines, and `perft 2` against python-chess wherever python-chess accepts the FEN.

Fields exercised (each on a legal start-derived board AND on a legal mid-game board, so the board itself is
never the reason for a refusal):
  halfmove clock: 0, 99, 100, 150, 9999, -1, 65535, 65536, 100000
  fullmove number: 0, 1, 10^9, -1, 65536
  ep square on a wrong rank: e3 for White to move, e6 for Black to move (both impossible)
  ep square with no moved pawn behind it
  two kings of one colour (two white kings / two black kings)
  zero kings of a colour
  pawns on rank 1 and on rank 8
Build: ASan+UBSan (`~/builds/asan/forge`), plus the release build as a cross-check that the answer lines match.

## Numbers I predict (each with the range that kills it)

P1. FENs refused outright: 13 to 18 of the 24 cases (my reading: every illegal-boards case is refused, every
    counter-only case is accepted). Below 10 or above 20 kills my reading of `set_fen`.
P2. `halfmove -1` and `fullmove -1` are REFUSED with `invalid FEN fields`. Any acceptance kills it.
P3. `fullmove 10^9` is ACCEPTED. That single acceptance is the case I most expect to be interesting:
    the field is validated as `>= 1` and then stored into a 16-bit member, so I predict the engine silently
    keeps 65535. Any crash here kills P4 as well.
P4. Crashes: 0. Sanitizer lines: 0. Any one line kills this.
P5. `perft 2` equals python-chess's perft 2 for EVERY case where both accept the FEN: 0 differences.
    One difference kills it.
P6. The engine's accept/refuse answer is the SAME on the release build and on the ASan+UBSan build for all
    24 cases (the parser has no build-dependent path). One disagreement kills it.
P7. No case produces `unknown option`, `invalid value`, or `illegal move` — I send no `setoption` at all.

## Why
`Board::set_fen` (src/board.cpp 211-…) reads `halfmove` and `fullmove` into `long long`, refuses
`halfmove < 0`, refuses `fullmove < 1`, then stores
`static_cast<std::uint16_t>(std::min<long long>(halfmove, 65535))` and the same for fullmove. That is a clamp,
not a refusal, so a huge fullmove should be accepted with a silently different number. The board legality
checks (one king per colour, no pawn on rank 1/8, castling right needs a real rook, ep square must sit on rank 3
or 6 with the right pawn behind it) are all hard refusals, so I expect those cases refused.
