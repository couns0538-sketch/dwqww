# PREDICTION — area 1, job 1 (FEN input, mutated FENs, ASan + UBSan build)

Committed and pushed ALONE before the first measuring command of job 1.

## What the job will do
20,000 FENs made from G (353 positions, set_G.epd) and R (random-game positions, SEED 101) by one random mutation
each (replace / insert / delete one character; swap two fields; drop one field). Each FEN sent as
`position fen <FEN>` + `isready` + `go depth 2`, one engine process per 500 FENs, ASan+UBSan build.
Control: a 3,000-character first field must be refused without a crash.

## Numbers I predict (each with the range that kills it)

P1. Refused by the engine: 72 % of the 20,000 (60 %–85 %). Outside that range my reading of `set_fen` is wrong.
P2. Accepted by the engine but rejected by python-chess (INVALID): between 5 and 300 FENs (0–2,000).
    Above 2,000 means the FEN filter is far looser than the code I read suggests.
    My named cause: consecutive digits in a rank are ADDED, so "17"/"44" style ranks and
    digit-sum-8-but-wrong shapes pass.
P3. Crashes (engine process dies before `bestmove`): 0–2. Above 2 is a real fault.
P4. Sanitizer lines (ASan/UBSan) on the whole run: 0. Any single line kills this prediction.
P5. Illegal bestmoves (bestmove not legal per python-chess on the position the engine says it kept): 0–3.
P6. Control FEN with a 3,000-character first field: refused with an `info string invalid FEN` line, no crash,
    no sanitizer line. Any crash here kills it.
P7. Accept/refuse decision is stable: no FEN accepted in one process run and refused in another.
    Any instability kills it.

## Why
I read `Board::set_fen` (src/board.cpp lines 211-…) before writing this: it counts a rank as "file" summed over
digits, requires exactly one king per side, refuses pawns on ranks 1/8, checks the piece inventory and promoted
counts, requires the castling right to sit on a real king+rook, validates the ep square against the pawn that
would have to have just moved, refuses adjacent kings and a side that left its own king in check. What it does
NOT check is what a legal game can reach beyond that (e.g. two bishops on the same colour square from promotion
maths, or pawn placement that no game could reach). So most mutations should be refused, a small tail accepted.
