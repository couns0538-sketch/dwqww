# PREDICTION — seat 20, area 10, job 14 (how far does `LmpBase=8` losing the mate reach?)

Committed alone BEFORE the first measuring command. Never edited afterwards.

Background (my own S-F19): on `8/8/1p6/8/p3p3/8/2Kpk3/8 w - - 0 1` at depth 12, `setoption name LmpBase value 8`
(default 4) turns `mate -7` into `cp -3114`, i.e. the mate score disappears. One position at one depth is a
suspicion; this job asks whether it is systematic.

Method, all with MY harness: for a small ladder of forced-mate positions I construct (a mate in 1, a mate in 2, a
mate in 3, and the S-F19 board), search at depths 8..14 with LmpBase default (4) and with LmpBase 8, and judge each
PV with python-chess.

Predictions:
1. At LmpBase=8 the mate score DOES NOT disappear on simple mates: the mate-in-1 and mate-in-2 positions still
   report `mate` at every depth 8..14.
   KILLED IF: any of them reports a cp score at LmpBase=8.

2. The effect is specific to positions where the mate needs a LONG defensive line (the S-F19 board, mate ~7): at
   LmpBase=8 it reports cp at least at one depth in 8..14 and reports a mate at a greater depth.
   KILLED IF: LmpBase=8 reports mate at every depth 8..14 on that board.

3. Where LmpBase=8 reports a mate, its claimed distance will be LARGER (a longer mate) than the default's at the
   same depth — a pruning setting should make the engine see a longer defence, not a shorter one.
   KILLED IF: LmpBase=8 systematically claims a shorter mate than the default.

4. The mate scores at LmpBase=8, when they appear, still verify as real checkmates in exactly the claimed plies
   (i.e. the remaining error is distance, not correctness).
   KILLED IF: a checkmate verdict fails on a full-length PV.

5. Conclusion shape: I expect to report this as "LmpBase=8 delays mate discovery on long mates, and can replace a
   mate score with a large cp score at intermediate depths", NOT as "LmpBase=8 is broken".
