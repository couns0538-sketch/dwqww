## verdict — the positive control 19-F9 was missing
A `go depth 16` workload with 4 cores: **Threads 1 median 154 ms, Threads 4 median 117 ms, ratio 0.765** —
a 23.5 % speedup, clearly DETECTED. So the timing method used for 19-F9's null CAN see a real speed difference;
its "not measurably faster" therefore means the effect was genuinely below the resolution, not that the method
was blind.

## a failed control attempt, kept
My FIRST attempt used `bench 13` at Threads 1 vs 4 and got 344 vs 340 ms (ratio 0.988, NOT detected). That is not
a failure of the timing method: `bench` constructs its own `Searcher(16)` (19-F37), so the `Threads` option never
reaches the bench searcher. **A positive control must use a workload the setting actually affects** — which is the
same lesson as the Hash finding, applied to the control itself.
