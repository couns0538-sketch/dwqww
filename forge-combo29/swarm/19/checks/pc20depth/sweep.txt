## the flip, and the mechanism
**PC20's depth advantage flips between 2,000 and 3,000 nodes:** -2 at 2000, **+1 at 3000**, +2 at 5000, +5 at
8000, +3 at 12000, +3 at 20000. So the self-play budget (`softnodes 2000`) sits on the wrong side by about
**1,000 nodes**.

**Which option costs the ply at 2,000?** One-option-at-a-time at softnodes 2000 (baseline S14 total depth 45):
CuckooRep 45 (+0), HistRework 45 (+0), **TtPruneV2 44 (-1)**, CorrHistV2 45 (+0). So **TtPruneV2 is the single
option that costs depth at the self-play budget** — its extra pruning needs more nodes than 2,000 to pay for
itself.

## what the owner can act on
- **At `softnodes 2000` (his self-play), PC20 is mildly counterproductive: 2 plies shallower.**
- **At 3,000 nodes or above it turns positive**, and by 8,000 it is +5.
- **If he raises the self-play budget to ~3,000 or more, PC20's switches start paying** — that is the concrete
  recommendation, and it costs 50 % more nodes per self-play move.

## the caveat on this sweep
Six positions, one run each. The individual deltas (-2, +1, +2) are within the per-position noise 19-C40 and
19-C45 already documented; **the TREND is the finding, not any single row.**
