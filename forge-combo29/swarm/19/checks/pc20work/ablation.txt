## WHY MateAlphaGuard is EXACTLY 1.000 — read from the source, not inferred

## So the finding is sharper than "no measurable effect"
It is: **MateAlphaGuard can only fire when the search reaches a node whose aspiration window's alpha is a mate
score.** On the four positions tested at depth 14 that never happens, so the option cannot be attributed any
node-count effect whatsoever. Anyone claiming it does something on ordinary positions must first show a node where
alpha is mate-valued.
