## So the honest state of 19-C46's recommendation
**19-C46's DEPTH result stands** (the sign flips between 2,000 and 3,000 nodes, `TtPruneV2` the culprit). **But
the follow-on claim — that raising the budget makes PC20's switches "pay" in decision quality — is NOT
established.** The depth gain is real; whether it converts into better moves at 3,000 nodes is unmeasured here,
and eight positions is too few to find out.

**What that means for the recommendation:** "raise self-play to ~3,000" is supported by **depth**, and by
nothing else yet. A decision-quality claim would need a proper sample (and ideally games), not eight positions.

## The general rule this produced (the part worth keeping)
**A zero needs a plant. A UNIFORM zero needs a better metric.**
The plant answers "could the harness have seen one?" It does NOT answer "could the METRIC have shown one?"
- `0 of 8` in ONE arm = a candidate null; plant it.
- `0 of 8` in EVERY arm = the metric hitting its floor. Planting it would only prove it is *reliably* broken.
- My score-equality-vs-depth-18 test was the second kind: a 2,000-node search legitimately disagrees with a
  depth-18 score, so no engine at any setting could have scored above zero. **The engine was never under test.**
