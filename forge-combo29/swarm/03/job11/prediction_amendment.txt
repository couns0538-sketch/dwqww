PREDICTION AMENDMENT — job 11, the position set had to change, and why

The first generator (`gen_loneking.py`, engine-vs-engine games at depth 6) produced **0 usable candidates from 40
games**, and the reason is itself a finding about this build, not a bug in my set:

every game collapsed into the SAME position, `8/8/8/8/8/5n2/4K1k1/4N3 b - - 11 60` (K+N vs K+N), where the engine
answers `cp 0` after 492 nodes at depth 12 and python-chess says the position is NOT insufficient material. That is
the known drawn-root behaviour (FABLE_TO_swarm_3: "a drawn root ... the engine searches 0 nodes and plays its first
legal move"), reached here through bare-king play. Self-play at low depth therefore cannot produce a varied lone-king
population at all.

Amendment: instead of self-play games, generate lone-king endings by **material-imbalanced play**: start each game
from a random opening position, let both sides play at depth 6, and record the first position where one side owns
nothing but its king. To get variety the games must not all reach the same fortress, so the set is built from
several distinct material preconditions (an extra rook, two rooks, a queen, a rook plus a minor, and a minor pair),
which is how real games actually reach lone-king endings — one side has converted an advantage.

This is a change to the SAMPLING, not to the definition of the signature. The signature (chosen move scores worse
than an alternative by more than a queen, by the engine's own scores at depth 16) and the threshold are unchanged, so
the prediction below still stands as stated for the rate.
