Reasoning behind the numbers (written before measuring): an adaptive time manager that stops on
`movetime` should add only the loop latency of one 1 ms poll, so the median sits 1-2 ms above the
budget; the largest sits a few ms higher because a single poll can be delayed by the OS scheduler on a
busy 4-core box while the other 2-3 jobs of this seat run. `wtime/30+winc` is the most common
allocation and I expect this engine to use a similar fraction, giving ~43 ms for the 1 s case and
~433 ms for the 10 s case; I predict it slightly under and slightly over respectively because I do not
know the divisor. Kill ranges are deliberately wide: the point of this job is the distribution's tail,
not a single number.
