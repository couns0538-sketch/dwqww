Committed and pushed ALONE before the first measuring command. Never edited afterwards.

## What S-F20 established
On PC r15/PC20 at Threads 1, 20 positions per clock, the tail shape reproduces (p99/median 2.20-4.43)
but absolute p99 is 1.5-1.8x LOWER than seat 08's base/S14 numbers at every clock, and 0/120 searches
exceeded its clock. Two explanations fit that symptom:
  (D) the PC build searches DEEPER per unit time (faster search or better pruning), so the same soft plan
      buys more depth — a strength;
  (S) the PC build STOPS EARLIER for the same clock (shorter plan, earlier cut), so it uses less time —
      not a strength, just less work.

The measurement that separates them: per cell, the achieved time AND the last completed depth, on BOTH
builds, with each build on its own settings (base: Threads 1/Hash 16/OwnBook false/S14; PC: PC20).

| # | measurement | predicted | range that KILLS it |
|---|---|---|---|
| V1 | median achieved time, PC vs base, at wtime 1000 | PC lower by 15-45 % | PC within 10 %, or PC higher |
| V2 | median last depth, PC vs base, at wtime 1000 | PC equal or higher | PC strictly lower at the median |
| V3 | nodes per second, PC vs base (from the last info line) | PC higher | PC lower |
| V4 | for a matched achieved time, PC reaches a greater depth | yes | no |
| V5 | the PC build's achieved/soft ratio vs base's | PC lower (stops earlier relative to its plan) | PC higher |
| V6 | the ratio of depths PC/base at wtime 30000 | 1.0-1.4 | below 0.9 |

Reasoning: the PC patch adds four search switches (CuckooRep, HistRework 2, TtPruneV2, CorrHistV2) which
are pruning/ordering changes, and a faster net update in nnue_v3.cpp. Pruning changes reduce nodes per
depth, so at a fixed time budget I expect MORE depth, not less — V2/V3/V6 predict the "deeper" branch. But
I also cannot rule out that the PC's time manager simply budgets less: its bench 11 under PC20 is 278,024
nodes vs S14's 242,796, so at the same nps the bench takes longer, which is not evidence either way about
the clock plan. If V1 and V5 both show the PC stopping earlier with no depth gain, the honest answer is
"stops earlier", and S-F20 is not a strength claim.
