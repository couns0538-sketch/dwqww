## Reading

The 'saving' here is not the overspend — it is the whole time difference between stopping at
the last completion inside soft and continuing to the depth actually reached. That is the
honest bound for a soft-scaled rule: it buys the saving AT the ply cost shown, because the
iterations it refuses are precisely the ones that produced the extra depth.
