## CASE B — abandon an iteration that would have been DISCARDED (costs NOTHING)

At the real 6+0.06 control, `wall_ms - engine_time_ms` is time spent on an iteration the search
threw away. Abandoning at the soft plan recovers that span at ZERO ply cost.

| base | 60 | 1 ms | 578 ms | 1 |
