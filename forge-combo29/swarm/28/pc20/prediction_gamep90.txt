# PREDICTION — S28: per-move search-time p90 at 6 s + 0.06 on real game positions

Committed and pushed ALONE before the first measuring command. Never edited afterwards.
Written 2026-11-16 ~23:17 UTC.

## The question, from the coordinator
"the per-move 90th percentile of search time at 6s+0.06, since a median of 105 ms against a 60 ms
increment means the tail decides when the clock runs out."

## What I will measure, and what I will NOT repeat
Real engine-vs-engine games, PC20, Threads 1, Hash 16, with a **real countdown**: each side starts at
6000 ms, and after each move its clock loses the search wall time and gains 60 ms. The engine is sent the
ACTUAL remaining clocks, which is what a GUI does. A side whose clock reaches zero is flagged and loses.

**I am explicitly avoiding the constant-clock design** I used in S-F30, because I then proved in S-F32/S-F34
that a constant 6000 ms clock inflates the tail (constant gave a 1585 ms worst case; the same positions under
a countdown gave max 828 ms). Using a constant clock here would manufacture the answer.

## My own measurements to compare against
- median 105 ms is the coordinator's figure (another seat, self-play). My `realclock` median at constant
  clock was 246/226 ms; under a countdown (S-F30 follow-up) 155 ms.
- So I expect the game median to land 60-160 ms, closer to the countdown figure than the constant one.

| # | measurement | predicted | range that KILLS it |
|---|---|---|---|
| G1 | median search time per move | 100 ms | outside 50..200 ms |
| G2 | p90 search time per move | 300 ms | outside 120..600 ms |
| G3 | p90 / median ratio | 3.0 | outside 1.5..6.0 |
| G4 | max single search | 1200 ms | outside 500..1700 ms |
| G5 | fraction of moves whose search exceeds the 60 ms increment | 85 % | outside 60..100 % |
| G6 | games where a side is flagged on time within 250 plies | 0 of 4 | 2 or more |
| G7 | the clock declines over the game (not flat) | yes | flat or rising |

Reasoning for G5: the median is above the increment, so most moves lose clock even though 60 ms is added —
that is the mechanism the coordinator named. For G6: at 6 s with ~35 moves per side and ~150 ms average, a
side spends ~5.2 s and gains ~2.1 s, so it should survive a 100-move game but the margin is thin at the
p90 — a p90 of 300 ms over 35 moves would be 10.5 s and would flag. So G6 = 0 depends on how many moves
actually reach the p90, and I may be wrong; if games flag, that is the most useful possible answer.
