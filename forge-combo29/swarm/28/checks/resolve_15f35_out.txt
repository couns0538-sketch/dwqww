## 1. ARITHMETIC: iterate the clock rule with a WORST-CASE search (always spends its full hard plan)

`clock' = clock - hard(clock) + 60`, which is the most a move can cost.

| move | clock ms | hard plan ms | spend | clock after |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 6000 | 1585.0 | 1585.0 | 4475 |
| 2 | 4475 | 1235.0 | 1235.0 | 3300 |
| 3 | 3300 | 970.0 | 970.0 | 2390 |
| 4 | 2390 | 765.0 | 765.0 | 1685 |
| 5 | 1685 | 605.0 | 605.0 | 1140 |
| 6 | 1140 | 480.0 | 480.0 | 720 |
| 7 | 720 | 385.0 | 385.0 | 395 |
| 8 | 395 | 231.0 | 231.0 | 224 |
| 9 | 224 | 128.0 | 128.0 | 156 |
| 10 | 156 | 87.0 | 87.0 | 129 |
| ... | **the clock stops falling at 111 ms after move 15** | | | |

lowest clock reachable under a worst-case spend: **111 ms** (never below MoveOverhead + increment = 70 ms)

Seat 15's 6000/73 = 82 moves assumes a CONSTANT 73 ms drain. The drain is not constant: it is
`hard(clock) - 60`, which SHRINKS as the clock shrinks, reaching zero at the floor. That is why the
clock approaches a floor instead of crossing zero.
