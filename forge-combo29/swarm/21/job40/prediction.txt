written before the first measuring command.

window-bounded alpha-beta with a transposition table and a node cap; any position the cap stops is
recorded UNDECIDED and never counted as a hit.

| P5 | 0 crashes, 0 sanitizer lines | killed by any one |

Direction: I expect P1 to hold at the low end (85-90) and fail towards 94 (15 half-moves is deep).
Whatever fraction I can decide, the report states it and the count is only over decided positions.
