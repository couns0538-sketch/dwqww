VERDICT: CONFIRMS seat 05 S-F8

`go infinite` at a terminal root never answers: 30 s with no bestmove, on BOTH a checkmate root and
a stalemate root, while `go depth 5` on the same board answers immediately and `go infinite` on an
ordinary board answers as soon as `stop` arrives. So the trigger is the terminal root (no legal moves)
combined with an unbounded search.

BEYOND their claim (this is the part that matters for a GUI): with `stop`, the terminal root DOES
answer — case B (checkmate root, `go infinite` + `stop` after 200 ms) gave 0000 in 0.21 s, case F (stalemate root) gave 0000 in 0.21 s.
So the failure is narrower than 'stop cannot rescue it': the engine never volunteers a bestmove at a
terminal root under `go infinite`, and only an explicit `stop` produces one. A GUI that uses
`go infinite`/`ponder` and relies on the engine finishing by itself, or that never sends stop because
the position looks terminal, will hang; a GUI that always sends stop is fine.

Both terminal kinds behave the same way, so this is about 'no legal moves' rather than about mate.
