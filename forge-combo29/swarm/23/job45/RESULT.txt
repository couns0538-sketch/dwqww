JOB 44's ASYMMETRY WAS MY OWN TYPO — withdrawn, and the corrected 2x2 is symmetric
======================================================================================
This file records a retraction and the measurement that replaces it. Nothing in job 44's output is deleted; its cell is
explained as a measurement of a DIFFERENT position than the one I labelled.

WHAT I CLAIMED (job 44, one cell per side, never written into FINDINGS.md)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
On two structurally identical threefold roots the base appeared to behave ASYMMETRICALLY by side to move:
  T-W (white to move, winning): base cp 0, **0 nodes**, d5d1.
  T-L (black to move, lost):    base cp -405, **21,755 nodes**, b1c2.
Read as: the engine bails instantly only when the side to move is WINNING.

WHY IT WAS WRONG
----------------
Job 44's T-L move list was typed by hand and is **illegal at ply 7**:

    a1b1 d5d6 b1a1 d6d5 a1b1 d5d6 a1b1 d6d5
                                     ^ ply 7: a1b1 is not legal in 1K6/8/3R4/8/8/8/8/1k6 b - - 6 4

python-chess rejects it (`job45/job45_fixture_check.out`). The ENGINE does not reject it — it prints

    info string illegal move a1b1

and **continues searching the position reached by the first six moves**, which is a ONE-repetition position, not a
threefold. So the T-L cell measured a legal, ongoing, lost position, and cp -405 with 21,755 nodes is exactly what such
a position should score. There was never an asymmetry; there was a typo and a silent-recovery path in the engine that
turned it into a plausible-looking number.

THE ENGINE IS SYMMETRIC BY SIDE, AND THE COUNT IS WHAT DRIVES IT: at TWO occurrences both builds search and report a
win/loss normally (base 22,108 nodes and 15,158 nodes). At THREE occurrences BOTH builds return cp 0 on BOTH sides.
The base returns 0 nodes in both cases — the documented drawn-root defect (23-F43). The PC returns 104 nodes on L-3
and 56,153 on W-3, so its repair searches briefly in both cases as well.

WHAT SURVIVES AS A FINDING, AND WHAT IT IS WORTH
-------------------------------------------------
1. **The base bails at ZERO nodes on a threefold root on EITHER side** — this is 23-F43 restated for the repetition
   case with a matched 2-vs-3-occurrence control, which 23-F43 did not have (it used a halfmove-150 root). The 2-vs-3
   contrast is what makes it a claim about the REPETITION RULE rather than about the fifty-move counter: the same
   board, the same side to move, only one more cycle, and 22,108 nodes become 0.
2. **The PC's repair moves the score on the winning side but NOT on the losing side**: W-3 goes cp 362 against the
   base's cp 0, while L-3 stays cp 0. Both are cp 0 on the base. So where the base and PC disagree, they disagree only
   where the side to move is winning — on the losing side the repair changes the node count and the move, not the
   score. That is a narrower and better-supported version of what I thought I had found in job 44, arrived at by
   having the typo pointed out by a validator rather than by reasoning.
3. **An operational hazard worth its own line, and the most transferable thing here: an illegal move in a `position
   ... moves` list produces ONE `info string` line and a SEARCH ON A DIFFERENT POSITION.** The engine does not refuse
   the command, does not return to the root, and does not stop. A harness that does not validate its own move lists
   against an independent implementation will silently measure whatever position the legal prefix reaches, and every
   number it reports will be internally consistent and about the wrong board. This is the same family as 23-C23
   (refused FEN -> previous game's board) with a different trigger.

INSTANCE 19 OF THE FAMILY, and the sharpest one: my fixture had been "validated" by looking at the engine's output. A
move list is an INPUT, and an input needs a checker from OUTSIDE the system under test. python-chess rejected the list
instantly; the engine never complained. **Validate inputs with an independent implementation, never with the system
being measured** — because the system being measured is precisely the thing whose error handling may be wrong.

PROCESS NOTE: I had already drafted the asymmetry as a finding. It is not in FINDINGS.md, and the withdrawal is
recorded here rather than the claim being quietly dropped. The prediction for job 45 said in advance that a single
disagreeing cell would mean "asymmetry not established" — and a single cell was all there was.
