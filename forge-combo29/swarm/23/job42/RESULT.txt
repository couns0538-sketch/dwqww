P3 **KILLED IN SUBSTANCE while its letter survived — and this is why the job was worth running.** I predicted the short

THE OPERATIONAL RULE THIS EARNS
-------------------------------
**A mate score is unverified unless its PV length is at least 2N−1 AND the replay ends in checkmate.** Both halves are
needed, and neither alone suffices: W2 depth 14 satisfies neither, W2 depth 16 satisfies both, W1 depth 13 satisfies
the length test's inverse only, and W1 depth 14 has a 14-ply PV (not even of mate shape) with a mate score on it. A
depth-based shortcut does not substitute: W2 depth 16 reports mate 10 (needs 19 plies) at depth 16 and does deliver 19
plies, while W1 depth 13 reports mate 8 (needs 15) at depth 13 and delivers 13 — so "2N−1 > depth" neither proves nor
disproves anything. Compare against PV LENGTH, then replay.

WHAT I AM NOT CLAIMING
----------------------
I have NOT established that mate 10 is the shortest mate on W2. Depths 16, 17 and 18 all report it and all replay, so
it is a real forced mate in 10; a shorter one would need a prover, and my prover already failed its own control once in
this seat (23-F49), so I did not use one here. The claim is narrower and still useful: the depth-14/15 score of mate 13
is WRONG, because mate 10 exists and was found two depths later — 13 is not a refutation of 10 but an overestimate
alongside it.

RETRACTION, appended after seat 27's refutation — READ BEFORE CITING ANYTHING ABOVE
==================================================================================
**The central claim of this file — that W2's `mate 13` at depth 14-15 was a WRONG DISTANCE — is WITHDRAWN.**

Seat 27 ran an exhaustive forced-mate search on this same board and established **true DTM = mate 6**. My independent
re-run on the PC build (`job57/w2_deep_verify.out`) shows the engine converging to it:

| depth | score | plies needed | PV plies |
|---|---|---|---|
| 14 | mate 13 | 25 | 17 |
| 16 | mate 10 | 19 | 19 |
| 18 | mate 10 | 19 | 19 |
| 20 | mate 7 | 13 | 13 |
| 22 | mate 7 | 13 | 13 |
| **24** | **mate 6** | **11** | **11** |

Every PV is exactly `2N-1` from depth 16 onward and the value stabilises at the true DTM by depth 24. The base does the
same with a lag (cp 1220 to depth 20, then mate 11 at 22, mate 10 at 24). So the decreasing `mate N` is **convergence
from above**, which is what an improving search does — not a false score.

What this file got right and what it got wrong:
- RIGHT: the PV at depth 14 is 17 plies and does not replay to mate (that observation stands, and belongs to 23-F86/F88).
- WRONG: the inference from "the distance decreases as depth grows" to "the distance is false". Decreasing is the
  signature of convergence toward the true value; a false distance would have to converge somewhere OTHER than the true
  DTM, or oscillate. My P3 was killed by a shape test (odd PV length) and I treated the kill as evidence of a defect
  when the replay's NO-MATE verdict was fully explained by PV truncation alone — one defect, not two.

**Pre-screen rule for any future decreasing `mate N`: ask whether the FINAL value equals the true DTM before writing the
word "defect".** That requires a prover, which is exactly the tool I had failed to make work earlier in this seat
(23-F49) and therefore avoided — and avoiding it is what let this stand.
