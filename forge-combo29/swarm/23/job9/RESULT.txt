ANSWER: on this board the unguarded search is NOT wrong at any depth 12, 13, 14, 15 or 16 — it reports `mate 2`
with a 3-ply PV that python-chess confirms is checkmate, at every depth, and the guarded search reports the
identical score and node count (398/431/464/497/530). So the gap the coordinator asked about does not exist on
this board: there is no band between 12 and 16 where the guard changes anything, which extends seat 01's own
result (they saw agreement at 8, 10, 12 and 16) to 13, 14 and 15 as well.

INDEPENDENT CHECK OF THE SCORE: a brute-force python-chess mate search for white-to-move confirms the only
first move that mates in 2 is `h6h7` ("mate in 2 (3 plies)"), so `mate 2` is the TRUE distance and the engine is
right with the guard off.
