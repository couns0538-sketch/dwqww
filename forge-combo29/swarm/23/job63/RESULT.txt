WHAT THIS DOES TO "THE REPAIR IS ADEQUATE"
-------------------------------------------
Two boards now, and they say different things:

| board | base's move loses | PC's move loses | repair improves the move? |
|---|---|---|---|
| 23-F129 (F60 board 1) | **+6** (a queen capture, knight nets 6) | **+0** | **yes, decisively** |
| this job (F60 board 2) | +1 | +1 | **no, neutral** |

So the accurate statement is **"the repair is adequate where measured, and on one of two boards it is a genuine
improvement over the unsearched move"** — not "the repair fixes the move". On the second board the PC still plays a move
that allows a one-pawn capture, exactly as the base does; what differs is that it searched 3,608 nodes to choose it and
reports the same cp 0.

That is a weaker claim than my last message to the coordinator said, and I am correcting it rather than letting the
stronger version stand. **It is also the third time tonight that a one-fixture statement I framed carefully still
turned out to be one-fixture-wide.**

WHY IT STILL MATTERS FOR THE ADVICE
------------------------------------
The practical recommendation does not reverse: the PC build is still the better choice on drawn roots (it searches, it
reports the draw honestly, and on the larger-loss board it avoids the loss entirely). What changes is the reason — it is
not that the repair reliably picks a better move, but that **it removes the 0-node path**, and the 0-node path is what
produced both losses. On a board where the searched move happens to be as bad as the unsearched one, the repair buys
honesty about the score rather than material safety.

LIMITS: two boards, base `go depth 10`, PC `go depth 12`; static exchange is a one-ply-deep estimate of material, so
"+1" means "a one-pawn capture is available and not immediately recaptured", not "a pawn is lost by force".

**The PC does not claim on 0 of 2 claimable roots — a CONSISTENT policy, not inconsistent behaviour.** So F128's
framing survives a second fixture: on claimable draws the repaired build reports the win, the base reports the draw, and

The third case is reported as excluded rather than dropped: on `8/5K2/7Q/4k3/8/8/8/8 w` no 4-ply castling-neutral cycle
exists (every shuttle available to that position disturbs something), so that board cannot be tested this way.

at three occurrences does not stop the PC from reporting a mate in one — which is consistent with the claim policy and
