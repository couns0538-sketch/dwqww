PREDICTIONS: P1 held (4 games reached a third occurrence, needed >= 3). P2 held (base answered 0 nodes in every one).
P3 held (the base's unsearched move was worse than -100 cp in 2 of the 4 — games 1 and 3 at -805). P4 held (moves
differed in 3 of 4).

WHAT IS NEW AND MATTERS, in game 4: **BOTH builds answered 0 nodes and the same move** (`d1c1`, cp 0 each). So the
defect is NOT universally repaired on the PC — there is at least one naturally reached repetition where the PC also
short-circuits. That is a case file 3's "repaired on the PC since 15 November" does not cover, and it is only visible
because I played games to their own third occurrence rather than reusing one board.

LIMITS, stated because this is a small sample: 4 games, all from seed games this build played against itself in this
session, one settings combination. Two of the six openings did not reach a repetition in 200 plies, so "how often a
game reaches one" is not measured here. And I am not claiming game 4's shared 0 nodes is the same root cause as the
base's — only that the observable is identical.
