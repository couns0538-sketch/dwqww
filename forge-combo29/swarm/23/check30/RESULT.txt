VERDICT: CONFIRMS the engine-side behaviour of 30-F33/F34/F38 on all six boards (base does not report a mate at

REFUTED a correct finding. It was caught only because it also reported None for a textbook mate-in-1 class. A
prover that is never asked a question with a known answer will confidently return a wrong zero. This is 23-F3's
