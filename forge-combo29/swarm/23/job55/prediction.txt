23-F110 established the history gate: the root bails only when its repetition is in the move list. But "its repetition"
is ambiguous between two mechanisms that a tool author needs to distinguish:
  M1 ROOT-SPECIFIC: the engine asks "has the position I am searching occurred 3 times?"
  M2 ANY-REPETITION: the engine asks "does the history contain any position 3 times?" and bails on that alone.
Under M1 a game that repeated some OTHER position three times and then moved on is searched normally. Under M2 it bails
on a position that has never repeated — a much broader and worse defect.

        -> the ROOT has occurred ONCE; some other position occurred 3x

Both move lists constructed by search over legal non-capturing non-checking moves and validated in python-chess:
for T1 I assert the root's occurrence count is exactly 1 and the most-repeated position is exactly 3; for T2 I assert
the root's count is exactly 3. Those assertions are printed BEFORE the engine is called.
