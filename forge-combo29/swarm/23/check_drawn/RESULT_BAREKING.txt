REFINEMENT of seat 30's lone-king claim: the PC's repair needs DEPTH 20 — at depth 12 it is still cp
=====================================================================================================
Seat 30's 30-F33/F34/F38 and my 23-C11 established that at `go depth 24` the BASE reports cp and the PC reports
mate on all six boards. That left the useful question unasked: at WHAT DEPTH does the PC's repair start working?

THE REFINEMENT, which the "repaired on the PC" summary hides:
- At **depth 12 the PC does NOT find the mate on any of the six** — it returns cp on all six, like the base.
- At **depth 20 it finds all six**, and at 24 likewise.
- The BASE never finds them at 12, 20 or 24.

So the PC's repair is a DEEPER-SEARCH effect, not an immediately-correct one: it needs about 20 plies on these
endgames before the mate surfaces. Two consequences:
1. A short time control that only reaches depth 12-16 will see NO benefit from this repair on these boards — the
   base and PC behave alike there (both cp).
2. This makes seat 30's "the PC fixes all six" TRUE AT DEPTH 24 and UNTESTED by them below it; my table shows the
   depth at which it becomes true, which is the practically useful statement.

CAVEAT: all six are forced wins with mates in 7-23 plies, and none of these depths is reached in a fast match on
these positions, so the practical reach of the repair depends on the clock, which I did not measure here.
