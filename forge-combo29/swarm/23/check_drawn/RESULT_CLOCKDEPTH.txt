WHAT THIS CHANGES, stated plainly against my own earlier finding:
- **The base finds the mate on 4 of the 6 boards at a real clock** (rows 1, 2, 4, 5) — so "the base cannot mate with
  a lone king" is NOT a general weakness. It is a DEPTH-SPECIFIC failure: at exactly depth 24 the base returns cp on
  all six, yet at depth 27-32 it mates on four of them.
- The base's two remaining cp rows (3 and 6) are the same two boards where it also fails at depth 24, and the PC
  fixes both.
- The PC reaches a LOWER depth than the base on every board at this clock (17-23 vs 25-32) and still mates on five
  of six; on row 6 it does not reach depth 20 and does not mate.

SO the honest summary, which supersedes the sharper claim I made in 23-F45: the lone-king failures are
**depth-window artefacts**, not a persistent base-tree defect. At the PC's real clock the base mates on 4 of 6 and
the PC on 5 of 6, and the two boards where they differ are rows 3 and 6.
