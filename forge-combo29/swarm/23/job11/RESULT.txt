SO THE CAUSE IS THE MARGIN, and it is measured, not asserted: positions that switch move between budgets are close
calls (median 14 cp apart at a fixed depth, 16 of 17 inside 30 cp), while positions that do not switch have much
wider separation (median 103 cp). A budget that changes the depth by 3-9 plies is enough to flip a 14-cp
preference and nowhere near enough to flip a 100-cp one. 23-F25's rejection of "deeper = different move" and this
result are consistent: depth grows everywhere, but only close positions are decided by it.
