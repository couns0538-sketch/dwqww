PREDICTION — SW-23 job 11 (WHY the budget changes the move)
Written before the first measuring command. Never edited afterwards.

23-F24 showed 18 of 60 quiet positions change bestmove between budgets 20000/50000/250000; 23-F25 rejected
"it searches deeper" as the cause. This job measures the remaining candidate I named: the MARGIN between the top
moves at each budget. For each changed position I will capture, at all three budgets, the full `info` line at the
final depth and — where the engine prints multiple root scores for different moves at that depth — whether the
best move is the same one ply earlier.

Protocol: PC r15, PC20, Threads 1, Hash 16, fresh process per (position, budget), drained to bestmove. I will use
`go searchmoves` on the two candidate moves to get their scores at the same depth, which is the only reliable way
to measure a margin from the UCI output this engine gives.

Prediction (each number with the range that kills it):
  P1  on the changed positions the losing candidate will be within 30 cp of the winner at the budget where it
      loses, i.e. these are CLOSE calls. KILLED if the median gap is > 60 cp.
  P2  on the unchanged positions the runner-up will be further away: median gap > 60 cp. KILLED if <= 30 cp.
  P3  the move chosen at 250000 will be the one with the better score at a FIXED deeper depth on a majority of
      the changed positions (i.e. the change is an improvement, not instability): >= 60 % of them. KILLED if < 40 %.
  P4  at least one changed position will show a score REVERSAL between the two candidates as the budget grows
      (the ordering flips), which is the signature of an unstable margin rather than a monotone search.

If P1/P2 hold, the practical statement is: the budget matters on close positions, and that is where a match
played at one node budget is not reproducible at another. If they do not hold, the margin story is wrong too and
I will say so rather than keep asserting a mechanism.
