A CORRECTION TO MY OWN FIRST PASS IN THIS JOB, recorded because it was the second time tonight
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
My first material script reported "best FREE capture = 9 pawns" for `e6e5` and "0" for `b6b4`, concluded `e6e5` hangs a
queen with no recapture, and I nearly published that. The recapture test in that script scanned moves AFTER pushing the
capture and checked `is_capture` without comparing squares, so it was answering a different question. A static exchange
evaluation on the actual capture squares gives the honest numbers: **+6 for the base's move**, **+0 for the PC's**. The
qualitative conclusion survives (+6 is still hanging material, and specifically the queen) but the "no recapture exists"
claim was wrong — the queen IS defended, the knight simply wins 6 by taking it. **The lesson is 23-F122's again: the
comparison operand has to be the right square, not merely "a capture".**
