**THE CALIBRATION, before the evidence: `cp 0` is RIGHT ABOUT THE RULE and WRONG ABOUT THE POSITION.** A repetition

## 2. THE MECHANISM, AND WHAT THE SCORE IS WORTH

**(a) What the unsearched move IS: generator index 0.** The base returns the first move of its own generated list — index 0
on six boards, with a same-board non-draw control serving index 5 (23-F98), and on a middlegame board where a piece was
hung, `e6e5` was index 0 of 42 (23-F100).

**(b) The generation order is FULLY SPECIFIED**, so index 0 is predictable without running the engine: piece types in the
order P→N→B→R→Q→K with absent types skipped, pawns by class single→double→capture, and ascending from-square within a
class — 13/13 on a holdout, and a predictor written from that rule before the engine was asked named the move 5 of 5 on
constructed roots and exactly on one self-play-reached root (23-F103/F106/F105/F108).

**(c) The gate is HISTORY.** The same position sent as a bare FEN searches 44,888 nodes instead (23-F109). The bail is
**exactly**: the base returns 0 nodes **iff the POSITION BEING SEARCHED has occurred at least three times in the sent
move list** — a monotone threshold at 2/3/4/5/6 occurrences, confirmed in both directions across nine boards, extended to
a dense middlegame position (~30 legal moves) and to the end of a long move list, and refined to follow the searched
position rather than the game's root (23-F125/F132/F133/F141/F143/F140). The halfmove path cuts in at 100 (23-F126), and
a four-command confound test separates this zero from the two automatic-draw zeros (23-F134).

**(d) What the score is worth: little.** `cp 0` is right about the rule and wrong about the position (see §1's
calibration).

**(e) What is NOT established.** Which of the three candidate code paths produces index 0 — an early return before root
ordering, a draw test after generation publishing the unmodified list head, or a root-ordering step skipped when the draw
short-circuits. I do not rank them (23-F100).

## 3. THE COST, AND ITS CALIBRATION

**The cost, as one chain rather than three findings (23-F171–F178):**

**On a genuine threefold root where the side to move has mate in one, the base reports `cp 0` and plays the generator's
FIRST move, forfeiting the mate — measured end to end, not inferred.** The six boards: `f2f3` is both the `go perft 1`

(back-rank and a direct queen mate). The PC plays the mating move in every case.

So the chain reads: **the bail returns the head of the generated list (F98) → on these boards the head forfeits a mate in
one (F171/F172/F177) → and the head is confirmed to be what was played (F178).** That is the family's practical claim in
its strongest form, and it is the one to lead the cost section with.

Calibration: `cp 0` is defensible ONLY as "drawn if the draw is claimed" — so it is right about the RULE and wrong about
the POSITION (§1), and a tool showing it tells a user a game is drawn that is won in one. The repetition here is
deliberate: this is the section a reader is most likely to quote, and the calibration must travel with the number.

**THE GENERAL COST, not just the mate case: playing generator index 0 costs a median of roughly 600-1,000 cp across TWO
DISJOINT 12-position samples** (~625 and ~1,004; nine or ten of twelve at or above 300 cp in each), on middlegame

Breadth, stated honestly: the mate forfeit reproduces on 6 of 6 fixtures across TWO mate types; four other structures had
no mate in one and could not test it (F175), and all six share Black's king on g8/h8 with pawns intact.

The three rules that follow it, **copied from the page by script** rather than retyped:

1. **`nodes == 0` with an EMPTY `pv` means nothing was searched** — never show its score. The empty `pv` is the reliable
   signal; the node count is a symptom and the two auto-draw rules share it. **And do not infer a search depth from the
   `info depth` line count here: on a bailed root it is the engine's ceiling (10 at `go depth 10`, 64 under `go nodes`),
   not the depth of any search, because there was none (23-F185). The 64 is simply the DEFAULT depth — `int depth = 64`
   in `search.hpp:29`, passed through the clamp at `search.cpp:2219` — reported once per depth by a loop that never
   searched (23-F187).**
2. **`bestmove 0000` is never a move.** Treat it as an error, and do not rely on a diagnostic to explain it — on a
   stalemated root there is none (23-F159).
3. **Count the `info string` lines on every search.** They are the only signal that your position or your restriction was
   rejected, and a corrupted case is otherwise indistinguishable from a healthy one.

Verified across all three draw kinds at MultiPV 3 (23-F160): threefold, fifty-move and insufficient material each give
10 lines / 0 nodes / empty pv / a legal move, so the drawn-root row is not a threefold-only observation.
