## 3. THE COST, AND ITS CALIBRATION

NINTH LINE — THE COST, AS ONE CHAIN RATHER THAN THREE FINDINGS (23-F171..F178):

**On a genuine threefold root where the side to move has mate in one, the base reports `cp 0` and plays the generator's

survived its own earlier proof because that proof exercised the one checker that did exit nonzero. The general form:
**proving a gate on one of its members proves only that member.**

because its evidence cell cited no resolvable file — a bare commit SHA, a `sed` line range, or a source path the
resolver could not find.** That is the audit enforcing its own rule that every row names something a reader can open, and
it is worth knowing that the refusal was correct each time: a commit SHA is not evidence a reader can check without the
repository, and a resolver cannot verify what it cannot find. **An evidence rule that rejects the convenient form is the
rule earning its place.**
