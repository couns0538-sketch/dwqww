A THIRD OCCURRENCE REACHED BY A REAL CLOCK GAME — the defect DOES fire, and my first attempt at this was wrong
============================================================================================================
Your two constraints: reach the third occurrence by an actual clock-driven game, and compare both builds on the same
game reporting move, score, nodes and the halfmove counter.

ATTEMPT 1 (clockgame.py) — WRONG PREMISE, and I record it because the mistake is instructive. I stopped the
self-play when python-chess's `can_claim_threefold_repetition()` became True. That function also returns True when a
third occurrence **could be reached by a move**, so it fired at only TWO actual occurrences; both builds then searched
normally (base 546,905 nodes, PC 494,515, both cp 0) and I nearly published "the defect does not fire on a natural
game". The engine's own test needs THREE actual occurrences (file 3), so my stopping rule did not match it.

ATTEMPT 2 (clockgame2.py) — counts ACTUAL occurrences of (board, side, castling, ep) and plays on until the count
reaches 3. That is the engine's own condition.

THE GAME, entirely clock-driven (PC build plays itself at `go wtime 6000 btime 6000 winc 60 binc 60`, every move a
fresh `go`):

Third occurrence at **ply 31**, halfmove counter **11** (a repetition draw, nowhere near the fifty-move rule),

VERDICT: the drawn-root defect **does fire on a naturally reached repetition**, and it is worse than the constructed
case suggested in one respect — the two builds choose **DIFFERENT MOVES** (base e6e5 after searching nothing, PC b6b4
after 1.38 M nodes). So 23-F44/F57 stand, and 23-F57's claim extends to a game-arrived third occurrence.

Both scores are cp 0, which is right: the position is drawn by repetition. What differs is that the base answers
without searching at all and picks a move from no analysis, while the PC searches and picks a different one.

MY ERROR, and the rule it adds: I used a LIBRARY predicate (`can_claim_threefold_repetition`) as a proxy for the
ENGINE's condition, and the two are not the same — "claimable" (a third occurrence reachable now) versus "occurred
three times". That is instance 11 of the family: a proxy for a condition is not the condition, and it very nearly
made me publish a refutation of a correct finding.
