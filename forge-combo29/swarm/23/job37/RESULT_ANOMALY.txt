THE COUNTER-90 ANOMALY IS REAL AND REPRODUCIBLE: the BASE explodes near the horizon, the PC does not
=============================================================================================
My first sighting of this was a single run at counter 90 (base 10.4M nodes vs PC 930k). Three FRESH PROCESSES per
cell, both builds, depth 10 — every cell identical across its three runs, so this is engine behaviour, not noise:

| counter | base nodes (3/3) | PC nodes (3/3) | base score | PC score |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 88 | 97,384 | 178,232 | cp 361 | cp 271 |
| 89 | 501,621 | 306,279 | cp 364 | cp 279 |
| **90** | **10,445,791** | **930,573** | cp 271 | cp 231 |
| **91** | **16,370,887** | **7,251,835** | cp 0 | cp 0 |
| 92 | 1,353,048 | 1,459,778 | cp 0 | cp 0 |

WHAT IS REAL: **the base build explodes as the counter approaches 100, by up to 11x the PC's node count (counter 90:
10.4M vs 930k), peaking at 16.4M at counter 91.** Every value is identical across three fresh processes, so this is a
deterministic property of the engine, not measurement noise.

WHY IT IS A FINDING AND NOT AN ANOMALY NOW: an 11x node explosion at a specific counter near the fifty-move horizon is
a real cost on a real board — it is the same family as seat 21's depth-13 explosion (23-F74) and is invisible at a
clock (the position is answered long before depth 10 is reached in a fast game). It also means a harness sweeping
counters at fixed depth meets an 11x wall between counters 89 and 90 that has nothing to do with the position's value
(the score only changes from cp 364 to cp 271).

WHAT I DO NOT KNOW: the mechanism. I have not read the code path, and I am not going to guess in a finding line. The
honest statements are (a) it is reproducible to the node in three fresh processes, (b) it is the base more than the
PC, and (c) it happens in the two counters immediately below the fifty-move boundary.

CONTEXT FROM 23-C26/F80: those swept the same board and saw 90 -> cp 271, 91 -> cp 0 at depth 10 without recording node
counts, so the earlier findings are unaffected — this is about COST, not about the score curve, and the curve is
unchanged (cp 364 at 89, cp 271 at 90, cp 0 at 91).
