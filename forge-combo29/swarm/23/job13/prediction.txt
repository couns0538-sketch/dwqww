      gave 30 % each). KILLED outside that band.
  P2  the median gap for changed positions will be SMALLER than for unchanged ones, by at least a factor of 2.

  P4  the recalled fraction of changed positions at a 30 cp threshold will be >= 60 %. KILLED if < 40 %.
