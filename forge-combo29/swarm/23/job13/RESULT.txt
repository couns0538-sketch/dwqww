IS "THE SECOND-BEST MOVE CHANGES IDENTITY BETWEEN THE LAST TWO DEPTHS" A PRECISE SIGNATURE?
==========================================================================================
Hypothesis (coordinator's): the instability may not be about the size of the gap but about whether the runner-up
move's identity is stable between adjacent depths. If changed positions are exactly those where the second-best
identity changes, the signature is exact.

Method: for each of the 60 positions, D = the depth the 20000 budget actually reached. Ranked the same candidate
set at depth D-1 and at depth D by scoring each move ALONE (`go depth X searchmoves <move>`). Signature =
"the second-best move at D-1 is a different move from the second-best at D".
PC r15, PC20, Threads 1, Hash 16, fresh process per search.

| group | n | second-best identity STABLE | second-best identity CHANGED |
|---|---|---|---|
| move CHANGED between budgets | 17 | 14 | **3** |
| move UNCHANGED | 43 | 37 | **6** |

ANSWER: the signature is REFUTED as a detector, and it points the wrong way if anything:
  fires in 3 of 17 changed positions (recall 18 %)
  fires in 6 of 43 unchanged positions (false alarms)
  of the 9 positions where it fires, only 3 are real changes -> precision 33 %
  base rate 15 %; changed set 17.6 %, unchanged set 14.0 % — statistically indistinguishable

A RELATED OBSERVATION, same data: the BEST move's identity between D-1 and D also fails as a signature —
3 of 17 changed positions show a best-move change at those depths, but so do 3 of 43 unchanged ones.

WHY (suspicion, not proven): for a changed position the two candidates were the moves the budgets chose, so by
construction BOTH are near-best at D; which of them ranks second at D-1 is then close to arbitrary, and the
budget flip at 250000 happens several plies deeper still. The signature was measured at the 20000 budget's final
depth, not at the depth where the flip actually occurs, which is a mismatch I could not remove without knowing the
flip depth per position.

SO: 23-F27's margin result remains the honest state of the art — a real mechanism (median 12 cp vs 46 cp) and a
weak per-position classifier (precision 45-56 %). This job's narrower signature is worse than the gap, not better,
and is recorded so nobody spends an hour on it again.
