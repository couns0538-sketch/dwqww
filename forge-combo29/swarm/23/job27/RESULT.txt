DO THE PC20 SEARCH SWITCHES CHANGE ANY MOVE? — yes, on 3 of 20 positions, and the scores shift everywhere
=====================================================================================================
Nobody had asked whether the four PC20 search switches (CuckooRep, HistRework 2, TtPruneV2, CorrHistV2) change a
MOVE, as opposed to a node count. 20 fresh quiet positions, PC r15, Threads 1, Hash 16, OwnBook false, fresh process
per search, `go depth 14`:
  A = S14 only (14 tuning options)
  B = PC20 (S14 + the six — the owner's setting)
  C = S14 + ONLY CorrHistV2

| measure | result |
|---|---|
| B (PC20) differs from A on the MOVE | **3 of 20** |
| C (S14+CorrHist only) differs from A on the move | 6 of 20 |
| scores shifted between A and B even when the move matched | 17 of 20 (e.g. cp 34 -> 44, cp -38 -> -27, cp 101 -> 105) |

The three move changes:
| position | A (S14) | B (PC20) | C (S14+CorrHist) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `rn1qkbnr/ppp1p1pp/3p1p2/8/1P6/B6N/...` | e7e5 (cp -457) | **d6d5** (cp -461) | d6d5 (cp -463) |
| `1nbqkbnr/1ppppppp/r7/p7/Q7/2P2PP1/...` | a6a8 (cp -24) | **c7c6** (cp -42) | h7h5 (cp -25) |
| `5rr1/2p2kb1/1q5n/p4p1p/P5pP/...` | g2g3 (cp -1885) | **d2b3** (cp -1928) | d2b3 (cp -1878) |

Prediction scored: P1 held (>= 1 of 20); P2 held (3 <= 8); P4 held (every combination reproduced identically when
re-run as part of the C column). P3 was NOT confirmable as stated — C agreed with B on 2 of the 3 A-vs-B differences
and disagreed on the third (it chose h7h5 where B chose c7c6), so "CorrHistV2 is the switch that matters" is only
two-thirds true on this sample.

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE OWNER: the PC20 switches are NOT cosmetic on move choice — 15 % of these quiet positions get a
different move under PC20 than under S14 alone — and they shift the evaluation on most positions even where the move
agrees. So a PC20 result and an S14 result are different measurements, in the same way the clock and node-budget
paths are: comparable only with the settings stated.

LIMIT: 20 positions, one depth (14), one build; "which individual switch does it" is only partially resolved (C is a
one-switch test, not an ablation of all four).
