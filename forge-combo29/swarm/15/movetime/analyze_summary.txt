=== THE ANSWER TO THE CUTOFF QUESTION ===
  For a position with real search work, movetime is honoured at 99-100% of BOTH 1000 and 3000 ms.
  There is NO cutoff in this range: the mode does not change character between short and long
  slices. The only cells that stop early are ones where the DEPTH LADDER ends first (depth 64),
  which is a property of the position, not of the clock.
