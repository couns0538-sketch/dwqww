=== THE CLOCK DRAINS: median 105 ms used per move against a 60 ms increment ===
So each move costs the clock about +105-60 = 45 ms on average. Starting from 6000 ms that
would exhaust a clock after roughly 6000/45 = 133 moves of one side, IF the per-move cost
stayed at the median.

=== SO: WOULD A LONGER GAME FORFEIT? ===
The observed minimum clock was 174 ms after 160 plies (80 moves per side).
Drain is roughly linear at ~22 ms lost per ply in the heaviest game, and the cost does
NOT accelerate in these games, so a game long enough to forfeit would need roughly
174 ms / (45 ms per move of the draining side) ~= 4 more moves of that side, i.e. about
8 more plies. That is a NARROW margin, not a comfortable one.
