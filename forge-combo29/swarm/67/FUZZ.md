## Method note: what a hang is, and is not

`no bestmove within T seconds` is **not** a hang. An unrestricted `go` (`go infinite`, `go mate 0`, `go` with no recognisable limit, `go btime` alone) is required to run until `stop`, so silence is correct behaviour.

A hang here is a **protocol-liveness failure**: after the case, the harness sends `stop` then `isready`, and the engine must answer `readyok` within 10 s. Every case in this report answered. The cases that merely ran without a limit are reported separately as *unbounded* (8 of 141).

This distinction was not academic: the first version of this harness used `no bestmove within T` and produced 7 false hangs, all of which turned out to be unrestricted searches that answered `stop` normally.

## Where the FEN refusal appears in the reply stream

The `info string invalid FEN` diagnostic is emitted **synchronously on the `position` command**, in both harness patterns. It never appears in the `go` output. This was measured by timestamping every reply line and attributing it to the most recent preceding write, not by reading text order.

| pattern | FEN cases | with refusal | silent | refusal owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `P_isready` | 31 | 22 | 9 | position |
| `P_direct` | 39 | 27 | 12 | position |

`P_isready` = `position` → `isready` → `go`; `P_direct` = `position` → `go`. The pattern does not move the diagnostic. A harness that sends `isready` after `position` will read the refusal **before** its `readyok`, so a `readyok`-only read loop consumes it and discards it.
