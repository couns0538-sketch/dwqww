3. IN DETAIL: WHAT WAS WRONG
==============================================================================

Step 1 — KRK forced mates require quiet waiting/zugzwang moves invisible to qsearch:
  In KRK endings, forcing checkmate requires quiet non-checking rook waiting moves
  and king-approach moves that restrict the lone enemy king into zugzwang. When a
  reduced search branch reaches `depth == 0` with the bare king not in check,
  `Worker::qsearch` searches only captures/promotions (none exist) and stands pat
  on static evaluation (~+430..+550 cp), completely blind to a forced mate 2-4
  plies deeper.

Step 2 — Forward pruning and LMR drop or horizon-truncate the unique mating move:
  In `Worker::search`, as soon as move 1 (typically a pointless rook check, since
  `MovePicker` gives +12000 to direct checks) returns ~+450 cp, `best_score >
  -MateInMaxPly` becomes true. For all subsequent quiet non-checking moves:
    * Late-Move Pruning (`move_count >= lmp_threshold`) sets `skip_quiets = true`,
      skipping the quiet mating move completely without searching it.
    * Futility / History / SEE pruning skip quiet moves when `lmr_depth` is small.
    * Late-Move Reductions (`LMR`) reduce late quiet moves by 2..5 plies (and also
      reduce the bare king's own 2nd..8th defensive flights), pushing the mate past
      the `depth == 0` horizon so the reduced search returns ~+470 cp <= alpha and
      never triggers a full-depth re-search.
    * Pre-loop Razoring, Reverse Futility Pruning, and Null-Move Pruning cut off
      non-PV bare-king nodes directly with centipawn scores (null-move pruning is
      especially invalid in KRK because KRK mates rely on zugzwang).

Step 3 — `Worker::search` stores full-depth TT bounds over pruned/reduced results:
  At the end of `Worker::search` (`src/search.cpp:1496-1503`), even though legal
  quiet moves were skipped by `skip_quiets` or reduced by LMR, the node stores
  `BoundUpper` (on fail-low) or `BoundExact` (on PV) at its full nominal `depth`.
  For example, on `3K4/5k2/6R1/8/8/8/8 w - - 0 1` (`PV#4`, mate in 6 via quiet
  `3.Ke8`), Worker 1 skipped quiet moves at `depth = 6` and stored:
    `BEFORE(a1a1, score=476, depth=2, Upper) -> AFTER(a1a1, score=488, depth=6, Upper)`

Step 4 — One ply up, the defending bare king converts that into a false `BoundLower`:
  At the parent Black-to-move node (`PV#3` and `PV#1`), Black sees that its king
  flight was only punished by `+488` / `+477 cp` (`-488` / `-477` for Black) rather
  than `-Mate`. That beats Black's null-window beta (e.g. `-520` or `-486`), so
  Black stores `BoundLower` (`score = -488` at `depth = 7` on `PV#3`, and
  `score = -477` at `depth = 10` on `PV#1`) into `shared_.tt`—falsely certifying in
  the shared hash table that Black cannot be checkmated within 10 plies!

Step 5 — Worker 0 hits Worker 1's `BoundLower` and cuts off the mating line:
  On a fresh `Threads 1` search, deterministic move ordering happens to fail high
  at the root and enter a full-window PV re-search (`!pv_node == false`, bypassing
  TT cutoffs) that discovers the mate. With `Threads 2`, Worker 1 writes these
  centipawn `BoundLower` / `BoundUpper` / `BoundExact` entries into `shared_.tt`
  ahead of or alongside Worker 0. When Worker 0 probes `PV#1` on a null-window /
  LMR search (`!pv_node == true`, `req_d = 3`, window `[-487, -486]`), it hits
  Worker 1's `depth = 10, score = -477, BoundLower` entry (`-477 >= -486`), takes
  an immediate TT cutoff without searching `1.Rc6`, and finishes depth 14 with a
  centipawn score (`cp 487`, `g6d6`) instead of `mate 8` (`g6c6`).
