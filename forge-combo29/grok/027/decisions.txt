Seat 027, idea 27, id S44, option IdeaS44.

The plain engine already returns eval from reverse futility when depth is at most
RfpDepth (8) and eval is at least RfpMargin (72) centipawns per ply above beta,
with 40 centipawns back when the position is improving. That drop does not look
at the hash move. S44 is not that drop.

IdeaS44 adds a second static return of eval, and only when all of these hold:
the option is on, the node is already off the PV and out of check (the same
outer guard as reverse futility), depth is at most IdeaS44_Depth, eval is at
least IdeaS44_Margin centipawns per ply above beta and is not a mate score, the
hash move exists and is a non-capture non-promotion pseudo-legal move, that
move's quiet history is at least IdeaS44_History, and no cheaper enemy piece
attacks a dearer piece of ours. A capture hash move does not qualify.

Defaults: Depth 8, Margin 48, History 6000. Depth 8 stays inside today's modest
band. Margin 48 is below today's 72, so the new drop can fire where reverse
futility does not. History 6000 is a high band against a per-table cap of 16384.
The flag is first in the conjunction, so with the option false the search does
not call the history or the attack scan.

SEED for the random positions is 2701. A game is kept only when it is still
going after ply 80. Positions are the FENs at plies 8, 15, 22, and so on.
python-chess claims threefold repetition and the 50-move rule as the end.
