The match is below -20 Elo and the whole +/- interval is below 0
(-100.06 +/- 11.96, so about -112 to -88). I looked once.

I did not find a sign error, a mate score being shrunk, or an illegal move.
Mate scores are returned unchanged. The shrink multiplies a non-mate score by
768/1024, so the sign stays and the value moves toward 0. The PGN endings that
say threefold repetition, the fifty-move rule, or insufficient mating material
are legal chess endings, not engine faults.

What the loss matches: the probe is real search, so its nodes count. At a fixed
20000 nodes the on engine spends part of the budget on that probe and searches
the position less deeply. The shrink also fires when two quiet plies leave the
static eval almost flat, including quiet positions that are still wins. The
idea text says an unconditional probe should be expected to lose strength, and
that many real wins are quiet before a pawn break. I did not change the code
after this look.
