IdeaS27, seat 076, written before any measurement of the patched engine.

What the switch does: with IdeaS27 false the quiet-history sum is unchanged. With IdeaS27 true the engine records the threat-role quiet history (that statistic is otherwise off under S14) and, when scoring a quiet move, compares the previous-move continuation with that threat-role value. If they have opposite signs and both sit outside IdeaS27_DeadZone (default 128), the score is the from-to history alone. Otherwise the usual sum is used and the threat term is included.
