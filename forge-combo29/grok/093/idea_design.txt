Idea 43, IdeaK4. The plain engine does not do this. Reverse futility at search.cpp uses
margin = RfpMargin * depth - RfpImproving * improving
and returns eval when eval - margin >= beta.

Decision: one check, IdeaK4, default false. One spin, IdeaK4_Shrink, default 24, range 0..200.
When the check is on, compare the opponent's static evals at the node one ply ago and the node
three plies ago (both are the opponent to move, both stored from that side's point of view).
If both exist and the later one is lower, the opponent worsened over two plies and the margin
is reduced by IdeaK4_Shrink. Sentinels before the root store ValueNone, so the comparison
cannot fire before both samples exist. The check is read first, so false leaves the margin
expression unchanged.

24 cp stays positive at depth 1 even when the improving bonus of 40 is also on
(72 - 40 - 24 = 8). A larger shrink would let the margin go negative at depth 1.
