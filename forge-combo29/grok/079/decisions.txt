The plain engine does not do this. HindsightDepth and root node-share time
management are different mechanisms. EXISTS no.

What it does, when IdeaB10 is true:
After a node finishes, each searched move is scored by nodes spent divided by
the depth it was actually given. If the best rate is at least IdeaB10_Ratio
percent of the next rate, and the move searched at least IdeaB10_MinNodes
nodes, that move is remembered. A later visit of the same position gives that
move back withheld late-move reduction, one ply plus one more ply for every
IdeaB10_Step percent of extra dominance. The plies given back along a path
from the root never exceed IdeaB10_Cap. A shallower mark is kept in preference
to a deeper one in the same table slot, so the first move's raw node share is
not the signal and a deep one-off node does not erase a mark the search will
meet again.

Defaults, chosen before the on measurement: Ratio 150, MinNodes 32, Step 100, Cap 3.
Off (the default, and an explicit false) does not read the table and does not
change a reduction.
