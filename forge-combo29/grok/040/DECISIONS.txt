Idea 40, IdeaK1, hash probcut. The plain engine does not do this.
EXISTS: no. The full-depth transposition cutoff in search.cpp returns a
hash score only when the stored depth is at least the current depth.
Capture probcut (ProbcutMargin) searches a capture; it is not a hash cutoff.

What the option does, and why:
- Check IdeaK1, default false. While false the new block is not entered.
- Spin IdeaK1_Margin, default 120, range 0..1000. 120 centipawns is a
  clear fail-high (more than a pawn) without waiting for the capture
  probcut margin of 180. A smaller margin would cut on noisy shallow
  bounds; a larger one would rarely fire at depth 12.
- Off the principal line only, and not inside a singular search (a move
  is excluded there). Root nodes are PV nodes, so they are untouched.
- The stored search must be at least depth-4 deep, and the node itself
  at least depth 4, so the formula stays non-negative.
- BoundLower or BoundExact. An exact score is a lower bound of itself.
  BoundUpper is not.
- Mate scores are not converted into beta+margin.
- The half-move clock must be below 90, the same guard as the existing
  transposition cutoff.
- The returned value is beta+margin, not the stored score, and nothing
  new is written into the hash. The stored entry stays as it was.
