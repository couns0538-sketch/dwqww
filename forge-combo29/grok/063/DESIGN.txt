Idea 13, S15, option IdeaS15. The plain engine does not do this. Search reductions
in search.cpp have no rook-versus-passer test.

What it does, and why these numbers:
A rook ending here means no knight, bishop, or queen on the board, and at least
one rook on each side. IdeaS15_MinPieces defaults to 8 because the note asks
for eight or more pieces (kings count). IdeaS15_MaxRookMoves defaults to 1:
the rook is already on the passer file (0 moves) or this move steps onto that
file (1 move). A pawn is passed when the engine's passed-pawn mask is empty of
enemy pawns and no own pawn stands in front on the file. Its next step costs
1 if the square in front is empty and 2 if that square is occupied. The rook
is in time when its remaining moves are at most that cost. The reduction is
set to 0, which searches the move at full depth. The extension is left alone.

The test is not called when IdeaS15 is false, so the reduction is the one the
plain engine computed.
