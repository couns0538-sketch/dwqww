IdeaS13. The plain engine does not keep a history of the quiet piece move that stood before a pawn break. EXISTS: no.

What was added, and why:
A butterfly table, break_history, indexed by side, from and to. It is not added to the evaluation and it is not mixed into quiet_history, so history pruning and the existing late-move history term stay on today's formula.
When a pawn move raises the score (it becomes best_move, which happens only after the score goes above alpha), look back IdeaS13_Lag plies. Credit a non-capturing, non-promoting move of a knight, bishop, rook, queen or king if the pawn key after that move is still the pawn key at the break. A pawn move in between changes the key, so the earlier piece move is not credited. Lag is at most 4 because the root frame sits four sentinel frames above the start of the stack.
The table is used in two places only: added to the quiet ordering score of non-pawn moves, and, when positive, subtracted from that move's late-move reduction by min(IdeaS13_Shave, history / IdeaS13_Divisor) plies.
Chosen numbers: Lag 4, Bonus 160, Divisor 48, Shave 1. One credit is 160. 160/48 is 3, and the cap cuts that to 1 ply, so the next time that from-to is tried it is ordered 160 higher and reduced one ply less.
The check IdeaS13 defaults false. While it is false the search does not read or write the table and does not change a reduction. clear() still zeroes the table.
