IdeaS31 is not already the engine. NullThreatVerify (search.cpp, the block that starts "Depth >= 14 always verifies") only re-searches a threatened cutoff below depth 14, and it re-searches every cutoff at depth 14 or more. It defaults to false. S31 asks for the opposite gate at every depth: immediate accept when no cheaper enemy piece attacks a dearer piece of ours and the static margin is comfortable, and a reduced re-search with null moves disabled otherwise.

The new check option is IdeaS31, default false. The spin is IdeaS31_Margin, default 80, range 0 to 2000. 80 is just under a pawn (SEE pawn value 100), chosen before any patched search. The margin is static eval minus beta, not the transposition-adjusted eval.

With IdeaS31 false the old block runs. The extra null-entry test is (!IdeaS31 || !idea_s31_blocked_), which is true for the whole default search, so the attack scan and the new re-search are not used. With IdeaS31 true, a threatened cutoff or a margin below 80 is verified at depth minus the usual null reduction, and idea_s31_blocked_ refuses null moves in that whole tree. A quiet cutoff at or above the margin returns immediately, including at depth 14 or more.
