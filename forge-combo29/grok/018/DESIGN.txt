A friendly pawn is a candidate when three things are true at the node, before the move.
Its passed-pawn span still contains at least one enemy pawn (a stopper). Every stopper
is attacked by a friendly pawn (a lever). The square one step in front of the pawn has
at least as many friendly attackers as enemy attackers, counting every piece. Zero and
zero passes: nobody attacks that square.

The move that is boosted is one of two kinds.
A quiet pawn push (not a capture and not a promotion, including a double step) of a
candidate, after which that pawn's span is empty.
A capture, including en passant and a promoting capture, of a pawn that is the only
stopper of some candidate, and whose static exchange is at least 0.

When IdeaS16 is on, that move's picker score gains IdeaS16_OrderBonus and its late-move
reduction loses IdeaS16_ReductionCut plies. The static evaluation is unchanged.
When IdeaS16 is off, both sites are skipped and the score and the reduction stay as they
are today.

OrderBonus default 4000. Quiet history starts near 0 and a direct check already gets
12000, so 4000 lifts the lever above ordinary quiets and leaves most checks first.
A pawn capture is scored near 1000 and a minor capture near 3000, so 4000 lifts a
pawn-capture lever above minor captures and below a rook capture.
ReductionCut default 1. One ply less is a milder reduction, not a full-depth search.
The spins are IdeaS16_OrderBonus (0..30000) and IdeaS16_ReductionCut (0..4).
