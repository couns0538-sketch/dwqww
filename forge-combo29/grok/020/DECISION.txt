IdeaS22, seat 020. The plain engine does not already do this. ProbCut is in
src/search.cpp and already refuses to run while the side to move is in check.
The new option refuses the whole ProbCut attempt (the shallow depth-4 proof and
the qsearch filter in front of it) on the ply after a capture, and on the ply
after a check has been answered. The margin line is unchanged.

IdeaS22 is a check, default false. IdeaS22_Plies is a spin, default 1, range
0 to 8. A capture, or a parent node that was in check, sets the counter to
IdeaS22_Plies. Any other node decrements it. The refusal is the counter being
above zero. When IdeaS22 is false the counter is not read, and the ProbCut
condition is the old one.

The in-check node itself is not the cooldown ply: ProbCut is already off there.
The following ply, where the side to move is no longer in check, is the ply
the idea names. A capture on that ply refreshes the counter instead of stacking.
