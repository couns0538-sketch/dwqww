What was added, behind option IdeaS5 (check, default false):
A node with remaining depth at least IdeaS5_MinDepth (default 4), and the side not in
check, is a nothing-to-play node when a one-ply search of legal captures and checks
does not beat the quiet evaluation by more than IdeaS5_Margin centipawns (default 40).
The quiet evaluation and the probe both use Evaluator::evaluate_uncached, the engine's
hand-crafted eval. That call does not write the hash and is not counted as a search node.
A capture whose static exchange already wins more than the margin skips the probe: there
is something to play. On a nothing-to-play node, a quiet move of a knight, bishop, rook,
queen or king that raises that eval is ordered IdeaS5_Order points earlier (default 80000,
above the check bonus of 12000 and ordinary history) and its late-move reduction loses
IdeaS5_Less plies (default 1). Pawn moves, including pawn breaks, stay in the list and
are not removed. With IdeaS5 false the probe is not called and no score or reduction changes.

Spins, all defaulting to the values above: IdeaS5_Margin, IdeaS5_Less, IdeaS5_MinDepth, IdeaS5_Order.
