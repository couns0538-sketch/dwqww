wrong if that sum is outside 8500000..11500000
test-match Elo, IdeaA1 true vs IdeaA1 false, 1106 games, 20000 nodes: +6
wrong if the Elo point estimate is outside -40..+50

Why: under S14, LmrHistoryDivisor is 5643, so the integer term
history/5643 is 0 for every quiet history in [-5642,5642]. IdeaA1
keeps that divisor and scores the same term in 1/1024 of a ply, then
rounds half toward zero. A quiet history whose absolute value is above
2821 then changes the reduction by one ply: good quiets one ply less
reduced, bad quiets one ply more reduced. The tree should change, and
the node sum should rise, because the extra ply on good moves expands
more than the saved ply on bad moves. At 20000 nodes that is a small
Elo effect. +6 is the center of a wide band.
