Idea 48 is IdeaK9: the null-move reduction also grows with (eval - beta) / a divisor.

EXISTS: forge-combo29/src/search.cpp:1701
The plain engine already does this. The reduction is

    tune::NullMoveBase + depth / tune::NullMoveDepthDivisor
        + std::min((eval - beta) / tune::NullMoveEvalDivisor, 3)

NullMoveEvalDivisor is already a UCI spin (default 200, min 50, max 1000),
printed from tune::parameters() in uci.cpp. NullMoveBase and
NullMoveDepthDivisor are already spins too. S14 sets NullMoveBase to 5 and
leaves the divisor at 200.

The only literal left in that formula is the cap 3. The idea's number, the
divisor, is already an option, so this seat does not add another switch.
The time goes to STEP 4 on another seat's patch.

Decision time is the date in stamps.txt on the STEP 2 line.
