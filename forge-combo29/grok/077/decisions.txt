Idea S44, option IdeaS44, seat 077. The plain engine does not do this.
Reverse futility at search.cpp returns eval when the gap is about 72 centipawns
times depth. It does not look at the transposition move, its history, or a
cheaper attacker. EXISTS does not apply.

What was added, only when IdeaS44 is true:
a static return of eval, off the PV, out of check, no excluded move, depth 1
to IdeaS44_Depth, eval at least IdeaS44_Margin above beta, the transposition
move present and quiet, its quiet-history sum at least IdeaS44_History, and
cheaper_enemy_attacks_dearer false. A capture transposition move does not
qualify. The option is tested first, so the history read and the attack scan
do not run when it is off.

Defaults, and why:
IdeaS44_Margin 40. Smaller than reverse futility at depth 2 and deeper
(72*depth, or 40 less when improving), so the drop can fire where today's
prune does not. At depth 1 while improving, reverse futility is already
tighter (32) and this drop adds nothing.
IdeaS44_Depth 5. Modest. Reverse futility already goes to 8, and a wrong
drop past that is expensive.
IdeaS44_History 5000. quiet_history is a sum of 16-bit tables. Under S14 one
cutoff bonus is at most 2212, and four of those on one table land near 7200.
5000 is a very high band on the sum, reachable after repeated cutoffs.

Seed for the random positions is 77*100+1 = 7701.
