The plain engine already does IdeaK3. HindsightDepth (default false) is that step.
After a reduced move, the child adds the two static evaluations (parent eval plus the
corrected eval of the side now to move). That sum is how far the mover's eval fell.
A fall of one pawn (SeePieceValue[Pawn], 100) searches one ply less. A fall of 0 or
less, after the parent withheld 3 or more plies, searches one ply more. The hash is
left alone when it is already deep enough. The comment and the step are at
search.cpp:1604 and search.cpp:1611 in the base tree.

The on/off switch and those two numbers were not both options. HindsightDepth is the
switch. 3 and 100 were literals. IdeaK3 is a second switch for the same step, default
false. IdeaK3_Withheld defaults to 3 and IdeaK3_Margin defaults to 100. While IdeaK3
is false the step still uses the literals, so HindsightDepth on keeps today's search.
While IdeaK3 is true the same step uses the spins. At the defaults that is the same
arithmetic, and the O depth-12 rows match exe B with HindsightDepth true.

I did not add a different depth rule. The idea is already in the engine.
