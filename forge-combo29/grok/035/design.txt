Idea 35, id A4, option IdeaA4 (check, default false).
The plain engine does not fold capture history into SEE pruning.
EXISTS: no.

The two capture SEE thresholds today:
1. Main search, captures only (quiet checks in the same branch stay on the old threshold):
   -SeeNoisyMultiplier * depth
2. Quiescence, captures only:
   -QsSeeMargin

When IdeaA4 is true, both thresholds move by
clamp(capture_history / IdeaA4_Divisor, -cap, +cap)
and the main-search cap is IdeaA4_Clamp * depth. Positive history lowers the
threshold, so a capture that has caused cutoffs is kept through a worse exchange.
Negative history raises it. Defaults: IdeaA4_Divisor 16, IdeaA4_Clamp 64.
When IdeaA4 is false the adjustment function returns 0 before it reads either
number, and see_ge is called with today's threshold.

Chosen because a history of a few hundred points then moves the quiescence
margin (40) and the depth-1 capture margin (150) without replacing the depth term.
