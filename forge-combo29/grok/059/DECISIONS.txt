The plain engine already does S23. AspCappedReopen (default false) is a UCI
check. On a root fail it calls capped_fail_low / capped_fail_high. A mate
score, or a score at or past 640 centipawns, opens that side to the win or
loss bound. Any other fail places the bound 13 centipawns past the score
that failed, and does not move the other bound except for the midpoint
assignment that was already there. Those two numbers were constexpr, not
options. The patch makes them spins:
  AspCappedReopen_Margin default 13, min 1, max 1000
  AspCappedReopen_Decisive default 640, min 1, max 10000
Min 1 keeps the failed score strictly inside the new window. A margin of 0
would make the re-search stop at once, which is not today's behaviour.
The check stays named AspCappedReopen. Adding a second check named IdeaS23
would be a different switch from the one the engine already has.

Switch off in the proofs sends AspCappedReopen=false after S14. The spins
are left at their defaults, so the on-path is today's on-path.

R positions, for a later match: python-chess, random.Random(5901). Each

with none of its positions kept. A game that reaches ply 80 without ending
contributes the positions at plies 8, 15, 22, 29, 36, 43, 50, 57, 64, 71
and 78. Stop at 300 positions.
