IdeaS34, seat 056. Front-loaded steps of the cutoff-count term.

The engine already has one late step: search.cpp adds one ply of reduction when
(ss+1)->cutoff_count is above LmrCutoffThreshold (default 3). That counter is
the next ply's fail-highs, accumulated across the moves already searched at
this node. It is not the front-loaded schedule in IDEAS.md S34.

What the option does, only when IdeaS34 is true:
- 0 up to IdeaS34_Couple (default 2) child cutoffs: subtract IdeaS34_EaseZero
  (default 2) from the reduction.
- from Couple up to IdeaS34_Full (default 4): subtract IdeaS34_EaseMid (default 1).
- at Full and after: no ease. The old +1 past LmrCutoffThreshold is not applied.
- a beta cutoff whose move is not the transposition move adds IdeaS34_NoTt
  (default 2) to cutoff_count. A transposition-move cutoff still adds 1.
  A missing transposition move counts as "no transposition move".

Count 1 stays in the EaseZero band because it is not yet a couple. The three
spin boundaries are the numbers the idea names (two plies, one ply, a couple, four).

The re-search that restores the full omitted depth when the shallow score is
above alpha is already in search() (full_depth_needed when score > alpha and
the reduced depth is shorter). It is left as it is, on and off, so the off
path stays today's path. "Test this schedule alone" is why the option replaces
only the cutoff-count term.

When IdeaS34 is false the reduction line is the old comparison and every
cutoff still increments by one. The new spins are not read.
