IdeaK11. The cutoff move's history bonus is scaled by how far the score beat beta.

EXISTS: no. search.cpp still gives a quiet cutoff one extra ply of the depth formula when the margin is above 100 centipawns. That is a step, not a scale by the margin. Captures never get even that step. The option leaves both of those paths untouched while it is false.

When IdeaK11 is true, the bonus that path would have used is multiplied by (margin + IdeaK11_Base) / IdeaK11_Divisor.
margin = score - beta, clamped to 0..IdeaK11_Cap.
A mate score on either side of the cutoff uses IdeaK11_Cap as the margin, so a mate distance cannot inflate the bonus.
The product is clamped to +/- HistoryBonusMax.
Defaults: Base 64, Divisor 160, Cap 320. A 0 cp margin keeps 64/160 of today's bonus. A 96 cp margin keeps 1.0. A 320 cp margin keeps 384/160.
The quiet path still adds the old +1 depth when the margin is above 100, and that result is what gets scaled. The capture path still starts from the unbumped depth.
The scaled bonus is passed into the existing history update. Fail-low and PV-only updates are unchanged.
Option IdeaK11 is a check, default false. The three numbers are spins IdeaK11_Base, IdeaK11_Divisor, IdeaK11_Cap.
