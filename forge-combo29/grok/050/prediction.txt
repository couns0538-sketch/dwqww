IdeaK11. Cutoff history bonus scaled by how far the score beat beta.
O depth-12 sum, switch on: 10400000
wrong if that sum is below 9600000 or above 13000000
test-match Elo, switch on versus switch off: 8
wrong if that Elo is below -25 or above 40
why: a bare cutoff (margin 0) keeps 64/160 of today's bonus and a 96 cp margin keeps 1.0, so the usual narrow cutoff is down-weighted and fixed-depth trees get larger. The match is the same ordering change at 20000 nodes, a small plus if a wide cutoff deserves more history.
