+// Exactness & no-overflow invariant:
+//   - a_i in [0, QA=255] and |w2_i| <= MaxW2=127 (checked by loader), so p_i = a_i * w2_i

+//   - _mm512_dpwssd_epi32 (VPDPWSSD, non-saturating) computes acc32_k += p_{2k}*a_{2k} + p_{2k+1}*a_{2k+1}.
+//   - For hidden <= MaxHidden=1024, even after folding sum0+sum2+sum1+sum3 across both perspectives,
+//     each of the 16 int32 lanes sums at most 2 * 2 * (1024 / 32) = 128 terms of magnitude <= 255^2 * 127
+//     = 8,258,175, giving |lane| <= 1,057,046,400 < 2^30 < INT32_MAX (792,784,800 for hidden=768).

+    const __m512i total32 = _mm512_add_epi32(sum0, sum1);
