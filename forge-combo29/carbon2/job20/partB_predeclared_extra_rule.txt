Pre-Declared Extra Rule for Job 20 Part B (Committed Before Generating or Inspecting Part A's 7,670 Rows)
=========================================================================================================

Rule name:
  R(12) — k = 12 member of the R(k) family (directly representable by UCI spin SoftStartGuard = 12)

Exact predicate (evaluated after iteration d finishes with n_d < N, where N = 2,000 and 4N = 8,000,
with n_0 = 0):
  Do NOT start iteration d + 1 when:
    n_d + 12 * (n_d - n_{d-1}) > 4 * N

A-priori algebraic rationale (derived before running Part A):
  Since iteration d finished below N = 2,000, n_d <= 1,999 and n_{d-1} >= 0:
    - For k in {1, 2, 3}: n_d + k * (n_d - n_{d-1}) <= (k + 1) * 1,999 <= 7,996 < 8,000, so R(1), R(2), R(3)
      algebraically never fire.
    - At a typical alpha-beta effective branching factor b ~ 2.2, delta_d = n_d - n_{d-1} ~ 0.55 * n_d.
      Then R(4) requires 3.2 * n_d > 8,000 (impossible for n_d < 2,000 unless b is huge), R(6) requires
      4.3 * n_d > 8,000 (n_d > 1,860), and R(8) requires 5.4 * n_d > 8,000 (n_d > 1,481).
    - Choosing k = 12 gives (1 + 12 * 0.55) * n_d = 7.6 * n_d > 8,000, guarding iterations once n_d > ~1,053
      at normal branching while leaving earlier iterations (n_j <= ~600, where 7.6 * 600 = 4,560 < 8,000)
      untouched, and mapping directly to SoftStartGuard = 12 in Part C if needed.
