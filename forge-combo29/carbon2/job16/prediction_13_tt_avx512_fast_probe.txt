4. Why every node stays the same:
   - `ctz(occ_m & (key_eq << 3)) >> 2` yields the lowest slot index `s in {0..3}` where
     `entry.key32 == key32 && (entry.gen_bound & 3U) != 0`, identical to the `0..3` loop.
   - When `hit_m == 0 && occ_m != 0x8888U`, `ctz(occ_m ^ 0x8888U) >> 2` yields the lowest
     empty slot index `s in {0..3}`, identical to `if (!entry.occupied()) return ...`.
   - When `occ_m == 0x8888U`, `replace_full_cluster_slot` evaluates slots `0..3` with the
     identical `depth - 4 * ((32U + generation - entry.generation()) & 31U)` formula and strict
     `<` tie-break; generation refresh, `sample_gen_counts_`, and `store_owned_fast` admission
     rules are bit-for-bit identical; public `TranspositionTable::probe`/`store` are unchanged.
