Part B: proving the wrapper's setoption lines arrive.

fastchess's -log engine=true records the traffic between fastchess and the
engine PROCESS. The wrapper writes its own setoption lines straight into the
engine's stdin, so those lines never pass through fastchess and cannot appear in
fastchess's log. The log therefore proves only that fastchess sent Threads/Hash/
OwnBook -- it cannot prove anything about the wrapper.

What does prove it: the same wrapper, run as the engine command, produces a
different bench 13 node count than the bare exe.

  bare exe, no options        bench 13 -> 862329   (the known off number)
  wrapper_B_12.sh (Mult 12)   bench 13 -> 748790
  wrapper_B_48.sh (Mult 48)   bench 13 -> 597400
  wrapper_B_24_400.sh         bench 13 -> 758728

So the options written by the wrapper reach the search. Commands in
logs/step1-B-known.txt show the multi-option case reaching the documented
690187 as well.
