Recovering the opening FENs for M1 and M2 (their games.pgn was deleted before the
per-game parser was fixed).

fastchess draws openings deterministically from -srand: the same seed yields the
same round -> opening map, independent of the node limit.  Two things were checked
before the recovered FENs were written into the M1 and M2 games.txt files.

1. Method validated against a match whose real PGN was kept (M4, seed 7306).
   A 600-node re-run with -srand 7306 produced 750 rounds; every round's opening
   FEN was compared with the FEN fastchess actually recorded in M4's surviving
   PGN (via M4's games.txt, copied before its pgn was deleted):
       rounds compared 750   mismatches 0

2. M1 spot-check: seed 7303's round 1 opening is
       rn1qk1nr/1ppbbppp/p2p4/3Pp3/2P1P3/5N1P/PP2BPP1/RNBQK2R b KQkq - 0 8
   which is the same FEN already written into M1's games.txt from M1's own
   full.txt (round 1 there had no FEN, but round 1's pairing is unambiguous).
   Seed 7304 likewise reproduced 750/750 rounds for M2.

So M1's and M2's opening_fen field comes from the seed, not from their own log.
The round, colour and result fields come from their own logs.  If the opening
draw and the game record ever disagreed, the results would be unaffected: the
results are read from each game's own "Finished game ... {reason}" line.
