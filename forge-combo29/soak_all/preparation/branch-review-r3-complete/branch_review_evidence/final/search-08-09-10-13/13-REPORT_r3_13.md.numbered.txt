    1 # QuietExtinction
    2 
    3 `QuietExtinction` is one UCI check option. It defaults to false. With every option left at its default, including this one, the engine is unchanged.
    4 
    5 ## Where the idea comes from
    6 
    7 The behaviour was specified for this task. Nothing in the search change was copied from another engine: not the gap, not the reduced margin, and not the control flow.
    8 
    9 A quiet move that has already been searched, and still sits far below alpha, is a sign that more ordinary quiets in that node are unlikely to raise alpha. The first such miss halves the futility allowance for later ordinary quiets. A second such miss stops the ordinary quiet tail. Captures, promotions, killers, and the counter-move stay in the list.
   10 
   11 ## What it does
   12 
   13 The option is read only at a non-root node that is not in check, not a mate search, not the bare-king mate-fix path, and not within the fifty-move horizon used by the other pruners (`halfmove_clock + depth < 100`). It also stays off when neither side has a knight, bishop, rook, or queen. That is the pure king-and-pawn case, including bare kings.
   14 
   15 A searched quiet counts as a wide miss when its score is at least 176 centipawns below alpha and the best score so far is not a mate-sized loss (`best_score > -MateInMaxPly`). While the best score is still a mate-sized loss, the miss is ignored and no quiet is removed.
   16 
   17 - After one wide miss, a later quiet that is not a killer and not the counter-move must beat a futility allowance of half `(FutilityBase + FutilityMultiplier * reduced_depth)`. The depth cap stays `FutilityDepth`. Checks, captures, and promotions are not removed by this test.
   18 - After a second wide miss, the move picker stops returning the rest of the ordinary quiet list. Good captures, bad captures, promotions, killers, and the counter-move are still returned. The quiet that just missed has already been searched.
   19 
   20 Root moves are never dropped by this rule, so a finished search still returns a move from the legal root list.

   38 ## Sanitizer
   39 
   40 `tests_r3_13/quiet_extinction.cpp` was compiled with `-O3 -fsanitize=address,undefined` and `-fno-sanitize-recover=address,undefined`, the same portable search flags, Hash 16, and one thread. It ran 200 searches to depth 10 with the option on: 197 positions reached by playing 1 to 23 legal moves from the start position, plus `4k3/8/8/3p4/3P4/8/8/4K3 w - - 0 1`, `4k3/8/8/8/8/8/8/4QK2 b - - 0 1`, and the start position. Each search had to finish at depth 10. A position that is not an immediate draw had to search at least one node. The returned move had to be legal and `make_move` had to accept it. A terminal position must return no move.
   41
