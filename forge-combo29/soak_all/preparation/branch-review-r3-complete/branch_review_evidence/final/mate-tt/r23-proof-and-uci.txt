     1	# r3-23 SoundMatePruning
     2	
     3	## Where the idea comes from
     4	
     5	Audit finding 4 on `audit/lock-source` at `302a08a272fbe8e8c4463731f3b9cd72d72d3f48`, in `forge-combo29/AUDIT_lock_source.md`. The position and the depth-8 / depth-9 search are the ones in `forge-combo29/tests_audit/proof_pruning_mating_and_check.cpp` on that commit. The finding is that move-loop pruning in `Worker::search` still runs on a principal-variation node, while in check, and when alpha is already a mate score. A skipped move can hide a faster mate, and the node can still be stored as an exact transposition score.
     6	
     7	The option, the guard, and this note were written for this tree. No search constants or tables were taken from another engine. The proof keeps that position.
     8	
     9	## What changed
    10	
    11	`SoundMatePruning` is a UCI check option, printed by `uci`, default `false`. `setoption name SoundMatePruning value true` or `false` sets it. Any other value is rejected and the flag stays put. Changing the flag clears the search state, so a later search does not keep transposition scores or histories built under the other setting.
    12	
    13	The move-loop block is unchanged when the option is off: not the root, not a mate-search, half-move horizon under 100, a score already better than a fastest loss, the side has non-pawn material, and bare-king mode is off. When the option is on, that block is also skipped if the node is a PV node (`beta - alpha > 1`), the side to move is in check, or `is_mate_score(alpha)`.
    14	
    15	## Default pins
    16	
    17	Threads 1, Hash 16, OwnBook false, every other option left at default. Printed before the edit, and again on the final binary:
    18	
    19	- `bench 11`: 312866
    20	- `bench 13`: 862329
    21	- `go perft 5` from the start position: 4865609

    25	## Proof position
    26	
    27	`kbK5/pp6/1P6/7p/8/7P/7B/1R6 w - - 0 1`
    28	
    29	The audit text calls `1.Ra1` mate in 3 and gives `1...a6 2.Rxa6 bxa6 3.Kxb8#`. That king capture is illegal: the white king would move from c8 to b8, next to the black king on a8. A full-width legal-move search finds no forced mate in 6 plies or fewer. The shortest forced mate is 7 plies, score `32000 - 7 = 31993`, which UCI prints as `mate 4`. One such line is `h2c7 h5h4 c7b8 a7a6 b1a1 a6a5 a1a5`. Replaying it is checkmate. The same distance count puts `h2b8`, `h2c7`, and `b1a1` on that minimum, and longer mates on other moves (`b1h1` and `b1b2` in 9 plies, `h2g1` in 11).
    30	
    31	`SoundMatePruning=false`, fresh search, Hash 16, one thread:
    32	
    33	- depth 8: `h2b8`, score 31991 (`mate 5`), 890 nodes, PV `h2b8 h5h4 b1b4 a7a6 b4h4 a6a5 b8d6 a5a4`. Replaying that PV does not checkmate.
    34	- depth 9: `h2b8`, score 31991, PV through `h4a4`. That longer line does checkmate (9 plies, `mate 5`).
    35	
    36	`SoundMatePruning=true`:
    37	
    38	- depth 8 and depth 9, exclusive table and a depth-8 shared table: `h2b8`, score 31993, PV `h2b8 h5h4 b1a1 a7a6 a1a6 b7a6 b6b7`. Replaying the PV is checkmate, and `32000 - 7 = 31993`.
    39	- A fresh UCI `go depth 8` prints `score mate 4`, 3088 nodes, the same PV, `bestmove h2b8`.
    40	- Turning the option off again and searching depth 8 returns the original `mate 5` line and 890 nodes. `bench 11`, `bench 13`, and `perft 5` in that same process are the three pins above.
    41	- The proof program passed again under address and undefined-behavior sanitizers.
    42	- The clang binary, option left off, matches the g++ depth-8 line (`mate 5`, 890 nodes). With the option on it matches the g++ mate (`mate 4`, 3088 nodes, the same PV).
    43	
    44	## What was not verified
    45	
    46	Playing strength was not measured. This position was not searched with more than one thread.
