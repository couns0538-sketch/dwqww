   1 # r3-21: mate scores from the hash table
   2 
   3 ## Where the idea comes from
   4 
   5 Finding 6 of the source audit on `audit/lock-source` at commit `302a08a272fbe8e8c4463731f3b9cd72d72d3f48`, in `forge-combo29/AUDIT_lock_source.md`. The failing case is `forge-combo29/tests_audit/proof_mate_score_ply_underflow.cpp` on that commit. That branch was read only. Nothing here was taken from another engine. The repair is the check that finding describes: if putting ply back onto a stored mate score leaves the mate band, do not use the score.
   6 
   7 ## What was wrong
   8 
   9 `MateInMaxPly` is `MateValue - MaxPly` (`32000 - 128 = 31872`). `is_mate_score` is true only at or outside that line. `score_to_tt` stores a mate as `score + ply` (or `score - ply` for a loss). `score_from_tt` used to reverse that with no further check.
  10 
  11 A stored mate of `31873` (`MateInMaxPly + 1`, mate distance 127) read at ply 2 became `31871`. That is below `MateInMaxPly`, so the rest of the search treated it as a centipawn score: it was not shifted again when stored by the parent, and the root printed `score cp 31871`. The loss side is the same mistake in the other direction (`-31872 + ply` steps inside the band).
  12

  14

  26 ## The change
  27 
  28 `score_from_tt` in `forge-combo29/src/search.cpp` still subtracts ply from a winning mate and adds ply to a losing mate. If that result is inside the centipawn range, it returns `ValueNone`. Callers already treat `ValueNone` as "no usable score", and they still keep the stored move and static eval. A mate that remains at or outside `MateInMaxPly` is unchanged. No UCI option. The default search does not hit this edge at the pinned depths, so the three node counts stayed the same and the fix is always on.
  29 
  30 ## Test
  31 
  32 `forge-combo29/tests_r3_21/proof_mate_score_ply_underflow.cpp` keeps the audit position, the seed `MateInMaxPly + 1`, and the depth-2 search. It fails if the root comes back as `31871` or as any mate or other huge score. On the fixed engine that search returns `score=0 is_mate_score=0 best=g3g4` (the proof stores eval 0 next to the fake mate; once the mate is ignored, quiescence stands on that eval). Depth 1 is still `cp -48`, because it never reaches the seeded node.
