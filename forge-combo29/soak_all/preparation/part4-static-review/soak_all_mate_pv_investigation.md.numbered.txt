   36 ## Independent mating assessment
   37 
   38 The scratch query includes `tests_t12/krk_mate_test.cpp` unchanged, renames its original main, and invokes only its exact KRK retrograde class and board operations. It does not run that test's repeated engine searches. The oracle algorithm is independent of alpha-beta search but shares the engine's attack/board primitives; it is not an external tablebase implementation.
   39 
   40 - Exact root DTM is **15 plies**, equivalent to mate in 8 moves. The optimal root move is `b3e3`.
   41 - The chosen `b3f3` forces mate in **17 plies**, equivalent to mate in 9 moves.
   42 - All 15 published PV moves are legal.
   43 - Its endpoint is `8/8/8/8/8/5R2/1k1K4/8 b - - 15 8`. Black has three legal moves and is not in check.
   44 - The endpoint's exact remaining DTM is **6 plies**, allowing mate within 21 total plies along the displayed prefix followed by optimal continuation.
   45 - UCI `mate 17` is measured in moves. Here it corresponds to a reported mating bound of 33 plies, not 17 plies.
   46 
   47 The oracle therefore does not establish an impossible mate bound or a nonmating chosen move. The reported distance is loose compared with exact DTM. An incomplete PV is not, by itself, proof that the score is false.

   49 ## Code mechanism and scratch trace
   50 
   51 The initial investigation did **not** instrument the exact return instruction. Its preliminary source-based assessment is preserved separately in `mate_pv_evidence/preliminary-analysis.md`. The authorized follow-up instrumented only a scratch copy of the merged `search.cpp` with two logging statements, one at the qsearch stand-pat early return and one immediately before its final return. Production source, binaries, and search decisions were unchanged. The original lock binary's instructions were not instrumented.
   52 
   53 One bounded NNUE-off run of that scratch executable reproduced all deterministic stdout exactly: after removing only engine-reported `time` and `nps` fields, its complete stdout matched the uninstrumented merged run (`diff` exit 0). It again searched 25568 nodes, returned `mate 17`, and emitted the identical 15-ply PV. Exit was 0; stderr contained only the intentionally added trace records.
   54 
   55 The record at the published PV endpoint is:
   56 
   57 ```text
   58 TRACE_QPV_FINAL root_depth=13 ply=15 alpha=-31967 beta=-31965 score=-31967 pv_length=0 tt_score=-31967 tt_raw=-31982 tt_bound=1 tt_depth=9 fen=8/8/8/8/8/5R2/1k1K4/8 b - - 15 8
   59 ```
   60 
   61 `tt_bound=1` is `BoundUpper`. The cached mate-valued TT score replaces qsearch's stand-pat evaluation. At this quiet Black-to-move endpoint, qsearch returns that score with an empty child PV. Its parents copy only their searched prefix, while the numerical mate score is propagated separately. The cached position-relative score `-31982` becomes `-31967` after the ply-15 adjustment, yielding root score `31967` and UCI mate distance `(32000 - 31967 + 1) / 2 = 17`.
   62 
   63 This confirms the final-return mechanism in the scratch reproduction. It does not audit the earlier creation of every TT bound or prove all mate reports correct. The independent oracle is the evidence that this particular reported bound does not overclaim.

   77 ## Recommendation and limits
   78 
   79 Do not suppress mate scores because the displayed PV is shorter than the reported mate distance. No targeted correctness fix, including a default-off switch, is justified by this reproduction. Reconstructing a complete continuation would be a separate display feature; blindly following TT moves can introduce cycles or misleading continuations.
   80 
   81 A real bounded fix would require evidence of an impossible mate bound, a move that fails to force mate within the claimed bound, or a traced unsound bound operation. None was demonstrated here. These checks do not establish correctness for other positions, settings, or thread schedules. No strength or speed conclusion is drawn.
