UCI and independent worker state
HistRework is a 0–3 spin option. PC20 uses 2. S14 leaves the option at 0. Each worker allocates both new history tables and the per-ply maps regardless of the setting and clears them on a full reset. Level 2 enables the reads and writes below. Allocation and clearing therefore do not distinguish the two levels.

Quiet-history reads: keyed table and ordering
The main-search node computes a map before the picker. Quiescence computes it for check evasions. A map records attacks by enemy pawns for our pawns, knights and bishops, also knights and bishops for our rooks, and also rooks for our queens. King entries stay empty. Sliding attacks use current occupancy. The twin entry indexes side, attack flags on from and to for that piece class, and the two squares. At level 2, 3/4 of the butterfly term uses the twin, 1/4 the original, with C++ integer truncation. The code adds blend-minus-plain to the pre-existing score, preserving the continuation and checking bonuses. Both normal quiets and quiet evasions can receive this term. With 0 the extra term is skipped.

Quiet-history writes and other readers
The winning quiet receives the history bonus while earlier tried quiets other than the winner receive maluses. A quiet PV best move that does not cut receives one quarter of the depth bonus. The twin uses the existing saturating history update. The resulting value feeds the static history-prune test and the history adjustment to late-move reductions as well as move ordering. Thus the history-only arm changes more than ordering.

Joint correction reads and writes
The key combines the pawn structure and non-pawn piece counts by side and type, masking to the existing correction-table size. Level 2 reads 75% of that entry into the combined correction, which is divided by CorrectionGrain (256) before altering the evaluation. Level 0 skips it. At an eligible search end a depth-weighted, clamped evaluation residual supplies the update step. The joint table applies its own self-damping update at every enabled level. Static evaluation in main search and quiescence, and the optional hindsight calculation, use corrected_eval. The code can therefore change evaluations used in cutoffs or ordering downstream. The CorrHistV2 continuation correction is a separate option, and the split keeps S14’s inherited NonPawnCorrection active throughout.

The two overrides added in the patch use -1 to follow HistRework and 0..3 to choose their own levels. The quiet override selects all seven quiet-side option accesses: the picker flag, the standalone history reader, the blend weight, the cutoff update, the qsearch map, the main-search map, and the quiet PV update. The correction override selects the joint-table read/weight and the joint-table update. The original HistRework parser and default stay in place.
