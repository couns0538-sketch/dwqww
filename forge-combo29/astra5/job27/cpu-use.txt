The Step 0 pin searches used separate fresh processes and ran before A0. A0 ran one thread on a single CPU. Each K27 and C27 game used two fresh UCI processes, one per side, with one search active at a time. The controls ran one game at a time.
The three match arms run consecutively within each block on identical openings. At most four games are active together on this five-CPU host. Each game searches one side at a time in a one-thread engine; the games share the available CPUs without assigning A and B separate cores. Exact CPU affinity and interval boundary times appear in matches/run.json.
