For counts n_i at two-game pair scores 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, put x_i=i/4.
N=sum n_i, p=sum(n_i*x_i)/N, V=sum(n_i*(x_i-p)^2)/N, SE=sqrt(V/N).
The score interval uses q=1.959963984540054, lower=p−q*SE, upper=p+q*SE.
Map score to logistic Elo L(p)=-400 log10(1/p−1).
Fastchess reports the estimate E=L(p) and margin H=[L(upper)−L(lower)]/2.
The primary displayed endpoints are E−H,E+H formed before rounding. The direct
transforms L(lower),L(upper), also recorded, can be asymmetric around E.
The runner's normalized Elo (nElo) and LOS use different statistics.
Use only fully completed colour-reversed pairs, denote cut matches incomplete,
and do not give a reading for them. Identical K8 pair scores of 1 give
zero empirical pair-score variance and a degenerate [0,0] Elo interval.
