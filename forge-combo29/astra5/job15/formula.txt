For counts n_i at two-game pair scores 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5, 2, put x_i=i/4.
N=sum n_i, p=sum(n_i*x_i)/N, V=sum(n_i*(x_i-p)^2)/N, SE=sqrt(V/N).
The score interval uses q=1.959963984540054, lower=p−q*SE, upper=p+q*SE.
Map score to logistic Elo L(p)=-400 log10(1/p−1).
Fastchess reports the estimate E=L(p) and margin H=[L(upper)−L(lower)]/2.
The primary displayed endpoints are E−H,E+H formed before rounding. The direct
transforms L(lower),L(upper), also recorded, can be asymmetric around E.
The runner's normalized Elo (nElo) and LOS use different statistics.
Use only fully completed colour-reversed pairs, denote cut matches incomplete,
and do not give a reading for them. Identical K8 pair scores of 1 give
zero empirical pair-score variance and a degenerate [0,0] Elo interval.

Job 15 clocks: individual C15 and T1b estimates use the audited completed
opening pairs. T1 and T1b contribute to POOLT only if the entire 300-pair
match finished. A cut or missing seed contributes zero opening pairs to
that pool; a partial individual estimate remains descriptive. POOLT uses
the sum of pentanomial counts of eligible seeds. With zero eligible pairs,
the pooled sample mean, variance, Elo and confidence interval cannot be
estimated. The rule's fallback label is not evidence of equal strength.
Decision: BETTER when its interval lies wholly above 0 AND T1b alone has
a positive Elo; WORSE when wholly below 0 AND T1b alone negative; LEVEL
otherwise. If C15 or T1b records more than five losses on time, append the
clock warning to every reading. Use raw A/B time-loss counts and disclose
complete-pair counts separately. C15 requires an upper endpoint below 0.
