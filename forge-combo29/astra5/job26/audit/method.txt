Part R / M independent readback

Use Python 3 and the chess distribution 1.11.2. For one stage, run
"python3 audit_games.py results STAGE astra5-general-openings24.epd OUT".
For the two controls after all three runs, run "python3 controls.py results OUT".
The stats_seed.py file alongside the reader is copied byte for byte from
Job 21 at 040834de294a31f81a3d6aa54a605af692a4fcb4. check_job21.py
accepts the local repository path and reproduces all three historical
reported intervals to eight decimal places. The separate clock-edge check
uses synthetic messages and tests the reader alone.

For each completed game, the audit checks the raw handshake and options
for the engine assigned each color, then reconstructs the full position
and go commands from the book and played moves. It reads the literal final
softnodes packet and bestmove and checks the search receipt. A hold search
counts once regardless of its number of hold lines. A charge is N // 1000
on every arm, including C26 A at NodeClock 2000. An equal charge spends
the entire previous clock then earns the 60-node-ms increment. A charge
greater than the clock loses before applying the move or adding increment.
The mover's previous clock stays in the end receipt in this case. Both
players' mean used includes all their attempted searches, including any
forfeiting attempt. End clocks are attributed to A/B using the actual
color assignment in that game. White/Black means are separately available.

After every legal applied move the independent replay tests checkmate,
stalemate, insufficient material, can_claim_threefold_repetition(),
can_claim_fifty_moves() and the 600-ply cap. The complete game receipt
must agree with the replay in outcome, cause, applied plies and both clocks.
The control check keys all records by opening and game identity. Its cost
comparison covers every final N and integer charge. The launch order is
read separately from the per-stage launch receipts.

Only complete color-reversed pairs enter the match statistics. The five
bins represent 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5 and 2 A points out of two. Divide each A
pair total by two to obtain one score fraction per opening. Job 21 uses
their population standard deviation divided by the square root of the
number of pairs. It multiplies that standard error by the 0.975 normal
quantile, maps mean fraction plus and minus that margin through
400 log10(p / (1 - p)), and uses half the difference as a symmetric
95 percent width about the logistic Elo of the mean fraction. A checkpoint
too small to have a finite transform is identified explicitly.

The match cutoff for starting new pairs was 19:11:02 UTC. The receipt
timestamp used to count pairs already complete by minute 45 is the
pair's controller complete time. Whole pairs subsequently finished before
the minute-50 playing stop also enter the final event totals.
