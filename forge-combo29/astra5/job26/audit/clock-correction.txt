The first independent K26-1 aggregation used the same counter key for
engine B and the Black pieces. The published correction separates player
and color totals and reruns the reader against all 20 raw games. The
previous B total 46,302 combined the correct player B total 22,042 with

The reader now verifies the balance for each game and player and for each
stage. With zero forfeits, final clock sum equals 6,000 times the number of
games, plus 60 times the number of searches, minus their integer charges.
A forfeiting attempt is excluded from the applied increments and charges.
The sum for both players must also equal the sum for both colors.
check_clocks.py independently recomputes all four numbers from the game
receipts without reusing the reader's aggregation. The underlying match
files, individual clock transitions, results, search charges, holds and
scores are unaffected.
