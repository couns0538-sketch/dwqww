Independent Part C readback

Use Python 3 with the chess distribution 1.11.2. For a completed stage run
"python3 audit_c25.py C1 /path/to/C1 /path/to/astra5-general-openings24.epd /tmp/C1-readback".
Replace C1 with C2 or C4 for the other stages. It uses games.pgn,
fastchess.log, argv.json, inputs.json and receipt.json from each raw stage.
The opening book can be regenerated with Job 24's committed generator.

The reader derives game counts and fastchess's exact ending names from
the started/finished lines of the primary log. It matches every PGN to
the two controller game IDs of its round by color and result, validates
the PGN header and movetext result, and legally replays every move from
the recorded FEN. It checks the terminal position against the literal
ending and scans every source log line for illegal moves, disconnections,
stalls, time forfeits and related process failures. The raw log line number
and text are saved for each match. Unknown termination reasons remain
visible and do not qualify as completed games by a rule of chess.

It checks both engines' named inputs, lookup hashes, the frozen PC20
settings, UCI command order, search limit, thread count and concurrency,
and independently rebuilds the physical EPD sequence from the seed by
std::mt19937_64 and fastchess's forward shuffle. Both colors in each round
must have the same FEN. The two UCI move lists are preserved in full.
Final PGN and log hashes are compared with the run receipt.

Wins, losses, draws, points and the five pentanomial bins are always from
kF's perspective. The bins count rounds scoring 0, 0.5, 1, 1.5 and
2 points. The kF share is (wins + draws/2) / finished games. The independently
computed W-L-D, point total and five bins must equal the final fastchess
summary. No engine or fastchess process is started by this audit.
