final/independent.json exposes arms by match (D1, D2, E, and D3/D4 if played).
Each arm has pairs, games, A_WLD (win-loss-draw), penta, elo_lower_upper (estimate,
lower, upper), reading, endings, plies, mean_plies, nodes_ratio (A/B mean spent),
and players A/B. Each player has searches, mean_n (stored n), n_total, mean_spent,
spent_total, depth_total, depth_reported, mean_depth (reported depths), mean_depth_zero
(0 for searches without a printed completed depth), hardcap, hardcap_share (proportion
of searches for soft sides, null for fixed nodes), no_score, go. E A spends 4000
per search; its n is last finished nodes. Top-level pooled_2000 carries all, 1-40,
41-80, 81+ with searches, n_total, mean_n. Top-level common_complete_blocks is an ID
list (blocks 1..9 main, 10..18 optional); complete_by_phase contains lists for main
and optional; blocks_complete_by_minute40 is the count across counted blocks.
final/thresholds.tsv supplies counted score crossings from engine A or B's own perspective.
Only complete common blocks enter the report stats. Stage summaries include uncounted
games separately. A positive mate counts for a side, and cp at or above E counts.
