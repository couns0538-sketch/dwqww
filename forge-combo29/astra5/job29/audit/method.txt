Independent recount. Python 3 and python-chess 1.11.2. Execute:
  python audit29.py PATH_TO_MATCHES L27.epd OUT
The archive has blockNNN/arm/receipt.json and schedule-end.json at its root.
A portable run reads the published per-game, search, opening, process and
option-profile tables, verifies each manifest, replays moves and terminal
rules, and regenerates all individual scores, threshold rows and pooled bands.
Supply --live ORIGINAL_GAME_DIRECTORIES to also verify the complete UCI wire
for every game: all 22 options in order, both readiness handshakes and quit,
exact opening FEN and every move sent before the search, exact go, last scored
info and last completed-depth nodes selected from all printed info, engine
footer and bestmove. Every completed game has two fresh process identities.
Legal rule onset is checked after every move. A ceiling of 600 plies is drawn.
Claims are python-chess can_claim_threefold_repetition and can_claim_fifty_moves.

The opening pair is the independent statistical unit. A receives 0, 0.5, 1,
1.5 or 2 total game points in one swapped-color pair. Pairs contribute that
total divided by two to stats_seed.py, identical to Job 28. The 95% error is
the normal 0.975 quantile times the population standard deviation divided by
the square root of the pair count, transformed symmetrically in Elo as in
Jobs 26-28. Positive lower bound gives A BETTER, negative upper gives B BETTER,
and an interval touching or containing zero gives LEVEL.

For each threshold and side, take only the first ply with that side's positive
mate or cp>=threshold in the final scored info. A negative mate describes the
other side's advantage and earns no crossing. The terminal bestmove is ply P,
so the number of plies after a first hit at ply j is P-j. The categories W/D/L
are from the crossing side. We use the number of printed depths for mean_depth
and also provide an all-search mean treating absent depth as zero. For the
plain side of E, n is the completed-depth count and mean_spent is the nominal
4,000-node budget. plain29.py separately sums the counters actually printed:
  python plain29.py OUT/final/independent.json PATH_TO_MATCHES L27.epd OUT/final
The output also gives the actual-to-soft budget ratio and the count and legal
move context of early ends, with their exact terminal score and board FEN.

One block counts only when the two colors of each opening are complete in
all match arms for its phase. The last actual game determines minute 40;
the scheduler receipt timestamp is preserved as a second check. The optional
phase requires all 434 primary openings in all three matches before minute 30.
