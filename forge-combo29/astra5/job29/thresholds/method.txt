First score crossings
Scores come from the last scored info line of each search, from the mover's viewpoint. The independent reader parses the literal score and depth from info_line and compares both to the recorded numeric search fields. Missing scores, zero mate and negative mates do not meet any threshold; a positive mate meets all five thresholds, even when no previous centipawn score reached them. An on-threshold centipawn score qualifies. Each game contributes at most once to a given side and threshold, even if its score later drops below the threshold. Both sides can independently qualify in the same game.
Each per_game.tsv row identifies one match, side, game and threshold, even if that side never reached that threshold. Blank first_ply and related fields mean there was no crossing. The game result in the outcome column is from that side’s viewpoint. The ending is always the actual game result. Plies remaining = ending ply minus first qualifying search ply. A move that immediately ends the game leaves zero further plies. A blank mean indicates no qualifying games.
Only matches in fully complete common blocks of their corresponding phase count. The status file identifies the exact blocks and whether the schedule has ended. For each stage, inputs.tsv gives SHA256 of the complete search and game tables and their sealing receipt.
Independent match cross-check
The per-side search table checks all raw go commands, soft node footers, printed last scored info and best moves. Mean scored depth uses the searches that reported a score and depth; the scored/no-score denominators are shown. Mean n uses searches with a recorded n in the compact table. For soft searches this is the complete node spend. Plain go nodes 4000 spends 4000 under the job's convention. Its n column records the nodes at the last finished depth. If a plain search emits an additional literal softnodes-style footer, the reader validates it and counts it separately. A/B spend ratios use 4000 in the numerator for match E.
Pairs contain two color-reversed games. W/L/D is from A's viewpoint. Opening pair scores of 0 to 2 are divided by two for Elo. The 95% normal margin in score is z times the population standard deviation across opening pairs divided by the square root of the number of pairs, with z = 1.959963984540054. As in Jobs 26–28, the Elo interval is symmetric about the point Elo with half-width equal to half the difference between the two transformed score endpoints.
