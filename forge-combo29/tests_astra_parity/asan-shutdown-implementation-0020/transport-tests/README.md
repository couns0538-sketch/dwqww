# Private diagnostic shutdown transport

The private `../uci_process.hpp` copies the frozen soak transport. It adds diagnostic fields, two pure diagnostic helpers and `finish_diagnostic()`. The private shutdown wrapper now invokes the diagnostic path, and the constructor's startup exception carries the full aggregate trace and stderr. Original send/read/parser behavior, the successful constructor handshake, and the original `finish()` remain unchanged. No engine source or old helper changed.

Call `finish_diagnostic()` exactly once instead of sending a separate quit. It records the final result, so the destructor does not send another quit or repeat cleanup. Startup failure and ordinary destructor cleanup also use this five-second diagnostic path; they cannot count as a completed replay.

The normal diagnostic path allows100ms for a nonblocking quit write, then5000ms of natural no-signal observation after successful delivery, then250ms after SIGTERM and250ms after SIGKILL if needed. Its requested total envelope is5600ms. The signal calls occur only after the natural observer returns at its declared deadline unless the child has already exited. A reap and pipe EOF are separate observations. Failed poll/read events cannot authorize an early signal.

OS scheduling can delay any observer beyond a requested deadline. A quit write observed after100ms records an explicit error. The full5000ms grace is never shortened to hide that fault; cleanup observation is shortened instead, and a large observer delay can exceed the declared envelope. Actual times are retained rather than relabeled as meeting the requested bound. Root uses a430-second native cap,435-second controller cap and440-second logger bound.

New result fields are `diagnostic_window`, `natural_exit_observed_within_5s`, `stdout_eof_before_shutdown`, `stderr_eof_before_shutdown`, explicit write/grace/cleanup budgets, quit-write begin/completion, stdin-close, observed reap and EOF times, natural deadline, signal-attempt times and errno, and `unreaped_child_pid`. Times are steady-clock milliseconds relative to shutdown entry. Absent times are−1. Preexisting EOF has its own flag; its reported time is when the diagnostic observed that already-recorded state. A natural-exit flag means a delivered quit, normal exit code0, no driver-delivered signal and reap observed within5000ms of delivery; it does not claim EOF or absence of other errors.

The private quit write retains the exact `> stdin quit` transcript line and sends `quit\n`. Its deadline is checked before each nonblocking write, including retries after EINTR. Signal-attempt timestamps are recorded immediately before each `kill`; success and errno are separately retained. An unreaped process at the end retains its PID in the result and constitutes an explicit failure. The helper never performs an unbounded wait or an undocumented extra signal after its diagnostic cleanup deadline.

If the diagnostic method itself throws, the noexcept cleanup wrapper uses a last-resort allocation-free fallback. It closes stdin, allows another full5000ms without signals, then uses SIGKILL if needed with at most250ms reap observation. It explicitly sets `diagnostic_fallback_used=true`, `timed_out=true` and `natural_exit_observed_within_5s=false`; exit0 cannot make this a normal diagnostic result. This exceptional fallback can add5250ms after the diagnostic exception and is not the normal5600ms protocol. It cannot safely allocate or retain newly arriving pipe bytes, so its detailed trace is incomplete; the entire run is an explicit helper failure requiring review. Already captured startup trace/stderr remain preserved. The fallback never sends an early SIGKILL.

Validation consists of22 compile-time assertions and a syntax-only compiler invocation:10 assertions cover natural/TERM/KILL deadline offsets, including nanosecond precision and late-observer handling;12 cover natural-exit classification and its separation from pipe-EOF timeout. `STATIC-VALIDATION.txt` verifies unchanged original code after removing the documented additions and restoring only the private shutdown/startup exception wrappers. It also checks source ordering of the grace/cleanup calls and startup aggregate preservation. No binary, engine or process fixture was launched. Live behavior and syscall integration still require the parent's independent review and a future authorized diagnostic run. Prior reviewed artifacts are retained under `superseded/`.
