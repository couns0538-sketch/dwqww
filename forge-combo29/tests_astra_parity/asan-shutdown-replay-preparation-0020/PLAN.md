## Limits and unresolved facts

Command order and contents are retained. Original per-command timestamps, scheduler interleavings, host contention, memory addresses, allocator state and sanitizer teardown state are not. Original signal-send timestamps were not recorded;150/200ms are calculated stage boundaries, not measured signal times. The shutdown finding has no stack trace identifying a stalled step. Neither a successful nor failed replay alone establishes deadlock, engine crash, leak-check cost or sanitizer overhead as the cause. Exact repetition frequency remains unmeasured.

The concrete next step is to agree the authorized replay/control count and drift policy from Fable's answer, implement the small private transport/driver variant above, independently review it, then let root execute the bounded diagnostics.
