So the RET line with depth 0 is a SEPARATE visit from the NODE line with depth 1: the RET
comes first, from the DEPTH-1 ITERATION, where the root (depth 1) calls its only child with
depth 0. Its alpha/beta are the untouched -32001/32001, which is what a fresh call looks like,
not a node whose window was already narrowed by a move loop. The NODE depth 1 line is the
depth-2 iteration's visit of the same position.

The correct statement: at the depth-1 iteration the mating node is handed depth 0, so it goes
straight to qsearch and the quiet mating move Kf7 is never generated. That is the ordinary
behaviour of a depth-limited search reaching the horizon one ply above a mate, not a pruning
rule and not HindsightDepth. What makes this position special is that the depth-4 search still
reports mate 4 at depths 12 and 16: the deeper iterations keep choosing a different branch, so
the horizon visit at ply 1 is never replaced by a real search of Kf7 until depth 18.
