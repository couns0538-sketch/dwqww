An earlier floor-1 run cost about 5.4% on embedded G. The synthetic G baseline has fewer shallow starts per node, so I forecast a lower floor cost there. At a fixed node and margin, Base4 moves only original child-depth-zero cases above quiescence, and Base3 also moves original minus-one cases. The floor moves every shallow case above zero without changing already-positive depths. Total search nodes and root choices may move in either direction. Baseline flagged G positions tended to have longer searches, so I expect a somewhat higher move-change rate there without treating flags as the only cause.
