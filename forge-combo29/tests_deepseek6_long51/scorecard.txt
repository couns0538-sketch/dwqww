## The result that matters

At depth 12 the four settings compose SUB-multiplicatively on G: the bundle is +7.02 % ABOVE the product of its
singles (the savings overlap, so four together save less than the product predicts). At depth 14 the same four
compose SUPER-multiplicatively: the bundle is **-5.69 % BELOW** the product (four together save MORE than the
product predicts). The pair does the same, +0.65 % -> -3.00 %.

So "do the interactions grow or shrink with depth" has no single answer: **the sign of the interaction itself is
depth-dependent**, which means the product-of-singles reasoning that long 41 and 42 used is only valid at the
depth it was measured at — and gets the direction wrong one ply pair deeper.
