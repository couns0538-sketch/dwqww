JOB 6 B2(a): halfmove clock 99 with a mate in one on the board.
The engine must play the mate: a mate delivered on the 100th halfmove is a mate,
the fifty-move claim only becomes available when 100 halfmoves have passed without
a capture or pawn move AND the position is not mate.

These five cases are promotions, which also reset the halfmove clock, so they test
that the engine plays the mate and not the 50-move interaction itself. The 50-move
interaction is B2(b)/(c) below, which uses non-promotion positions.

FEN 3k4/1P6/4K3/8/8/8/8/8 w - - 99 1
  only mating move b7b8q  engine b7b8q  played=True  score mate 1
FEN 3k4/5P2/2K5/8/8/8/8/8 w - - 99 1
  only mating move f7f8q  engine f7f8q  played=True  score mate 1
FEN 3k4/1P6/4K3/8/8/8/8/8 w - - 99 1
  only mating move b7b8q  engine b7b8q  played=True  score mate 1
FEN 4k3/2P5/5K2/8/8/8/8/8 w - - 99 1
  only mating move c7c8q  engine c7c8q  played=True  score mate 1
FEN 1k6/3P4/K7/8/8/8/8/8 w - - 99 1
  only mating move d7d8q  engine d7d8q  played=True  score mate 1

RESULT: 5/5 played the only mating move at halfmove clock 99.
