## Limits (documented, not fixed)
- **Boundary walk budget.** `SyzygyRootExact` bit 2 tries at most 1000 moves per root. A boundary root it cannot decide keeps its possibly drawing or winning moves and reports the search score. No false claim, but the search may pick a worse one of the undecided moves. The reach depends on the clock. Rows of this purpose-built fixture left undecided, by root clock: 90-98: 50 of 2429; 80-89: 54 of 153; 70-79: 147 of 330; 60-69: 555 of 1086; 50-59: 71 of 244; 40-49: 35 of 111; 30-39: 7 of 48; below 30: 0 of 168. In all 919 of 4569. The independent solver also ran out of its 8 s per root on 812 of those 919, so they are hard for anything, and on them only the absence of a false claim is checked. On the 2821 rows the solver finished: 107 undecided, 34 kept moves worse, 4 engine moves worse at depth 8. How often such roots occur in games is not measured. A rejected two-phase variant at budget 2000 reached 32 undecided at 83 ms against 54 at 34 ms on the first 2500 rows; the budget stays low because the walk runs inside `go`.
- **Partial table sets at the boundary.** A walk that meets a missing table cannot decide its move: it stays undecided, as above, and may use the whole budget on retries. 15,495 boundary roots on folder `nochild`: 14,887 undecided, the others show what the full set shows, none false.
- **Store-side rule: not at a root, not in qsearch.** By reading (R3 finding 6): a root above the tables is stored without the raise, and one earlier occurrence is not a draw, so that entry can be read at a later clock of the same game at an inner node. A false cut would need a clock of at most 89, k + clock <= 100 and a mate line of the loser of at least 12 plies. No failing scenario was found: purpose-built rows (the root searched at clock 0, a position one reversible move before it searched next at clock c - 1): 4024 rows, depth 12, 2,979,697 TT cuts: 0 wrong at every site, against 2290 wrong in 200 rows without bit 1. Numbers for the frozen head and two-move rows in BENCH.md. Left as it is: no code without a failing test.
- **WDL-only move filter.** With a DTZ table missing the ranks cannot see the counter; a kept move can be a 50-move draw at this clock. Only the report is corrected. Full table sets are not affected.
- **Body damage in a table file** that still parses can give wrong values: file checksums are not verified, and verifying them is a reader change outside this job. 44 damaged-folder runs under ASan with heap copies: no crash, no out-of-range read. The rest of the set stays usable. Files that fail validation (truncated, junk, empty) are rejected at load and counted.
