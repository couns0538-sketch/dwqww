## Eval call map of the lock (answers the sub-track questions)
- Per node visit at most ONE static_evaluate: search :936 and qsearch :808, only on a TT miss or a TT eval of ValueNone; TT cutoffs (:921-925, :794-798), mate-distance cuts (:895-898) and in-check nodes (:930, :802-804) never evaluate. The net and the HCE run inside static_evaluate only (:599-621); ply >= MaxPly (:781, :894) is the only other call.
- The TT keeps the raw eval (TTEntry::eval int16, search.hpp:71) at :817 (qsearch stand-pat, only without a TT hit), :868 (qsearch end; ValueNone in check), :1023 (ProbCut), :1214 (node end; not for excluded searches). Every non-check position that reaches a store gets its raw eval; the corrected eval is recomputed on every visit (right: correction history moves; storing it would not be node-identical).
- Same-position re-entries: razoring qsearch on the same frame (:955) repeats is_draw, the probe, static_evaluate (TT miss) and corrected_eval; ProbCut child search after the child qsearch (:1016-1018) repeats is_draw and corrected_eval (eval comes back through the TT); singular verification (:1094-1098) reuses ss->static_eval (:932-933) but repeats is_draw and the probe; null-move verification (:990, depth >= 14) re-evaluates on a TT miss.
- Exits that store nothing: RFP (:960-963) and null-move cutoffs (:984-998) - their evals are lost and recomputed on every revisit (next iteration, PVS / LMR re-search, transposition). This, not TT replacement, is the main miss source for an eval cache (T05F-01).
- Eval hash keyed by the position key: node-identical (raw eval is a pure function of the position: no rule-50 state in eval.cpp, full-key pawn cache eval.cpp:397, integer RuntimePsqt, float accumulators exact because WeightScale = 4096 and sums stay < 2^24 units). Hit rate: never counted (missing) - step 0 of T05F-01.
