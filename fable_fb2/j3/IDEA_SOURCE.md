# IDEA_SOURCE — FB2-J3 (search improvements behind options)

All code is written for Forge on top of the lock's search (`src/search.cpp`); nothing was copied. Ideas:

| option | idea | source (idea only) | what Forge's lock already had |
|---|---|---|---|
| `CorrHistV2` | continuation correction history: the static evaluation is corrected by tables indexed by the previous move's (piece, to-square) and by the move before it, updated like the pawn correction from the search result | Stockfish's `continuationCorrectionHistory` (2024, several contributors); Caissa / Alexandria "continuation correction" | pawn-key and material-key correction histories (`corrected_eval`, `update_correction`) |
| `SingularV2` | triple extension when the TT move is far ahead of everything else and the path budget allows; double extensions also at PV nodes with a wider margin; multicut: when the reduced search without the TT move still fails high, several moves beat beta, so return that score; deeper negative extensions (-3 when the TT score is at/above beta at non-PV nodes, -2 at cut nodes) | Stockfish singular extension code (double / triple extensions, `singularBeta >= beta` multicut, negative extensions), Ethereal / Berserk variants | single/double extension (non-PV, margin 24, budget 6), `singular_beta >= beta` return, -2 / -1 negative extensions |
| `LmrTableV2` | a statistically fitted reduction: logistic regression of P(the reduced search raises alpha) on ln depth, ln moves, their product, history, improving, cut node, TT-PV, fitted separately for quiet and noisy moves on `bench 16` decisions (FORGE_LMR_STATS build); the reduction is the lock's log-log table shifted by `scale * (mean log-odds - log-odds(x))` with scale = 0.75 ply per standard deviation; the continuous history term replaces the clamp(history / divisor, -2, 2) | the general practice of history-dependent LMR (Stockfish, Ethereal), fitted here from Forge's own search statistics (own method: calibrated logistic model, `tools/search/fable_lmr_fit.py`) | log-log reduction table plus a clamped history term and +-1/+2 adjustments |
| `TtPruneV2` | (1) the TT move is never pruned by futility / history / SEE pruning; (2) internal iterative reduction also at cut nodes whose TT move carries an upper bound (it failed low before); (3) reverse futility margin lowered when the opponent's position got worse on their move; (4) `improving` also drives the quiet futility margin and the history-pruning threshold | Stockfish (IIR at cut nodes with a failing TT move, `opponentWorsening` in RFP, improving-dependent futility), Ethereal / Weiss (improving in history pruning) | improving in LMP, RFP, probcut and LMR; IIR only without a TT move |

Caveat recorded for the fit: the LMR statistics are observational (collected under the lock's own reduction policy), so
the fitted probabilities describe "how often the lock's reduced search raised alpha", not the counterfactual of a
different policy; the gate decides whether the redistribution helps.
