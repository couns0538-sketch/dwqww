## Incremental update (evaluation_extra.inc)

- Per replayed ply: the child's placement is rebuilt (redo), threat lists regenerated as in O6; pawn-pair lists are
  regenerated only when the ply's dirty pieces include a pawn (touches_pawns: move, capture of a pawn, promotion all
  list a pawn change), otherwise the parent's two lists are copied to the child's slot.
- King-key change of a perspective: refresh_full_cached recomputes that perspective from the cache and regenerates its
  pawn-pair list with the new flip into the child's slot (overwriting the copied list), so a king crossing the mirror
  axis is handled even when no pawn moved. The other perspective keeps the copy. Correct.
- Pawn move without key change: list regenerated with key[c].flip (unchanged key), multiset delta against the parent's
  list; pawn_counts returns to all-zero after each delta (checked: every feature's net count is consumed by the removal
  or the addition loop). Correct.
- Column buffers sized MaxThreatPairs + MaxPawnPairs + 4 (<= 4 piece changes per ply). MaxPawnPairs = 16 * 7 = 112
  (a pawn anchors at most 7 pairs). Correct.
- Default OFF: evaluation_threats.inc is no longer included; static_evaluate_v2 dispatches to static_evaluate_extra only
  when the active net has an extra block; a pawn-pair net is activated only with NNUEPawnPairs 1 and the header bit
  must agree both ways (fail-loud), NNUEKingBuckets 0 selects a one-bucket net. CI identity ALL OK (run 40486074459).
