## 1. Architectural Separation: Mandatory Integrity vs Experimental Quality Selection

A core design flaw in many ad-hoc NNUE filters is conflating **data corruption guards** with **heuristic quality filters**, and permanently mutating/discarding records before the model sees them. Forge's live `filterqc.py` overwrites rejected records with `score = 32002 (VALUE_NONE)` in `filtered.plain`, permanently destroying the original search score in the active training file.

### Tier 1 — Mandatory Data-Integrity Checks (Non-Negotiable, Always ON)
Every record entering any training cache or shard MUST pass:
1. **Structural & FEN validity:** Exactly 8 ranks, 8 files per rank, valid piece letters (`PNBRQKpnbrqk`), exactly 1 white king and 1 black king, `2 <= piece_count <= 32`, valid side-to-move (`w`/`b`).
2. **Score & Result validity:** `score != VALUE_NONE (32002)`, `|score| <= 32000` (fits `int16`, no mate sentinel leakage), `result in {-1, 0, +1}`, finite integer values.
3. **Score & Result perspective verification:** Both `score` and `result` strictly from **side-to-move (STM)** perspective. In Forge: `datagen_uci.py:_after_game` writes `res = result_white if wtm else -result_white`, and `run_selfgame` emits `info.score` from STM. Automated sanity invariant: across any shard of `>= 10k` records, `corr(score, result) > +0.50` and `mean(result) ~ 0` (catches inverted-perspective bugs immediately before GPU hours are burned!).
4. **In-check policy (`skip_check = true`):** Forge's `forge_train_v3.py` NEVER trains on in-check positions (`8.47%` of `filtered.plain`). Because `forge/r15dsg/src/uci.cpp:467` ALREADY computes `st.check = board.in_check()` every ply, skipping or tagging `in_check` at generation time (or via exact 64-byte `is_in_check_fast`, verified `0/25,000` mismatches at `102.5k pos/s` 1-thread) is a pure integrity + efficiency win with ZERO change to trained positions.
5. **Cache-key integrity (Target 149):** `forge_train.py` `_load_one` AND `_count_one` must hash `hce_dump.exe` (`size:mtime:sha256[:16]`) into the cache key (verified patch `patches/cache_key_t149.patch`).
6. **Whole-game split hygiene & leak-free validation:** Validation sets MUST be split by **whole independent games** (never random rows), generated by a **modern net** (not Day-1 `AL1 s198`), deduplicated against the training window, and tracked alongside a clean modern holdout (`eval_sets/final_eval_r0203_w14_w15.plain`, `1,555` whole games) plus external real-game benchmarks (SF19 M4).

### Tier 2 — Experimental Quality Selection & Sample Weighting (Reversible, Verified by Phase C Elo!)
Because raw complete games archive in Stockfish `.binpack` at **`2.00 bytes/position`** (`~4.0 GB` for `2.0B` positions!), **never destroy raw data at generation time**. Instead, store raw games compactly and test Tier-2 policies reversibly at shard-packaging or GPU-training time:

## 2. Mechanism Analysis of Core Data-Quality Problems in Forge

### 2.1 Sigmoid Gradient Saturation & Mop-Up Waste (`14.0%` of Current Data!)
- **Observed in Phase A (`audit_r0202.json`):** `14.015%` of all filter-kept positions (`2.12M`/round, `~280M` at Server Day 2B) have `|score| >= 800`, where:
  - Outcome correlation is already `0.989–0.991` (game is already decided).
  - Under Forge's WDL scaling (`K = 2.25`, `scale = 400`, `p = sigmoid(K * s / 400)`), the gradient factor `p*(1-p)` collapses from **`0.2427`** (`0_99 cp`) to **`0.00172`** (`800_1199 cp`, `141×` smaller) and **`0.00007`** (`1200_2000 cp`, **`3,466×` smaller**)!
- **Why NOT hard-drop 100% of `|score| >= 800`?** Hard-dropping ALL high-score positions removes the upper anchor of the sigmoid, allowing NNUE outputs to fold back or become non-monotonic in winning positions (a known failure mode in NNUE training).
- **Forge-Specific Solutions to Test in Phase C:**
  1. **Soft Sample Weighting (`EXP-W1: soft800`):** Keep ALL positions on GPU, apply `w = 0.50` for `800..1199` and `w = 0.25` for `>= 1200` (mean-normalized to `1.0`). Zero positions permanently lost; gradients reallocate to `< 800 cp` contested chess.
  2. **Deterministic Hash Downsampling (`EXP-F1`):** Keep `25%` of `800..1199` and `10%` of `>= 1200` using deterministic `blake2b(fen4) % 100` -> shrinks GPU/disk footprint by `~11%`, freeing budget to include **older/additional unique rounds** at equal retained-position count!
  3. **Generation-Time Adjudication Levers (Server Day):** Lowering `eval_limit` from `2000` to e.g. `1000–1200` (with 3–4 consecutive plies confirmation so tactical spikes don't false-adjudicate!) directly saves **`~10–14%` of CPU self-play cost per euro** by ending already-won mop-ups earlier! (Must be validated against endgame/winning-conversion accuracy!)

### 2.2 Tactical Instability & Horizon Blunders (`Q3` Ply-to-Ply Swings)
- **Primary literature:** Stockfish `nnue-data` uses `d6pv2` / qsearch stability checks; Bullet/Viridithas skip captures & checks.
- **Critical Forge nuance (`config.forge.toml:428`):** Forge empirically gained **`+15.4 +/- 5.9 Elo` (6,000 games)** from `keep_noisy = true`! Why? Because Forge's static eval is called inside search/pruning (RFP, futility, razoring) on positions where captures exist; training ONLY on quiet positions leaves capture-rich positions out-of-distribution for Forge's search heuristics.
- **Original Forge-Specific Alternative (Zero Extra Search Cost!):**
  - Instead of blindly dropping all `19.0%` noisy positions, exploit **1-record lookahead (`ply t` vs `ply t+1` in the same game)**: `swing(t) = |score(t) + score(t+1)|`.
  - Phase A (`audit_val_and_r0203_raw.json`) proved that controlling for score band, `swing >= 400` collapses score–result correlation by **`-34%` in `100_249 cp`** (`0.669 -> 0.438`) and **`-27%` in `0_99 cp`** (`0.259 -> 0.188`), and manual inspection of 20 real examples confirmed textbook tactical horizon blunders where `score_t` has the WRONG sign (`+128..+198 cp` while losing the game and flipping by `400–600 cp` on ply `t+1`)!
  - Crucially, `swing >= 200` in `|score| < 500` affects ONLY **`~0.16%`** of positions (precision surgical removal/downweighting, NOT throwing away `19%` of data or removing valid hard tactical positions whose score stays consistent on `ply t+1`!).
  - **Selective Rescoring Proposal (Server Day):** Instead of simply dropping the `~0.16%–0.8%` high-swing contested positions (`|score| < 500, swing >= 200`), **rescoring ONLY those ~0.5% positions at 8x deeper search (`16,000` softnodes)** costs ONLY **`+4%` total CPU** while turning the single noisiest, most tactically instructive positions into **ultra-high-value gold labels**!!

### 2.3 Contradictory Labels & Drawn-Game High-Score Conflicts (`Q2`)
- **Observed in Phase A:** Live `filterqc.py` drops ONLY `465`/round (`0.003%`) win/loss conflicts (`|score| >= 600` opposite win/loss) while leaving **`14,542`/round (`0.096%`)** drawn-game conflicts (`result == 0, |score| >= 600`), of which **`81.3%` are Endgame/TB**.
- **Nuance from 20 real examples:** Most inspected cases are **unconverted winning `K+Q vs K+N` / `K+R+2P vs K+R` endgames** where 2000-node Forge failed to convert (3-fold repetition, 50-move rule, or cursed Syzygy DTZ) — so `result = 0` is noisy while `score = +850` is right; a minority are true fortresses where `result = 0` is right and `score = +650` is optimistic!
- **Policy:** Do NOT assume all `res == 0, |score| >= 600` are bad; test both hard-drop (`EXP-F1`) and soft downweighting (`w = 0.35`, `weight_mode=soft_quality`), and on Server Day tag `end_reason` (`rule`/`drawadj`/`plylimit`/`stalemate`) inside `selfgame` so 50-move/rep draws can be distinguished from true fortresses + Syzygy 6-piece TB adjudication!

### 2.4 Exact Duplicates, Cross-Round Multiplicity & Sequential Correlation (`Q1`)
- **Known defect in live `filterqc.py`:** Uses Python's process-randomized `hash()` -> deduplicates ONLY within a single round (`3.33%`), NEVER across the 25-round training window (`378.9M` positions)!
- **Measured 25-round exact cross-round curve (`results/cross_round_dedup_r0178_r0202_m64.json`):** `3.145%` cumulative duplication (`~11.91M` wasted rows = `0.78` rounds, climbing `0.74%` -> `4.84%`, `87.4%` at `ply >= 81`). On 2M CC0 v2 (`cc0_shards_4k_v2`), exact dedup found `12.34%` dups (`60.30%` from `k=0/1` startpos deterministic replays, eliminated by `startpos k=3/4` -> `2.61%` dup, `0.0%` ply 0–15). Bucketed parallel vectorized dedup (`partition_fqs_buckets.py`, `results/partition_fqs_buckets_bench.json`) runs at **`766,285 rec/s`** (`5.56x` speedup, `0/9` diffs, `< 0.72 GiB RAM/worker` at 2.0B / 128 buckets -> `6.6 min` / `23 GiB` RAM on 32w!).
- **Target Averaging vs First-Occurrence Dedup vs Inverse-Sqrt Weighting:**
  - Keeping only the *first* occurrence of a duplicate throws away variance reduction from independent game outcomes (`result` is binary `-1/0/+1` with high variance!).
  - **Duplicate Target Averaging** (averaging `score` and `result` across all `k` occurrences of the same `fen4`, storing continuous `res_mean` or `score_mean` + weight `sqrt(k)` or `1.0`) gives a **strictly lower-variance WDL target** for recurring opening/endgame positions without duplicating gradient steps!
- **Sequential Correlation:** Consecutive plies in one game share `~95%` of pieces. Stockfish (`--random_fen_skipping`) and Viridithas (`random_skipping`) subsample plies per game (e.g., skipping 1–2 plies or keeping 50–70% of plies) so every retained training slot comes from a MORE diverse game! With `selfgame` generating `129.9` plies/game, skipping e.g. 25–35% of early/correlated plies trades slightly more CPU generation for lower correlation per GPU sample — critical to cost-optimize on Server Day when CPU is cheap (`€0.002/core-hr`) and GPU VRAM/hours are tighter!

### 2.5 Opening Diversity & Distribution Shift (`Q5`: Why Forge Blunders `2.45×` in Moves 01–10!)
- **Explosive Phase A Discovery (`m4_vs_train_shift.json`):**
  - Training (`r0202`, `8` uniform-random legal moves from startpos) vs Real Games (`M4 c100`, UHO book):
    - **`0_99 cp` (balanced/close chess):** **`0.739×`** (`-26%` under-represented in training!)
    - **`250_499 cp` (lopsided random openings):** **`1.766×`** (`+77%` OVER-represented!)
    - **`ob7` (29–32 pieces):** **`1.663×`** (`+66%` over-represented, YET mostly chaotic random positions!)
    - **`shield_broken` (Forge's #1 blind-held-out weakness, `z=+5.47`!):** **`0.822×`** (`-18%` under-represented!)
    - **`ob0` (`<= 5` pieces TB endgames):** **`0.562×`** (`-44%` under-represented!)
- **Server-Day Opening Mix Proposal (Highest-Impact Generation Change!):**
  Replace `100%` 8-uniform-random-ply openings with a **stratified 3-way opening curriculum** during Server-Day selfplay:
  - **50% Realistic Imbalanced Openings (`UHO / Pohl / diverse opening book + 2 random plies`):** Covers realistic castled king-shield structures, bishop-pair imbalances, and `±50..±180 cp` fighting positions (`0_99` and `100_249` bands) — directly attacking Forge's `2.45×` Moves 01–10 blunder rate and broken-shield blind spot!
  - **30% Controlled Random Openings (`6–8 random plies`, optionally filtered so opening exit `|score| <= 350 cp` or `random_multi_pv`):** Preserves robustness to weird/eccentric non-book positions without flooding the net with `+450 cp` blundered start positions.
  - **20% FRC / DFRC + Endgame-Seeded Starts (or reduced random plies):** Exposes king buckets (`0..7`) uniformly across unusual castling/king placements and boosts `ob0`/`ob1` endgame representation!

### 2.6 Weighting vs Hard Discarding & Informative-Position Selection (`EXP-W1..W4`)
- **Why Soft Weighting Beats Blind Discarding in Theory:**
  1. Preserves full support of the feature space (rare endgames, unusual king buckets, high-score anchors never collapse to zero count).
  2. Allows **dynamic curriculum / reweighting** without regenerating or re-filtering terabytes of data.
  3. **Power-Law Loss (`|pred - target|^p`, `p = 2.5–2.6`, Stockfish `nnue-pytorch` standard):** Automatically acts as *online informative-position selection* — positions where the net already agrees with the target (`|err| = 0.02`) get tiny gradient `2.5 * 0.02^1.5`, while hard/surprising positions (`|err| = 0.30`, e.g., broken king shields or bishop pairs!) receive **`~3.9×` larger relative gradient** than under standard `p = 2.0` MSE, WITHOUT permanently deleting a single position!
- **When Hard Discarding Beats Weighting:**
  - When **GPU VRAM or training wall-clock time is the bottleneck** (e.g., fitting `1.4–2.0B` positions into an H100/4090/7900XT training budget): dropping `11–15%` near-zero-gradient mop-ups (`|score| >= 800`) and `10–25%` exact cross-round duplicates frees **real VRAM and epoch wall-time** to train on **15–30% MORE unique contested positions per euro**!
- **Phase C tests BOTH head-to-head** (`EXP-W1/W2/W3` soft weighting vs `EXP-F1/F2` hard drop at equal raw AND equal retained budgets) on real `fastchess` matches!

## 3. Server-Day Parallel Streaming, Compression & Recovery Architecture (`1.4–2.0B` Positions)

| Stage | Current Live Pipeline | Proposed Server-Day Pipeline | Speedup / Savings |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Raw Game Archive** | `raw_selfplay.binpack` (`2.00 B/pos` -> **`4.0 GB`**) | Keep `.binpack` (`4.0 GB`!) — **already optimal for archival & transfer** | `1×` (keep!) |

| **Training-ready storage** | `filtered.plain` (`190 GB`) + `.cache.npz` (`135 GB`) = **`325 GB`** | Compressed `.fqs.z` 38-byte GPU shards (**`10.35 B/kept` verified -> `~20.7 GB` total!**) | **`15.7×` smaller disk/transfer** |
