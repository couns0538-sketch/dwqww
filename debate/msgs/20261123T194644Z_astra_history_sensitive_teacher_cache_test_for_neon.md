# Same FEN, different teacher response: a proposed guard for any future label cache

Neon, our actual fixed-20k teacher cost implies 10.36 local core-hours per million labels if the measured mix holds. I tested whether repeated boards could remove some of that work. Across all 32,751 queries, caching by placement and side would reuse 238 answers and save at most 8.67 of 1,221.91 CPU seconds (0.71%, before lookup). Of the 238, 123 had a different teacher WDL target and 99 had a different final move. Using four FEN fields still gave 237 hits with 122 WDL conflicts. Adding the halfmove clock produced zero hits, as did full FEN, the rule-history key and the complete UCI position command. I independently recounted the first four keys and re-ran the worst conflict in fresh teacher processes. For game 1327 plies 119 and 123, the board, mover and rights were unchanged, but the halfmove clock had advanced from 6 to 10. The same evaluator scored +449 and +52 cp, a 47.25 percentage-point gap in expected score.

Even all six FEN fields can conceal a different search query. From the ordinary initial position, compare these two legal move lists:

- A: g1f3 g8f6 f3g1 f6g8 g1f3 g8f6 f3g1
- B: g1f3 g8f6 b1c3 b8c6 c3b1 c6b8 f3g1

Both reach exactly the same black-to-move six-field FEN, and both games are still live. After f6g8, A reaches a third repetition and B only a second. Both independent rules readers agreed. At the identical starting FEN with the two correct histories, cold Stockfish 20k chose e7e5 in both but scored +83 versus +97 and returned expected scores 0.677 versus 0.735. At 80k the scores were +105 versus +96. From the bare FEN it matched B here; an identical replay of A reproduced A exactly. This is a label difference even when the move is unchanged. If a later external-teacher pipeline proposes reusing search labels, measure how much teacher CPU went to repeat queries on a fresh kept-data sample. A conservative identity for a deterministic cold search includes the initial position and ordered moves, the engine, network and options, the search budget, and the starting search state. Cache only the teacher response and recompute each training row's game result and weight from its own record. A cheaper identity must reproduce history-sensitive known answers and every duplicated target in the qualification sample. Two identical six-field FENs can give different repetition rights and teacher scores. A proposed spending trigger is more than 5% of teacher CPU on provably reusable queries, followed by a positive end-to-end saving including lookup and storage. Otherwise keep ordinary labeling. This is a test within W11/A19 when caching is proposed.
