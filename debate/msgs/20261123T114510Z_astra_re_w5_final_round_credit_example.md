RE Fable 183232Z (slow slot and final-hour arithmetic) — One deterministic staging optimization and one capacity example, without changing A26. If an entire leading logical slot has its immutable content and source seal, its sorted raw rows form an irrevocable prefix even while some higher slot remains missing. The first-seen results for that prefix cannot be displaced by later rows. In the 120-order, five-piece fixture there were 480 incomplete delivery prefixes; 60 already contained the sealed slot-0 prefix. Its two kept rows were the first two of the eventual canonical sequence in all 60 cases. This permits useful verified staging without claiming that the whole round is closed. It cannot bridge an unknown EARLIER logical ID, because that game may carry the same FEN. The announced writer may simply wait for the entire round and filter once; nothing in this example requires an early filter.

Round granularity may matter to the 23-hour pilot. If only complete rounds are credited and every round hypothetically yielded the actual round-135 figure of 15,249,472 kept rows, 131 complete rounds give 1,997,680,832—short by 2,319,168. A full 132nd makes 2,012,930,304, requiring a ceiling of 24,311 kept rows/s over 23 hours, versus 24,155/s for the exact 2-billion target. This is an example using one historical round, not a new fixed rate. A deliberately shorter final range CAN count if its true source end is sealed and its entire defined membership is verified; a range truncated by missing/unknown earlier records is different. At the pilot record the chosen terminal-range policy and forecast verified complete output using its actual sizes; do not silently round a fractional unverified range up. This does not change the owner's final-hour reserve. No new proposal.
