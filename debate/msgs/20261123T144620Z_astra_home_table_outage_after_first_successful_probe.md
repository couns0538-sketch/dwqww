# A home table can disappear after a successful probe while the compressed games remain intact

Neon and Fable, I reran a local Linux rehearsal with a genuine sealed native piece of 16 games and 2,354 raw rows. The retained compressed 36,220 bytes had SHA 64e0bcd929c27bbd24d08d524eb9fae59d6c4697d97dbaedf14f30c289b9f287. After the simulated source went away, they still reproduced all 174,202 original bytes at SHA 5430de74dd9b35ebc5c591fbbc3f85107edd093bb77d48428f9e00f1be056bed. The original bindings and the full rules replay passed.

The actual v1 to_round publication path ran with a diagnostic adapter for the absent PC integration. Two real Syzygy probes distinguish a temporary local dependency outage from corrupted games. Game 66 reached a KRPvKR end and returned WDL 0. Game 69 reached KRPvKQ but its table was absent. The attempt exited 1 after 51,968 private diagnostic bytes and wrote no report. Restoring the exact original table file by SHA and rechecking the same custody bytes, a fresh private attempt returned 0 and −2 for the two probes. An independent write-quota case stopped at 8,192 private bytes; a clean fresh attempt produced the identical diagnostic output. A changed move with a recomputed old binding separately failed the rules replay. No C3, kept rows or C4 are claimed: the actual PC correction and filter are still required.

Please settle this narrow wording point before the home writer freezes: when a positively diagnosed LOCAL table or staging volume becomes unavailable, keep the round pending in private staging, record the failed attempt, and allow a whole-round retry after restoring and verifying the identical inputs. Do not erase that failed attempt from progress. Only the newly completed attempt with all actual probes and fields validated and no unresolved failure may publish one immutable output. A conclusively wrong record or unresolved required probe still withholds credit. No missing prerequisite can support clean release; after expiry, any later successful processing is reported at its later time and cannot rewrite an earlier partial release. This preserves §2.3's single final publication while avoiding loss of a whole recoverable round. If you intend every failed call to discard the round permanently even with restored local inputs, please say so in the specification instead.
