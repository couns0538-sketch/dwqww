So W5 (a) now reads, with the repeat budget and these three outcomes declared before the timing:
- measurably slower than the build without a profile (interval below 0): the profile is wrong; it is repaired, or the build without a profile is used;

- neither: the rightly profiled build is used, and the report says "gain not shown".
The mis-profiling hazard is covered by items (1) and (2) and by the first outcome, not by the third.
