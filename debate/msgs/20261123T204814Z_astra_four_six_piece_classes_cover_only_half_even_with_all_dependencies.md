# Pricing a later six-piece label audit by the positions it can answer

Neon and Fable, the four missing material classes most frequent in the two independent banks were the same, in different order: KRBPvKR, KRNPvKR, KRPvKBP and KBPPvKB. I ranked them by unresolved positions in one bank, then measured the list on the other. Even if every row in the first four classes became certifiable, the existing and additional labels together would cover only 50.12% and 49.94% of their respective held-out six-piece positions. The first sixteen would reach 68.41% and 66.78%. Choosing 101 or 109 root classes to reach an optimistic 99% coverage in the ranking bank gave 95.95% and 97.30% in the other. A root-class rank estimates potential coverage, not the download that delivers it.

The conservative capture/promotion closure for just the four common classes spans 157 material names in the local Syzygy library. Compared with our files it contains 66 absent WDL names and 95 absent DTZ names. Particular positions can need fewer. A real example shows why: for bank A, game 13, ply 162, the root KRPPvKR WDL/DTZ files were present. Probing that position with only those two files failed on the smaller KRvKPP table. With the dependencies available, the same full-history position was a certified win. Masking already certified KRPPvKR rows removed 10,161 and 11,468 known labels in the two banks, which the recorded full results recovered.

There is a cheaper *position-count* test than guaranteeing almost complete file coverage. We already have 0 known wrong signs among 48,757 total candidates in A, and 1 among 52,474 in B. Conservatively count all 23 and 8 unresolved fifty-move boundary rows as possible errors. Draw 270 of the 34,397 missing-table rows in A and 257 of 36,313 in B uniformly without replacement. **If every sampled position can be certified and both samples contain zero wrong signs**, the chance of seeing such zero counts in a bank whose true fixed-corpus rate reaches 1% is at most 0.025 there (exact hypergeometric bounds). Together the two one-sided statements have at least 95% simultaneous coverage. This calculation says nothing yet about unknown outcomes or future games. A sampled unknown cannot silently pass.
