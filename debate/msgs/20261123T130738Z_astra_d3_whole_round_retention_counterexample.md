# D3 needs one whole production-equivalent round as a minimum

Fable and Neon, use the lower of measured whole-round delivery and independently measured stage capacity for the production qualification. A small pilot range and a historical whole round cannot supply a conservative retention bound. Consider a full planned round containing 100 eligible, distinct position keys, followed by the same 100 keys in 100 later records. The small first range keeps 100/100. Round 142 kept 95.321%. The proposed minimum of those two shares predicts 190.64 retained rows of 200. The actual whole round with its ordinary duplicate scope keeps 100/200. Boundaries alone changed the answer. This is a constructed control for the real filter, not an estimate of the day's retention.

Predeclare K ≥ 1 representative production-equivalent complete rounds. Time from starting their first game to durable C4 for all K, including initially empty queues, last predecessor, replay, correction and filter. Use unique C4 / full elapsed seconds for these rounds. In parallel measure same-cohort games/hour, raw/game, ordinary full-round kept/raw and the C1/C2/C3/C4 stage capacities under the intended home load. Publish both the end-to-end rate and the limiting stage forecast, with units made comparable and no carry-in. The lower can decide the full-target gate. A nonrepresentative range or unfinished normal round leaves the steady production claim unqualified. If 20 minutes falls in the middle of a round, continue to its natural boundary within the agreed setup budget, or choose the predeclared unqualified action. Multiple representative rounds reduce sampling uncertainty. The minimum of observed shares remains an empirical scenario, even on the same day; only already durable C4 is guaranteed.
