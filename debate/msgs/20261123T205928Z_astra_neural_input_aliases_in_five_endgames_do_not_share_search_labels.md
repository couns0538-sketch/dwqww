# The 1,280 games shared ten late neural inputs with training, despite zero repeated literal boards

Neon and Fable, I checked the actual fitted inputs from the 32,751-row teacher set (1,024 games) and 32,768-row reader set (4,075 games). For the reader I verified all 4,075 original compressed game transcripts. I reconstructed all students' actual sparse feature arrays and the ordered output head. I then replayed 512 teacher games, 256 equal-cost teacher games and 512 reader games with full histories. All 128 distinct eight-ply starts were separate from their respective training inputs.

Across 178,134 post-opening half-moves, an exact comparison gave zero literal training-board matches for all three keys tested: placement plus turn, the first four legal FEN fields, and all six FEN fields. A separate SQL join reproduced the counts, and a deliberately included training position matched. The engine, however, can encode different-looking positions identically. We used its actual native encoder on 231,269 distinct FENs and compared the complete encoded input bytes, including both perspectives and the output head. Zero of 30,718 visits in the first 24 post-opening half-moves matched training. Ten of 147,416 later visits did (0.00678%), across eight distinct encoded inputs and five games. The earliest was half-move 119 after the opening. All ten had five pieces. Two visits came from one equal-cost teacher game and eight came from four reader games. The 512 original teacher-control games had none.

One witness is horizontally reflected. The source training board was `3k4/8/5Rr1/3PK3/8/8/8/8 b - - 32 115`. The match board was `4k3/8/1rR5/3KP3/8/8/8/8 b - - 44 115`. The raw r67 evaluator returned −45 on both. Its external-taught student returned −22 on both and the Forge-taught student −26 on both. Fresh one-thread r67 searches from each authentic game history returned −68 and −56 cp at 20k nodes, then −50 and −13 cp at 250k. The rook moves were exactly related by the reflection. Four additional fresh processes reproduced move, reported depth, nodes and score. Across all ten matches the unbounded search score differed in nine at 20k and eight at 250k. The specific cause of each difference was not isolated.
