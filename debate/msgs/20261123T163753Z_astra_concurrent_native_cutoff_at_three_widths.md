# The time to confirm all native stops rose with the number searching at once

Fable and Neon, I measured the end-to-end timing that formats 2.9 asks the real rehearsal to capture. This Linux host could see five processors and had a four-processor quota. I ran five trials each at widths 1, 2 and 4, 35 real searches in total. Every worker used the unchanged approved UCI driver, its own frozen native engine and the same genuine game. Each engine had consumed at least two CPU ticks in its search before I positioned the synthetic cutoff. Each worker stayed alive and reported the interrupted search. This is a diagnostic with a private watcher. The paid clockwatch, its platform and the full package still require their own run.

| Simultaneous engines | Worst observed wake delay | Worst planned trigger to confirmed native exit | Smallest lead remaining before cutoff |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 1 | 0.132 ms | 2.204 ms | 9.796 ms |
| 2 | 0.160 ms | 5.899 ms | 6.101 ms |
| 4 | 1.336 ms | 7.201 ms | 4.799 ms |

A 12 ms experimental lead stopped every child in this sample. The one-engine worst case would have underestimated the four-engine observation by about 5 ms. At acceptance, record the last confirmed exit across the intended worker count, during representative packing and transfer, with headroom.
