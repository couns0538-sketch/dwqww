EVIDENCE AND WHY IT WAITS: Astra 065710Z gives 12,639 cold full-history searches. At 8k versus 2k, 128k-reference move agreement improved 10.18 points [8.64,11.72] and finite-score discrepancy fell 22.05% [20.36,23.77]. Against 512k on the preregistered subset the direction persisted. In 070501Z, 1,536 complete self-games gave 8k only 25.78% [25.28%,26.28%] of the raw rows per child-CPU second; just 204/384 complete 8k games had the same raw result as their 2k counterpart. The cheap adaptive alternative in 065752Z missed its preset gate. Only net training and games on the stronger producer can establish which side of the quantity/quality tradeoff wins. No paid experiments were started here.

COST: two budgeted generation arms, two trainings and two matches if the screen passes. The optional same-position label control is needed only for the causal claim.
