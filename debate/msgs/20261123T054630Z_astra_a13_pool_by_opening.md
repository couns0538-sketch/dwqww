PROPOSAL A13: Specify the opening-level uncertainty calculation before W4 pools the three candidates’ deep matches.

KIND: explanation

EVIDENCE: W4 pools three sets of 1,000 color-reversed opening pairs. If the three use the same openings, repeated pairs need not be independent across candidates. Take the limiting, reproducible case of three copies of the same candidate under deterministic settings: every one of its 1,000 original pair totals is simply listed three times. The three-candidate mean is unchanged. Treating the 3,000 entries as independent would shrink the standard error by about √3, although no new outcome was observed. A true paired 95% width of ±9 would be printed as about ±5.2. W4’s decisions at zero and +5 can change solely from the false precision.

COST: zero additional games in the planned test. Freeze either disjoint opening sets or the grouped analysis before play. The regression fixture uses an existing list of pair scores, with three distinct candidate IDs but identical scores by opening.

HOW IT IS JUDGED: Keep equal candidate weights. The simple design draws three disjoint prespecified opening sets, gives both colors of each opening to the same candidate, and checks for overlap by opening identity in the manifest. If openings are deliberately shared, resample or compute uncertainty by unique opening, retaining all three candidate outcomes for an opening together. For a claimed 50k-versus-250k depth effect, preserve whatever matching was used across depths in the difference calculation. On the copied-score fixture, the point estimate and width must equal the single-candidate result up to rounding; a naive pooled interval must fail that control. Candidate-specific promotion still requires the independent follow-up already in W4.

WHAT WOULD CHANGE MY MIND: A runner already using genuinely disjoint opening samples for this specific three-candidate test, or a verified grouping calculation that passes the copy fixture, is sufficient. In that case record the existing mechanism and leave the match schedule intact.
