PROPOSAL A21: For later, distinguish a win by one frozen net from evidence that a changed training recipe warrants repeated use across independently initialized or shuffled trainings.
KIND: source evidence, paired-match variance and a prospective test.
WHEN: W8 is qualified, the target store is frozen, an eligible ingredient has passed the existing R6 test on that scale, and the owner is about to use it for several new trainings or retire the rival recipe. Trigger a replication only if a 5-Elo reversal in another seed would alter this decision and the expected expenditure saved warrants a new matched pair. An exact hashed champion remains subject to its existing promotion rules.
WHAT: First look for an existing pair with the same frozen training rows, parent/init specification, trainer version, optimizer/steps/recipe except the treatment, and a different recorded seed. Otherwise name one held-out training seed before running either arm; pair treatment/control within that seed and retain separate estimates for both training pairs. Replaying the same two nets on new openings measures match uncertainty but cannot measure training-to-training variation. A seed chosen after viewing many results is discovery only.
EVIDENCE: STATE.md sections 1/3/4/5 describe changing rolling windows, a width comparison, a breadth mixture, and a TD twin changing labels, not a replicated seed-only contrast. I found no such controlled repeat in the published artifacts; the PC's private records may contain one. The three capped deep match tables in debate/evidence/fable_f2_deep_pair_tables.txt sum to [55,713,1399,760,73]. Pair results 0,.25,.5,.75,1 give variance 0.04130633; at 2000 pairs near score .5, a single contrast has SE 3.16 Elo, approximate 95% half-width 6.19. Two separately opened equally sized contrasts have SE about 4.47 for their difference. Even two truly independent known positive training draws yield a one-sided 95% exact lower bound of only sqrt(.05)=.224 on the population chance of a positive effect. The actual Forge training-seed variance is unknown.
TEST/BAR: At the decision's row scale and matched compute, train the new pair with the identical recipe difference. Play each seed's treatment vs that seed's own control on a predeclared, independently held-out 4000-game/250k-node paired schedule (or R3 if speeds differ). Retain both confidence intervals and all attempts. A held-out upper bound below zero is adverse replication: keep any properly promoted specific net, but do not deploy the recipe broadly on an alleged repeatable gain. A held-out lower bound above zero supports the recipe under both tested seeds; an interval spanning zero leaves cross-seed repeatability unresolved. Do not force two significant results as a universal net promotion gate or claim a general population probability from two runs.
COST/WHY IT WAITS: One additional matched treatment/control pair only when the result genuinely changes repeated future spending, plus one 4000-game check on the new pair. At the reported 2917 seconds per 388M training this is about 1.62 PC card-hours plus 1.18 match-slot hours at 3400 games/hour. Two 2B trainings must be priced from the actual pilot; today's small-window price is not evidence of their cost. A reported original seed estimate can stand without playing it again if it was predeclared and valid.
WHAT WOULD CHANGE MY MIND: If there is an adequately controlled archived independent training repeat at the decision's scale, use it; if the decision concerns only a particular hashed net, or the repeat cannot change a decision, skip it.
