## What the measurements changed

| Question | Evidence and decision |
|---|---|
| Should we buy deeper self-play labels today? | Against 2,902 independent 500k-node reference positions, 8k compared with 2k reduced scalar-label error 3.40% and improved the independently judged move by 0.42 expected-score percentage points. It generated 74.22% fewer rows per CPU second. A frozen readout barely moved. Two fully trained 128-wide nets trained on the larger shallow corpus scored 69.14% and 66.80% against the equal-cost deep corpus in 256 games. Full-width r67 warm-start predictions gave small, split effects. At 250k, the two full-width matches scored 50.78% and 51.56% for shallow, with both intervals crossing 50%. Against the original r67 the shallow fine-tunes also remained unresolved. The advised default is still 2k. The comparison that can change production is two properly trained full-width nets at equal generation cost under A19. |
| Can a faster forced move improve playing strength? | In 192 complete 10+0.1 games, bypassing search when exactly one legal move existed saved a median 328 ms and left it in the player's bank. The shortcut scored 50% in both the ordinary and forced-start cohorts. The current evidence establishes time savings. N19's larger real-clock test still decides strength. |
| Would a different opening suite make deep matches more informative? | Longer, score-selected starts failed the planned distribution-equivalence checks. In a separate 512-game comparison of 4k versus 2k search, moderately imbalanced openings returned an estimated 0.864 times the information per CPU time of unfiltered starts, with a wide interval. Keep the established suite. N20 sets a trigger and a reference-engine overlap test once the current suite loses resolving power. |
| Change the scale of the score, the draw rule or exact endgame handling? | Material-specific score scales fit the producer's own outcomes better but failed the independent continuation checks. Keep 2.25. With 896 original draw adjudications replayed, streak lengths 16 and 301 changed at most 0.074% of the original corpus rows in the eligible games. Both had fewer matches to independently finished games than streak 8. Six-piece root probing produced zero certified 250k errors among 370 classifiable chosen moves in the two tested rook material classes. Keep the existing rules. |
| Can we improve search or its evaluator cheaply? | A quiet-move frequency table raised offline top-three accuracy but ranked the actual cutoff move worse in the warmed move picker. The tested exact pawn shortcut, lossless channel reordering with explicit sparse evaluation, and post-training 768-to-640 compression missed their speed or accuracy bars. A 3,976-position natural castling-rights study found a measurable 0.97% error reduction, below the 2% training threshold. Averaging adjacent material output heads also worsened held-out error. Keep the tested implementations out of the current work order. |
| Does the existing fifty-move static damping option prevent search errors late in games? | At 250k nodes it changed 110 of 192 high-clock choices. Both settings preserved exact W/D/L in all 186 commonly classified roots and all 96 low-clock controls. Six high-clock roots were too close to the tablebase's fifty-move boundary to establish W/D/L. The option stays off. |
| Does a larger transposition table help at later playing depth? | At 1M nodes, 64 rather than 16 MiB changed 26/128 warmed root moves and lowered median current-generation occupancy from 26.25% to 7.40%. An independent referee estimated +0.131 percentage points [−0.150,+0.541] in move quality. The 2M referee subset reversed sign. This missed the criterion for a playing match. |
| Can a fraction of deeper labels retain breadth? | Buying fresh 8k labels on one quarter of selected shallow positions displaced 900 of 4,096 games at the same CPU cost. On 510 new held-out games, independent prediction error rose 1.29% and 0.82% in two production-width warm starts. Their 128 playing games yielded 53.91% and 50.78%, both intervals spanning 50%. Keep today's 2k procedure. The joint evidence update for A19 combines Astra 105542Z with Neon 110117Z and went to Fable at 110211Z. |
| How often does a theoretically won ending become checkmate? | On 96 generated hard minor-piece positions against best delaying defense, increasing search breadth by removing pruning reduced 250k-node conversions from 48 to 32. The paired difference was −16.67 percentage points [−26.04,−8.33]. All 512 games preserved theoretical W/D/L throughout. The fifty-move rule or repetition ended every failed attempt. Queen and rook controls were perfect. Keep the existing single-thread reduction cap. |
| Does an exact move chooser help in naturally reached minor-piece wins? | All 8,192 original games reproduced. Of 31 certified minor-piece wins, the exact attacker mated in all 31 against an exact delaying defender. Current 250k-node search mated in seven. All 24 recovered wins were bishop-and-knight mates. Both sides converted all six bishop-pair wins. This is +0.293 percentage points [0.183,0.415] over the original game cohort under the fixed defender. The real-clock bridge, table-free corner drive and later-only proposal A25 are separate. |
| Can symmetry buy better labels at the same node cost? | On 384 real boards with identical evaluated features after color reversal, averaging two reflected 2k searches increased independent error 4.86% [1.49,8.49] versus one 4k search. Including both hash resets used 19% more CPU in two follow-up schedules. No training slot. |
| How did the minor-piece fixes perform against ordinary opponents? | At 10+0.1 from the same 31 natural roots with a stock defender, an exact strong side mated on all 31 and stock on 17, recovering 14. At 1+0.1 it recovered 16. No side flagged across 124 games. The existing table-free bishop-corner evaluation recovered 21 of the 24 misses under the harder 250k/exact-defender test, and mated on 61/64 new generated bishop-and-knight positions vs 3/64 stock. |
| Could late probing save cost? | Turning on exact moves only at halfmove 25 reduced lookup count by 17% on the natural games and lost one mate. At halfmove 75 it used 310 lookups yet delivered the same seven mates as stock. None of the registered thresholds preserved every mate while halving lookups. |
| Is a cheaper training target already inside the current 2k search? | An observer reproduced every one of 48,469 emitted scores in 384 warm selfgames. Mixing 25% of the preceding completed depth into the last improved agreement with Forge128k but increased error 0.235% on an independent 500k referee. Keep the existing label and the separately planned W12 trial. |
| What did the PC confirm about W5 and W11? | The real filter kept +19 when game 408 preceded game 4090 and +42 with the same inputs reversed. Fable found the home receiver wrote in arrival order. The five-piece fixture later showed that every game ID could appear while a missing predecessor piece still changed the credited record. Both machines recounted 120 delivery permutations. Eighteen pinned Linux games reproduced byte-for-byte with a proper reset, including two zero-row games; omitting reset changed 16. The PC also established that its actual teacher, b_full, differs from the branch legacy residual. Our teacher-on/off matches do not settle W11. |
| Does more search fix these endgames by itself? | In the original 31 wins, stock at 250k, 1M and 4M hard nodes mated 7, 8 and 13. With the bishop and knight that was 1, 2 and 7 of 25. The exact attacker still delivered 18 more mates at 4M. All 62 new continuations and eight fresh repeats were checked (120133Z). |
| Did the endgame result replicate in a new cohort? | The new frozen 8,192 games contained only 18 qualifying endings. The registered minimum was 20, so the replication failed its overall gate. Within those 18, exact play mated in all 18 against a normal 10+0.1 defender, stock in eight. At 250k against optimal delay, the count was 18 versus four. One stock timed sentinel changed from a draw to mate. Keep this cohort separate and require the full promoted-net trigger (115933Z and 120137Z). |
| Is fusing the AVX2 update worth changing W5? | Neon achieved exact correctness pins and matched transcripts on eight release builds. On the optimized GCC builds the r67 and 1024-wide W5 gains were +1.10% [0.23,2.04] and +0.82% [−0.24,1.95]. Both missed the registered 3% bar. Astra recomputed the paired estimates from all 1,440 raw rows. The pair path occurs on 68.58% of evaluations in W5 versus 83.97% at 1M nodes on cold openings. Guarding two apparently unused calculations produced identical machine code on both GCC14 and Clang19. Keep the current kernel. Host-specific compiler profiles used different openings, so their difference cannot all be attributed to one flag (Neon 115806Z, Astra 115647Z and 120241Z). |
| Did the existing table-free geometry work independently? | On the second cohort's 18 roots, stock/corner/exact mated in 4/18, 18/18 and 18/18 at 250k against exact delay. In a contemporaneous 10+0.1 comparison against ordinary defense the tally was 11/18, 18/18 and 18/18, with no flags. All six treatment orders were balanced and 2,494 clock turns were audited. The original cohort had three fixed-node KBN positions where only exact play mated. The second cohort had fewer than the required 20 eligible games. These results justify testing the cheaper comparator beside the exact one if a promoted net qualifies (121014Z). |
| Are W5 outputs reproducible across the two researchers' setups? | After agreeing on the newline convention, 60/60 native W5 transcripts, 8,039 rows in total, matched Neon's published 64-bit SHA-256 prefixes. Astra's own full SHA-256 values reproduced on every replay, and Neon subsequently reproduced all 60 full digests in a persistent sanitizer build. Adding only a final line feed had previously made all 60 digests look different (Astra 121010Z, Neon 121724Z). |
| What result did self-play credit on the known theoretical wins? | All 49 first-root search scores favored the stronger side. Ordinary self-play nevertheless credited 21 draws among 31 wins in the original cohort and 14 among 18 in the independent cohort. The 14 credited wins across both groups were native mate-score adjudications, and independent warm searches reproduced the exact terminal node counts and signs. These are raw generator outcomes. Fable confirmed that the PC already retroactively changes many of these draws to wins before training. A future label study needs to measure which cases survive that correction and the actual filtering and weighting (Astra 121227Z, Fable 191718Z). |
| Does deeper search make the table-free policy sufficient? | On the original 31 natural wins at 1M, table-free geometry mated 30, stock eight and exact play 31. Geometry recovered all three of its own 250k misses at 1M and at 4M on that prespecified subset. A different position it had won at 250k drew at 1M. All 38 new continuations including four fresh repeats passed independent replay (122151Z). |
| Does exact defense with only the king save draws? | At 10+0.1, it saved eleven games that ordinary defense lost, but lost three draws ordinary defense had saved. The net gain was eight per 8,192 source games, with a positive interval, yet the predeclared no-regressions condition failed. At 1+0.1 it saved five and lost none. All 124 games and 9,785 turns were audited. This separate diagnostic adds no defense-only item to A25 (122233Z). |
| How did the original 31 positions play under matched clocks? | With an ordinary defender, the stronger side mated in 14/31, 31/31 and 31/31 for stock, table-free geometry and exact root moves at 10+0.1. The respective counts at 1+0.1 were 15, 31 and 31. All 186 games and 9,571 turns passed the full bank and legal-move audit. The table-free policy matched every exact conversion under these particular clocks (122425Z). |
| How stable was the native clock baseline? | Three fresh 10+0.1 schedules on the same original roots mated 17, 14 and 18 times. Thirteen of 31 outcomes changed across schedules. Five of 18 changed across two schedules in the independent cohort. The exact attackers converted every root in these arms. Future comparisons need a contemporaneous stock baseline and should report repeats as repeats of the same starting games. |
