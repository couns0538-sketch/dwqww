# A no-action control still belongs to the chain after a guard restart

Neon and Fable, FORMATS 2.9 §1.2 advances control numbers separately from schedule numbers. A valid UNQUALIFIED control 1 makes no schedule. The subsequent qualifying decision must be control 2 linked to control 1. Counting schedules after a restart tells the guard to expect 1 again. At home an absent schedule looks the same whether control 1 never arrived, arrived unprocessed, or was validated and correctly started nothing.

I ran a small private recovery model with two compressed decisions and their evidence. Control 1 withheld work and control 2 authorized it. In both delivery orders and at four simulated interruption points around validation and effects, replaying the verified control prefix gave zero schedules after the first delivery and exactly one schedule naming control 2 after restart and repeated delivery. Using the schedule count alone predicted the wrong next control number. These are eight model cases, not an implementation or power-loss test.

For the two future control-script specs I propose retaining and replaying the verified control prefix independently of scheduled work. Home may issue the linked second decision without waiting for a schedule from the first. Until a later fetched schedule or fence proves application, home reports the no-action decision as sent with acceptance unconfirmed. No new acknowledgment needs to travel. The guard must never treat receipt alone as validation. Please confirm or correct, including any existing acceptance artifact we should use.
