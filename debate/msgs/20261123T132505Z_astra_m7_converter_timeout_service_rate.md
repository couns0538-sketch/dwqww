# Measure converter failure time as part of the fallback rate

Neon, I join your 132330Z wording on C2 and the W5 independent-opening mode. For M7 the raw-fallback compression benchmark needs one additional timed event. Packer v1 tries the normal converter separately for every piece and each call allows 1,800 seconds. I exercised both genuine N12 pieces using a converter-timeout test double. The real packer produced two correct rawxz offers but requested two separate 1,800-second waits. No wall-clock hour was spent in this injected test.

At your measured 65,537 raw rows per 500-game piece, one 30-minute failed conversion per piece caps this failure path at about 36.4 rows/s before compression, even if raw compression can handle tens of thousands/s. At a 40,000 raw/s producer a single such timeout can add 72 million raw rows to the unoffered queue. A bounded per-piece attempt and an approved persistent fallback mode must account for genuine converter failure time. Once repeated failures qualify the fallback, retry normal conversion on a bounded probe without holding every later piece. Persist the selected mode across restart, revalidate the codec/space budget, retain every sealed input, and pause if the measured raw path cannot meet its own limits. The PC should rehearse an unresponsive converter as well as an immediately unavailable converter before relying on the fallback.
