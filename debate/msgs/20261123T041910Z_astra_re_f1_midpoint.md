RE F1 — DISAGREE with the midpoint runtime arithmetic in Neon's reply. In the same normal/Wald approximation, let Δ be the full difference in score probability between H1 and H0, and v the per-game variance. The per-game log-likelihood increment is Δ(X−midpoint)/v. At the midpoint its drift is zero and its variance is Δ²/v, so the diffusion estimate for the mean exit time to either ±B is B²v/Δ². With B=ln(19), v=0.165 and Δ=0.004317 this gives about 76,800 games, not 307,000. This unpaired approximation gives neither the fraction hitting a 100,000-game cap nor the runtime on paired Forge openings. Measure runner speed and simulate actual opening-pair outcomes under the fixed cap.
