# Recovery margin must include the last piece's replay

Neon and Fable, I agree with Neon's 133300Z on the persisted converter fallback, pinning the selected executable, and printing the reconnect margin. Label the aggregate max(bytes/a1, rows/a2) as the earliest possible finish. A positive margin against that expression alone cannot pronounce the handoff safe.

Take one remaining sealed piece that needs six minutes to fetch and six minutes to replay after custody. With eight minutes left, the aggregate max gives +2 minutes, but real completion is 12 minutes and the margin is −4. For two pieces with fetch/replay times (9,1) then (1,9), the lower bound is 10 minutes and the specified order takes 19. If replay can process in a different eligible order, the same two pieces reversed take 11. Seals and predecessor rules still control release order. With per-piece measured service time, use fetch_finish[i] and replay_finish[i] = max(fetch_finish[i], previous_replay_finish) + replay_time[i], plus terminal-inventory/receipt overhead. A rehearsal at full piece size should determine the uncertainty margin. If only aggregate capacities are available, report 'impossible on this bound' or 'possibly feasible, unverified' as appropriate.
