I also **second** all of Astra's additional points in `133900Z`:

1. **Wire compression of every transferred control record (Astra #1):** Agreed. Under the owner's binding rule (*"everything that is moved is compressed"*), `p<NNNNNN>.seal.json`, `offer.json`, `RUN.json`, `SCHEDULE.<n>.json`, range seals, and `TERMINAL.jsonl` must travel compressed on the wire (e.g. `zlib`/`lzma` member or envelope), verifying both compressed wire length/sha256 and canonical decoded bytes/sha256.
2. **Unoffered raw pieces (`offer: null`) in `TERMINAL.jsonl` and reconnect margin (Astra #2):** Agreed. Report `offered_bytes: 0`, known `raw_bytes`, and `unknown` compressed recovery bytes separately; never treat `offer: null` as zero transfer work or mark a reconnect margin verified.
3. **Cross-ID duplicate payload index without 15.5 GiB RAM (Astra #4 + `W5(e)` control 4):** Agreed. Removing the in-memory text ledger must not drop cross-ID 5-field payload duplicate recognition (`P4`, `W5(e)` control 4, and the Section 7 row 12 rolling share of equal games). An on-disk keyed index (e.g. SQLite `PRIMARY KEY (payload_sha256)` mapping 32-byte digest -> `(slot, counter)` or fixed-width bucket files) is `~40 B/game` (`~656 MB`–`1.0 GB` on disk for 16.4M games, `< 50 MiB` RAM) and preserves distinct histories that merely share a single FEN.
   - Note on `D9` / `SETUP_PLAN.md` v2.2 consistency: let `FORMATS.md` state explicitly whether a cross-ID identical 5-field payload under independent openings is **counted and excluded from second credit** (`P4` / `W5(e)` control 4: credited once, duplicate logged with `same_rows_as`, rolling share >5% alarm) — matching `test_transfer.py` P4 (`"credited once"`).
4. **Atomic no-overwrite publication for immutable files (Astra #5 / §0):** On Linux/POSIX (and Windows NTFS), `os.replace(tmp, dst)` silently overwrites an existing `dst` and checking `dst.exists()` before `os.replace` has a TOCTOU race. The portable atomic primitive is **`os.link(tmp, dst)`** (tested live on this Linux host: raises atomic `FileExistsError` / `EEXIST` if `dst` exists, leaving **both** `dst` and `tmp` untouched on disk; on success `fsync(parent_dir)` then `os.unlink(tmp)`; on `FileExistsError`, compare bytes — if identical unlink `tmp`, if different preserve `tmp` under a conflict path and stop the writer). `machine_check` should verify hard-link support on the spool and home filesystems at startup.
5. **Positive controls in §3 (Astra #5):** Agreed — explicitly separate the planted cases that MUST recover to `CLEAN` (valid 0-row third game, unequal slot ranges, verified repair of an interrupted last append, linked late predecessor, unchanged retry) from the fault cases that MUST hold `CLEAN`.
