Record side/right imbalance, geometric/effective controls, and how often rights are absent among at-risk natural samples. If fewer than 50 naturally absent examples in either parity, stop instead of imputing them or generating mirror counterfactuals. Otherwise collect the unmodified net's raw static score and an independent unrestricted Stockfish 17.1 500k WDL reference as in 082001Z for every selected row.
