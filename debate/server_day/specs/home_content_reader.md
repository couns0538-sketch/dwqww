# Home content reader, stage a2

**Duty.** Turn a piece in home custody into an immutable content verdict and entries in the on-disk equal-games index. Run this step alongside fetch and stage b. Only accepted records contribute their raw rows to C2. Stage a1 already writes receipts. This reader starts at those receipts.

**Inputs and outputs.** Use the pinned run header, opening rule, actual game command, scheduled ranges, eligible chain of seals, receipt, and the kept wire bytes. Reopen and hash those bytes. Rebuild the exact sealed plain piece from raw fallback or from binpack plus compressed metadata. Check length, raw hash, every binding, game and row counts, first/last game and counters, and ordered ID digest. A byte, binding or seal mismatch refuses the whole piece before any rules verdict. For structurally sound records, independently derive the opening from run seed and reserved ID, compare the exact sent position, and replay the game under the header's settings. Verify canonical move spelling, position and ply of every row, terminal reason, counts and final board. Scores are sealed data. Search replay is reserved for the first-hour fixed games, disputes and spot checks. Publish the per-game verdict and reasons under the piece key. Preserve overlapping-ID evidence with both bindings for A26.

**Restart and limits.** Commit the equal-games index before publishing the verdict. Key equality by the complete ordered five-field rows and result. Distinct IDs with that digest remain candidates for the first eligible game in round, slot and counter order. A zero-row game has no rows digest. Reprocessing the same piece after a crash produces the same verdict and adds no second entry. Keep the index on disk. Set a room bound on the physical volume including its temporary and journal growth.

**Known answers to check before implementation.**

- The published 86, 101, 0, 127, 81 and 67-row games total 462 rows. The genuine zero-row game is accepted on its full end line and contributes zero rows. A stopped opening with no legal move is a separate disposition; a stopped opening with a legal move left is set aside.
- In the agreed three-game fixture with 345 rows, the middle record has a valid binding but its sent position omits an opening move. That game is set aside. The other games add 198 to C2. Change the sent line without updating its binding and the entire piece adds zero. Change a sealed score from +19 to +42 and leave the old seal, also zero.
- With an eight-ply opening and three moves played, a draw by the score-run rule has four searches and four rows. The fourth proposed move is checked for legality and never applied. A mate-score cutoff at the same move count has four searches and three rows. Reject an extra row, a castling alias, or a counted third repetition that the replay cannot reach. Also run all seven required end reasons, stalemate and the three rule draws.
- Cut between committing the index and publishing the verdict. After two restarts the one verdict and the index contain the same logical games as an uninterrupted pass. A second game that shares one position but has different rows stays eligible.
