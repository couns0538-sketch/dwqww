# Home round correction, filtering and publication, stage b

**Duty.** Convert a complete, eligible round into the owner's packed training rows. Inputs are the frozen schedule, all range seals, custody bytes and content verdicts, disk index, frozen PC correction and filter versions, configuration, net and full tablebase manifest. Reread and hash the inputs. Process records in slot/counter order after eligibility of earlier rounds is final. Correct outcomes with the PC's own function before filtering. Count games examined and outcomes changed by reason separately, including required probes made or failed. The final compressed kept object and report are staged privately on the destination volume. Publish their directory once onto its free round name. The report carries the hash of each input, counts, deficits, repeats, and output hash. Only a complete durable round adds C4.

**Failures and restart.** The equal-games representative is the earliest eligible ID in frozen order. For competing complete records under one ID, preserve the immutable dispute with both pieces and binding values, then apply A26 using an independent replay from the reservation. Only the lower-numbered piece can qualify, and only when its binding and independent replay match. Keep a round waiting while a named piece or range seal remains in transit. A partial publication requires the guard's immutable drain record. A diagnosed local outage leaves the round pending, logs each failed attempt permanently, and retries the whole round only after identical hashed prerequisites return. A conclusive deficit withholds C4. A later correction or receipt cannot overwrite a prior release or change its time. Bound the temporary round, output and previous durable data on their common physical disk before writing.

**Known answers to check before implementation.**

- Give a complete round four eligible distinct input rows. Arrange for the frozen correction function to change one game's outcome and the frozen filter to remove exactly one row. Four examined rows, one changed game, one removed row, three durable kept rows. A repeated receipt and a second run leave C4 at three. Use a fixture with an independently verified output digest.
- Two one-row games carry the same ordered row with +19 and a third game differs only by its +42 score. The later +19 is a repeat. The +42 survives. With a fixture filter keeping both, forward and reverse arrival each credit two rows. The remaining candidate takes the place of an earlier ineligible representative only after A26 is resolved.
- Deliver two required pieces and the inventory in all six orders. If all inputs pass, each order yields one identical complete round. An inventory arriving first leaves the round pending. With a permanently missing required ID and a valid drain, a partial round credits zero, even if 99 other rows passed C2.
- Use the agreed queen ending with an intact root table. Remove each named required child table in turn after a successful preflight. The probe fails and the round stays pending. Restore both original hashes and retry from the beginning. Its WDL is draw, one round publishes, the failed-attempt count survives, and the final successful attempt has zero failed probes. Apply the same restart test to a full staging volume. Zero changed outcomes in 10,000 examined games raises the agreed alarm; it never invents a correction.
