# Home guard and hourly progress

**Duty.** Keep the approved fetch, custody, content and round programs working independently, with bounded durable queues and one short consistent snapshot for every hourly line. Write progress at home even when the server is unreachable. Track jobs stopped, retries, faults, last contact, stage-b attempts and alarms. On home restart, bind each durable receipt, verdict, round output, failure and partial transfer to the current run before reuse. An explicit confirmation requires a verified run header and an affirmative result for the requested piece. An old receipt under the same slot and index cannot confirm a new run. Respect the owner's Windows launch and sleep choices. If no one has logged in or started the guard after a reboot, measure and report the entire gap. Existing sealed bytes stay on the server during the lease.

**Progress contract.** For C0 report the latest observed server state with its time and “unknown beyond”. Report RECEIVED separately from chain-eligible C1. C2 is accepted raw rows. C3 separates examined games/rows, outcomes actually corrected by reason and failed probes. C4 is durable, packed kept rows of complete rounds. For each of the three transitions name unresolved object identities, amounts in that transition's input units and the oldest outstanding identity and time. Keep intended filter removals, duplicates excluded and permanent deficits out of the queue. Report trailing-hour and cumulative rates, needed rate, room by physical volume, stage failures, equal games, D4 row and measured reconnect margin. Close the snapshot before network work.

**Stop and recovery.** Fetch, replay and publishing can advance independently until each stage's own measured space limit binds. Publish a create-only DRAIN record only under the agreed authority and source/end conditions, with known missing wire members and sizes. Final inventory arrival alone never causes drain. Do not interrupt a healthy transfer or in-custody check to declare a range partial. The independent expiry reporter can record a partial as-of result while the guard continues draining.

**Known answers to check before implementation.**

- The PC round with 16,007,134 raw and 15,258,168 kept has 748,966 removed rows after completion. Stage-b backlog is exactly zero, oldest absent. A conclusively set-aside game remains a deficit rather than waiting forever.
- With a2 held for ten minutes, two 100-row pieces reach custody. C1 can rise 200 while C2 stays fixed and the content queue contains the two named pieces and 200 raw rows. Restart the guard. The same two entries remain; once both pass, the queue drains to zero. No receipt is counted twice.
- Pin two snapshots around a commit adding 100 verified rows. Each individual progress line reads entirely before or entirely after the commit. The service cannot combine old C1 with new C2. On disconnect last-seen C0 does not grow from an extrapolated rate.
- Two pending pieces take 4 s each to fetch and 3 s each to check. With one fetcher and one checker, earliest completions are 7 s then 11 s. Aggregate lower bound is 8 s. With 10 s left and an already-local inventory, the lower bound alone is inconclusive while the per-piece calculation misses by 1 s. Add measured inventory time if it is still outstanding.
