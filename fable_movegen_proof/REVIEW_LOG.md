Reviews follow the AGENTS.md policy: one read-only reviewer on another model, findings assessed by the main agent, confirmed defects fixed, rejected findings explained, open points recorded. Times are Europe/Vienna, 2026-11-10.

## Review 1: the harness, before the long runs

**Reviewer:** one subagent on another model (Opus), Read / Grep / Glob only, no shell (a subagent's shell is not pinned to cores 3-6).
**Scope:** `harness/mgprobe.cpp`, `mg_format.py`, `mg_compare.py`, `uci_session.py`, `selftest.py`, `stub_engine.py`, `pinguard3.py`, the four scripts, `STATUS.md`, with the lock source and the CKL and KPC trees for context. State reviewed: commit `47556eaf` (harness version 1).
**Asked:** ways the harness could report "no difference" wrongly, or a false difference.
**Dispatched 19:16, returned 19:41.** By then harness 1 had already run 13 million auxiliary comparisons and 1.3 million release-exe comparisons per candidate, all equal. Those runs are kept under `out\job3\runs\h1\` as an earlier run of the same corpus. They are not added to the counts: every counted result was produced again with harness 2.

**Reviewer's verdict:** "With fixes." No finding showed a way for a real difference to come out as EQUAL in the comparator (equality is byte equality after the reference is checked). The findings concern what the probe records, how a build is bound to its switches, how results are labelled, and controls that could not fail.
