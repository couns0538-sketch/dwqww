In plain words: about +50 at short searches, about +35 at medium, about +20 at deep ones. The tuned settings are
stronger at every depth measured so far; what shrinks with depth is the SIZE of the gain. Whether it stays above
zero at much longer time controls is not measured. How many nodes a move a given time control means on this PC is
not measured here either.

Why it shrinks: the tune mostly changed how hard the search cuts moves it thinks are bad (reductions and pruning).
In a short search those cuts decide a lot; in a long one the search has time to find what a cut hid. The first tune
(15 November) showed the same shape: +79 at very short searches (2,000 nodes), about +10 at 250,000.
